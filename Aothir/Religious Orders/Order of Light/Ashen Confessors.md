# About
The militant enforcers of spiritual and moral purity within the [[Order of Light]], tasked with rooting out heresy, corruption, and apostasy. Blending unwavering faith with relentless martial discipline, they wield both divine authority and steel to ensure the sanctity of the faithful. Whether through confession, inquisition, or battlefield purges, they operate in the shadow cast by the light of the order, ensuring no heretic escapes judgment.
## Responsibilities
- **Confession & Redemption** – Administering confessions to clergy and laity, offering penance or judgment to those who falter.  
- **Inquisition & Purification** – Investigating and interrogating suspected heretics, rooting out corruption before it spreads.  
- **Trials & Judgments** – Overseeing ecclesiastical trials, delivering verdicts, and enacting sentences ranging from penance to execution.  
- **Internal Discipline** – Keeping the clergy and faithful on the righteous path, ensuring no darkness festers within the Order itself.  
- **Banishers of the Abyss** – Studying forbidden knowledge to understand and combat heretical practices while resisting their temptations.
## Structure & Function
The Confessors are a small yet highly specialised force, relying on fear, precision, and reputation rather than overwhelming numbers. With just over 400 members across the continents, they are a mythic presence rather than a widespread force, known more for their terrifying efficiency and efficacy than their numbers.
- **Martial Hierarchy:** The chain of command is rigid, with each rank answering to those above. Promotions come through piety, effectiveness, and battlefield success.
- **Sacred Warfare:** The Ashen Confessors maintain separate training halls where they refine interrogation techniques, swordsmanship, and the wielding of divine magic.
- **Silent Authority:** While the organisation answers to the Ascendant Hierophant, their influence often allows them autonomy, as even high-ranking clergy fear their scrutiny.
## Maintaining Faith
- **Fear & Reputation** – With such small numbers, the Ashen Confessors rely on legend and dread to enforce obedience. Many fear their judgment long before they ever arrive.
- **Selective Action** – They do not waste time on petty crimes. Instead, they focus on **high-profile heresy, internal corruption, and threats that could destabilize the faith.**
- **Informants & Espionage** – Each Confessor maintains a web of informants within cities, monasteries, and noble courts, ensuring they always know where to strike.
- **Brutal Efficiency** – Unlike a standing army, the Ashen Confessors act with **surgical precision**, striking swiftly and decisively against heretics, ensuring that the mere threat of their arrival is often enough to deter rebellion.
# Chain of Command
### Dusklord of Atonement (Voice of Atonement)
The highest authority within the Ashen Confessors, commanding all inquisitions, confessions, and purifications. The Dusklord serves as the ultimate arbiter of spiritual justice, ensuring [[Order of Light|the Order]] remains free of corruption
**Number of Members**: 1
### Penumbral Justicars (Hand of Atonement)
The battlefield leaders and master interrogators of the Confessors, each overseeing vast regions with **limited but absolute authority**. They lead purges, oversee high-profile trials, and ensure heresy is stamped out before it festers.
**Number of Members**: 3 to 4
### Purifiers (Blades of Contrition)
Rare and formidable, these warriors are the executioners and champions of the Ashen Confessors. Often working alone or in small teams, they are dispatched to eliminate the most dangerous threats, leading strike forces or executing high-value targets.
**Number of Members**: 10 to 15
### Confessors (Voices of Repentance)
The backbone of the Ashen Confessors, handling day-to-day inquisitions, interrogations, and confessions. Many operate alone, traveling between cities and monasteries to uncover heresy and deliver judgment. They are feared figures, their arrival signalling that [[Order of Light|the Order]] has turned its gaze upon the wicked.
**Number of Members**: 100 to 150
### Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)
The largest portion of the Ashen Confessors, but with fewer available positions, most will fail their training or perish during trials. The path to becoming a Confessor is brutal, requiring initiates to pass the [[#Trial of Cinders]], a test of both physical and spiritual endurance that few survive.
**Number of Members**: 300 to 400

# Rites and Rituals
## Trial of Cinders
For any Duskbound to be promoted to the rank of [[#Confessors (Voices of Repentance)|Confessor]], they must prove their faith to the ranks of their superiors. The trials are meant to purge weakness, eliminating those from their ranks that are deemed unworthy. Those that fail either leave the order, disgraced and scarred by the experience, or join the elite force of unwavering justice.

The trial has three parts, designed to test the physical, spiritual and mental strength of an Initiate. It is not simply a test of endurance, but a rite of purification.
- The Ordeal of Fire
- The Purging of Weakness
- The Ashen Oath
### The Ordeal of Fire
Either a metaphorical or literal trial by fire, [[#Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)|Initiates]] may face isolation, starvation, direct confrontation of their fears, or enduring physical pain or torture. Designed to test their commitment to the order, and faith that their god will provide them the guidance to succeed, those who falter often die or are cast out, deemed too weak to resist corruption or exact holy judgement.
### The Purging of Weakness
The Ashen Confessors do not want members of their ranks who hesitate nor do they want members who break their vows easily under pressure. The second part of the trial is designed to test force of will, either requiring the initiate to commit an unspeakable act, endure agonising pain, or even strike down another initiate who has failed [[#The Ordeal of Fire]]. Those who succeed emerge hardened, their devotion unshaken. Those who fail are judged unworthy of the burden.
### The Ashen Oath
Having proven themselves, the [[#Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)|Initiate]] must stand before a burning pyre and make a choice: Renounce the order, scarred and broken but alive, or swear the Ashen Oath and cast something of themselves into the pyre, a ceremonial and symbolic sacrifice of their old identity. Many never make it, and those who take the oath, offering themselves fully to the Order's cause, are marked forever as [[#Confessors (Voices of Repentance)|Confessors]].
