# About
[[Ashen Confessors#Dusklord of Atonement (Voice of Atonement)|Dusklord]] of the [[Ashen Confessors]], Malrik Duskveil is a solemn and unyielding figure within the [[Order of Light]]. His presence alone commands reverence and fear. With a gaze that seems to pierce the soul, he embodies both unwavering faith and the light's ability to cleanse darkness. Malrik believes redemption is earned through fire and trial, and those who resist must be purified lest they bring darkness upon the faithful.

A figure shrouded in both mystery and fear, Malrik is said to have never spoken an unnecessary word, his voice carrying the weight of finality. Once a Confessor of unparalleled skill, Malrik earned his title through ruthless efficiency in rooting out corruption within both the clergy and nobility. He is rarely seen outside of his sanctum, issuing orders through his [[#Penumbral Justicars (Hand of Atonement)|Penumbral Justicars]], but when he does emerge, it is always for a matter of dire consequence—a reckoning that none can escape.

Referred to as the orders greatest weapon by some, and darkest necessity by others, even among the [[Ashen Confessors]] he is feared. His [[Ashen Confessors#Penumbral Justicars (Hand of Atonement)|Prenumbral Justicars]] receive their orders through sealed letters, never spoken aloud. His rise was not one of politics or ambition, but of sheer inevitability. [[Order of Light|The Order]] did not choose him - he simply became the only possible choice.

Some say he has never failed to extract the truth, with no heretic ever leaving his chamber unbroken, while others whisper that his methods blur the line between divine justice and something far darker. Regardless, Malrik Duskveil is [[Order of Light|the order's]] blade, the final arbiter, and the shadow in the Light.

# Key Events
## The Black Sepulcher Purge 
As a confessor, Malrik was assigned the task of investigating a monastery formerly known as "Vaelheim's Sepulcher". It had long been a place of quiet study and contemplation, yet rumours had started to spread that its scholars had begun delving into forbidden texts, twisting the tenents of the [[Order of Light]]. Those living in the area around the holy site claimed that he both entered and left alone - seventy of the order's number left burning behind him.

The official records state that he uncovered an insidious cult within the monastery, thought none but Malrik know the full extent of what happened within the Sepulcher's walls. The ashes of the temple still smouldered when he was promoted from a [[Ashen Confessors#Confessors (Voices of Repentance)|Confessor]] to a [[Ashen Confessors#Penumbral Justicars (Hand of Atonement)|Penumbral Justicar]].
## The Betrayal of the Silver Choir
The event which cemented his authority as a [[Ashen Confessors#Penumbral Justicars (Hand of Atonement)|Prenumbral Justicar]], a highly respected conclave of priests known as the Silver Choir had fallen into heretical doctrine. Claiming that their divine visions superseded the will of the [[Order of Light#Ascendant Hierophant|Ascendant Hierophant]], it wasn't long before their misgivings reached Malrik.

When confronted, they did not resist - at first. Malrik interrogated their leaders for three days and nights, after which half the choir took their own lives rather than endure another moment under his scrutiny. The survivors were brought before [[Order of Light|the Order's]] Grand Council, where Malrik personally conducted the public trial and execution of the remaining heretics, burning them alive in front of the assembled faithful.

His cold and measured handling of the situation, weighted with the divine scriptures of the [[Order of Light]], were a defining moment of his ascension. His was the light that exposed darkness within the clergy itself while maintaining [[Order of Light|the Order's]] dignity.
## The Year of Hollow Thrones
The [[Order of Light]] had long maintained a delicate balance with the noble houses, ensuring faith and law remained separate yet intertwined. However, an unnamed noble challenged [[Order of Light|the Order's]] authority, believing [[Ashen Confessors|the Confessors]] to be overreaching. A series of mysterious disappearances followed, with lords, clerics and scholars vanishing without a trace. Some resurfaced, broken and repentant, while others were found swinging from trees with no explation.

By the year's end, ten noble seats and there high-ranking clergy positions lay empty. The [[Order of Light#Ascendant Hierophant|Ascendant Hierophant]] named Malrik as the heir apparent to the aging Dusklord shortly after, acknowledging that his methods, though brutal, had restored [[Order of Light|the Order's]] absolute control.
