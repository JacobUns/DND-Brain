# About
Following the [[Light Domain]], the Order of Light are a religious order who live to the virtues of their god and strive to live with honour, truth and vigilance. As one of the oldest religious orders of [[Aothir]], the Order of Light has a foothold in many parts of the world. Their temples are architected as grandiose structures, either standing open to the elements, bathing their followers in natural light, or with either massive stained-glass windows, splashing beautiful and vivid colour over its altars and iconography.

It's members are often seen in cities holding services, meeting with people in their homes or preaching the virtues of living with honour, speaking and living with truth in their hearts, and maintaining vigilance for those that would look to deceive and cause them to stray from their path.

Considered by its followers as a faith which celebrates purity, the Order has come under attack historically as overly indulgent and, ironically, blind to the plight of peoples around the continent. Its ostentatious designs and elaborate use of expensive materials make some feel that it rubs salt in the wounds of those that live modest lives. Conversely, among the nobility the faith is very prevalent with noble families often making hefty donations in the orders name. It is also, therefore, a powerful presence in the noble courts in an advisory capacity.
# Doctrine
## The Everlasting Radiance
The Light is eternal, unyielding, and pure. It is the flame that drives back the darkness, the beacon that leads the lost, and the warmth that kindles hope in all hearts. Every member of the Order is a bearer of this flame, tasked with spreading its radiance to the world. Members of the Order must walk this path with truth in their hearts, as deception weakens the soul, and keep watch, as the darkness never truly sleeps.
## The Threefold Mandate
Every member of the Order of Light follows the sacred Threefold Mandate:
### Illuminate the Path
Bring knowledge, truth, and wisdom to the ignorant. The Light does not suffer deception, nor does it allow ignorance to fester. The faithful must guide others to righteousness through teaching, healing, and revelation. Guidance is sacred, and to lead another into wisdom is to honour the Light itself.
### Burn Away the Taint
Purge corruption, sin, and shadow wherever they may hide. Whether by word or steel, the Light must cleanse that which festers, lest darkness take root. This does not always mean destruction, for even the lost may be redeemed—if they are willing. Honour must temper judgment, ensuring that righteousness is not twisted into cruelty.
### Stand as the Dawn
In the face of evil, never falter. The Order does not retreat before despair, nor does it bow to wickedness. To be of the Light is to be unwavering, even when the night is darkest. Renewal is a sacred duty—should one fall, they must rise again, for the dawn will always return.
### The Burden of Fire
A flame is a guiding light, but it can also burn. The faithful must know when to temper their judgment with mercy. The [[Ashen Confessors]], with [[Dusklord Malrik Duskveil]] at their head, stand as a reminder that zeal unchecked can lead to ashes, and that those that walk in light may stumble into shadow.

The Order teaches that purity of purpose must be balanced with wisdom—to destroy without understanding is to risk becoming as monstrous as the darkness one seeks to banish. Insight is a virtue, for knowing when to act is as important as knowing how.
### The Tenets of the Faithful
All followers of the Light must abide by these core tenets:
#### Truth is Sacred
Lies and illusions weaken the soul. Do not speak falsehoods nor allow them to take root in others.
#### Light and Shadow War Eternal
The battle between righteousness and corruption is unending. One must always be vigilant, for the enemy does not always wear a monstrous face.
#### Redemption is a Choice
Not all who fall are lost. Offer the way back to the Light, but never at the cost of morality. Some cannot be saved.
#### The Dawn Must Rise Again
No matter the loss, no matter the failure, the Light must endure. Even in the face of despair, one must rise again, for the Light never truly fades. This is the essence of Renewal.
#### Beauty is the Mark of the Divine
That which is beautiful—in art, in kindness, in courage—reflects the Light. Honour the beauty of the world and protect it from those who would defile it.
# Structure
The Order of Light uses the following tiered structure:
- [[#The Ascended Throne]]
- [[#Celestial Prelates]]
- [[#Hierarchs of the Covenant]]
- [[#Luminous Priests]]
- [[#Emberwardens]]

Within each tier will be one or more roles that serve specific purposes. In some instances, like [[#The Ascended Throne]], this may be one or two people whereas at the [[#Emberwardens]] may have hundreds of members in differing ranks.
## The Ascended Throne
### Ascendant Hierophant
The singular, supreme leader of the order. They are seen as the living voice of their deity and have the final say on matters of faith, war, and law. Some say they are touched by divinity itself.
## Celestial Prelates
Senior of the leadership within the [[Clerical Structures#Ordained Members|Ordained]] members of the clergy, the Celestial Prelates sit as the first tier of the [[Clerical Structures#High Clergy|High Clergy]]. Though members of the [[#Hierarchs of the Covenant|Hierarchs]] are responsible for the day-to-day leadership, the accountability for decisions lies with the Prelates and so they retain control over policy for the good of their order.
### Auric Sovereign
The highest-ranked member in a given nation. They commune directly with celestial beings or gods and make weighty political and theological decisions.
## Hierarchs of the Covenant
Most senior of the [[Clerical Structures#Ordained Members|Ordained]] ranks in the [[Light Domain]], the Hierarchs are the junior of the leadership, responsible for operation, recruitment and advisory responsibilities within the realms. Members of this tier wield both significant political and arcane power.
### Luminarch
Regional leaders overseeing multiple temples. They dictate doctrine, judge major disputes, and wield divine authority over lesser clergy.
### Herald of Light
High-ranking priests who act as envoys, spreading the order’s influence, leading crusades, or negotiating with rulers. They wield powerful divine magic and carry sacred relics.
## Luminous Priests
The first of the [[Clerical Structures#Ordained Members|Ordained]] ranks in the [[Light Domain]], the Luminous Priests are the bulk of the religious order's number. Responsible for the the general operation of the temples and interface with the populous of the kingdoms, cities and providences they operate within. 
### Radiant Watcher
Experienced clerics who oversee smaller temples and guide initiates. They are responsible for interpreting divine omens and leading larger rituals.
### Dawnkeeper
The first official rank of the clergy. They serve as preachers, healers, and defenders of the faith in temples and settlements.
## Emberwardens
[[Clerical Structures#Initiates|Initiate]] ranks of the [[Light Domain]], the Emberwardens are taught the skills of their holy order. Those that show a strength of faith begin to rise through the ranks while those that fail to do so are excommunicated from the virtuous ranks.
### Flamebearer
Acolytes who have proven themselves capable of wielding divine magic in minor ways (healing, blessings). They begin traveling with mentors to spread the faith.
### Kindled Acolyte
Initiates who have shown promise and have begun their first divine studies. They assist full clerics and undergo trials to test their faith.
### Emberborn
New recruits who have been accepted into the order. They begin by studying sacred texts, practicing prayers, and assisting in rituals.
# Special Titles & Roles
Within the order of light are optional paths for those that devote their lives to special callings.
### [[Ashen Confessors|Ashen Confessor]]
Clerics who serve as inquisitors, rooting out heresy and corruption. Given the remit to seek out and eliminate undead, demons or devils that attempt to infiltrate or corrupt the order's ranks, [[Ashen Confessors]] have full authority to root out and destroy the evil forces they discover without prejudice.
### Veilkeeper 
Guardians of forbidden or secret knowledge, often secluded in holy libraries or crypts.
### Sacred Arbiter 
Judges who oversee disputes within the order and in kingdoms that recognise their authority.
### Warden of the Dawn
Battle-chaplains who lead sacred warriors into battle against the order’s enemies.

# Roleplaying Help
- Preach Truth and dispel deception.  
- Be vigilant for corruption and stand against it.
- Treat knowledge and wisdom as sacred and use them to bring others to the light.  
- Honour the beauty of the world, seeing it as a reflection of the divine.  
- Hold themselves accountable, for even those who walk in Light can cast a shadow.
