# Chromatic
Dragons whose scales are coloured, rather than reminiscent of gems, metals or flora, are typically evil creatures that operate as agents of their own, and often selfish, desires. They seek power, treasures or dominion over others, and are willing to do what it takes to grow their hoards.

## Black
**Breath Weapon**: Acid
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Cruel and vicious, they delight in suffering and ruin. They seek to tear down all they see and rule over what remains.

**Description**: Terrifying creatures with curved horns and withered visages suggestive of fiendish skulls. Their wings end just back of their hind legs.

**Habitat**: Typically inhabit stagnant swamps, crumbling ruins, or places of magical or environmental corruption. Their acid breath scars their domains, eroding the features from ancient statues and leaving nature with festering wounds.

**Hoards**: Tarnished symbols of hope and relics of fallen empires. The more sought-after the treasure, the more black dragons prize it—particularly if they were responsible for it being lost.

## Blue
**Breath Weapon**: Lightning
**Alignment**: Lawful Evil

**Temperament**: Arrogant and imperious, they seek to transform their territories into empires, domains to be feared by nations. Ancient blue dragons think beyond dominating groups of short-lived servants. Instead, they seek to claim their places above other dragons and their world’s most powerful inhabitants. If world domination seems too tedious or cliché, these dragons might seek to seize control of extra-planar realms, multi-world empires, or forces of reality - like life, storms, or time itself.

**Description**: They have sharp features with piercing horns and scales that range from sapphire to the shades of stormy skies. Typically, Blue dragons have one large horn in the centre of their snout like a rhinoceros, but different varieties exist.

**Habitat**: Dwell in deserts and badlands, particularly regions with dramatic spires from whose tops they might see for miles. They seek lairs near sites of symbolic power, such as the abandoned fortresses of giants, the colossi of fallen empires, or monuments raised by their followers.

**Hoards**: They crave control and collect followers like other dragons hoard treasure. Regalia of rulership and artistic masterpieces fill blue dragons’ hoards. These dragons have no interest in treasures that are common or flawed, preferring one-of-a-kind gemstones, the crowns of fallen royals, and magic items capable of spreading the dragons’ influence.

## Brown
**Breath Weapon**: Sand (Bludgeoning)
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Territorial and aggressive, Browns have been known to attack any creature they perceive a threat without warning. Masters of stealth, they glide through the sand of their lair effortlessly.

**Description**: Their scales are sandy brown and almost leathery to the touch from the wear of earth across their lithe bodies. Compared to their size, their wings are far more muscular than one would expect. Spikes connected by membrane cover them head-to-tail, a perfect tool for sensing vibrations in the sand and earth around them. Their horns are slicked back against their skulls and serve a similar utility to a ram, allowing them the ability to break rock faces and obstacles as they carve their lairs from rock.

Though they have some of the most powerful wings of their draconic siblings, they are rarely seen in the air. Instead, they prefer to use them more as shovels to move earth and sand while their powerhouse tail moves as a serpent to propel them forwards. When they do fly, they are rapid; being known to cover a continent in a days travel, they usually only fly to relocate lair should a new and desirable location become known.

## Purple
**Breath Weapon**: Psychic
**Alignment**: Neutral Evil

**Temperament**: Cunning and devious, purple dragons seek to take what others have built through corruption and madness. 

**Description**: Nightmarish cousins of other chromatic dragons. Their scales are deep purple, near black, and are generally smooth. Their eyes are pale and glassy with wings that attach to their forelegs. Their bodies are are low to the ground, as they typically live deep in the underground and need to navigate narrow passageways. Their breath contains poisonous spores that corrupt the mind, and as they age these spores cause fungi to bloom across their skin, especially around their heads and necks.

**Habitat**: Also going by the name Deep Dragons, these creatures live in the depths of the Underdark in the corrupted keeps of their fallen foes. They cover their lair, caverns and tunnels with fungi from their breath, their corruption slowly spreading and expanding their reach over years.

**Hoards**: Full of the relics of their conquered empires, many a Dwarven, Duergar or Gnomish artefact can be found protected by forests of mushrooms that explode with spores as unsuspecting foes near them.

## Amethyst
**Breath Weapon**: Gravitational (Force)
**Temperament**: Generally aloof creatures, dwelling in isolation for long periods of time and rarely cultivating connections with other creatures. When they do connect with others, it is most often for intellectual and philosophical pursuits or to fulfil some arcane purpose involving the dragons’ echoes across the worlds.
**Alignment**:
**Description**:
**Habitat**: Prefer lairs near water, ideally mountain lakes, where they take advantage of existing caves and tunnels. A lair behind a cascading waterfall is ideal, and often part of that lair is submerged, allowing the dragon to rest in the water and cultivate the fish that are their preferred food. Frequently exhibit or develop large and beautiful clusters of amethyst crystals, including massive cathedral geodes split open to show their contents and clusters of crystal points more than a foot in diameter that extend six to ten feet from the interior stone surfaces of the lair. These crystals resonate with the dragon’s presence, and glow with a soft inner light. The older the dragon, the deeper the purple color of the crystals.
**Hoards**: Often collect crystals and gems, particularly their namesake stones, in raw, polished, or cut forms. They have less interest in coins and precious metals, although many have a fascination with metal items that have a silvery finish. Amethyst dragons favor treasures with particular scholarly or philosophical value as a part of their hoard, including fine books, scrolls, or carved tablets; tapestries or woven “tomes” of embroidered cloth; complex scientific instruments; and religious icons or objects. They are fond of unique and unusual art objects, especially those connected to far-off or long-lost cultures, other [[planes of existence]], or other worlds—and of magic items that allow movement between those planes or worlds.

# Tail Weapons
Tail weapons are a home brew addition to the fifth-edition armoury of dragons. They can either be used as actions, for a weapon attack, or as a legendary action with increased ferocity. All varieties of dragon can exhibit different "breeds" based on their tail shape.
## Types
### Club-tail
**Rarity**: Common
**Traits**: Vicious bludgeoning attack
**Scholar's Description**: Club-tails are the brutes of the dragon world. Head-to-tail muscle ripples their body, their broad set frame showing the inherent power they possess. Their tails end in a knot of thick hide and scale. As its club-tail cuts through the air with immense strength, it leaves the sound of a rushing vacuum in its wake. Club-tails, especially of the Black or Brown variety, have been known to use their tails to destroy fortifications when attacking cities.
