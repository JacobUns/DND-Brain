## Black
**Breath Weapon**: Acid
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Sadistic and vicious, they delight in suffering and ruin. They seek to tear down all they see and rule over what remains.

**Description**: Terrifying creatures with curved horns and withered visages suggestive of fiendish skulls. Their wings end just back of their hind legs.

**Habitat**: Typically inhabit stagnant swamps, crumbling ruins, or places of magical or environmental corruption. Their acid breath scars their domains, eroding the features from ancient statues and leaving nature with festering wounds.

**Hoards**: Tarnished symbols of hope and relics of fallen empires. The more sought-after the treasure, the more black dragons prize it—particularly if they were responsible for it being lost.

## Blue
**Breath Weapon**: Lightning
**Alignment**: Lawful Evil

**Temperament**: Regal and ruthless, they seek to transform their territories into empires, domains to be feared by nations. Ancient blue dragons think beyond dominating groups of short-lived servants. Instead, they seek to claim their places above other dragons and their world’s most powerful inhabitants. If world domination seems too tedious or cliché, these dragons might seek to seize control of extra-planar realms, multi-world empires, or forces of reality - like life, storms, or time itself.

**Description**: They have sharp features with piercing horns and scales that range from sapphire to the shades of stormy skies. Typically, Blue dragons have one large horn in the centre of their snout like a rhinoceros, but different varieties exist.

**Habitat**: Dwell in deserts and badlands, particularly regions with dramatic spires from whose tops they might see for miles. They seek lairs near sites of symbolic power, such as the abandoned fortresses of giants, the colossi of fallen empires, or monuments raised by their followers.

**Hoards**: They crave control and collect followers like other dragons hoard treasure. Regalia of rulership and artistic masterpieces fill blue dragons’ hoards. These dragons have no interest in treasures that are common or flawed, preferring one-of-a-kind gemstones, the crowns of fallen royals, and magic items capable of spreading the dragons’ influence.

## Brown
**Breath Weapon**: Sand (Bludgeoning)
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Territorial and aggressive, Browns have been known to attack any creature they perceive a threat without warning. Masters of stealth, they glide through the sand of their lair effortlessly.

**Description**: Their scales are sandy brown and almost leathery to the touch from the wear of earth across their lithe bodies. Compared to their size, their wings are far more muscular than one would expect. Spikes connected by membrane cover them head-to-tail, a perfect tool for sensing vibrations in the sand and earth around them. Their horns are slicked back against their skulls and serve a similar utility to a ram, allowing them the ability to break rock faces and obstacles as they carve their lairs from rock.

Though they have some of the most powerful wings of their draconic siblings, they are rarely seen in the air. Instead, they prefer to use them more as shovels to move earth and sand while their powerhouse tail moves as a serpent to propel them forwards. When they do fly, they are rapid; being known to cover a continent in a days travel, they usually only fly to relocate lair should a new and desirable location become known. 

**Habitat**: Brown Dragons thrive in arid wastelands, dune seas, jagged canyon networks, and sun-scorched badlands. They carve labyrinthine lairs beneath dunes and rock formations, where winding tunnels and hidden chambers provide both security and ambush points. The entrances to their dens are often carefully concealed beneath collapsed sandbanks or natural cliff faces, making detection almost impossible. Regions prone to sandstorms and seismic shifts are particularly desirable, as the unstable terrain serves as a natural defence against invaders and predators alike.

**Hoards**: Browns are obsessive collectors of dominance and survival tools rather than simple wealth. While they still covet gold, jewels, and enchanted weaponry, their hoards favour rare desert minerals, ancient artefacts tied to elemental power, and magical resources that grant them greater control over their harsh environments. Many also curate trophies from defeated rivals; bleached bones, shattered weapons, and scorched banners, displayed prominently within their lairs as warnings to intruders. To a Brown Dragon, the worth of treasure is not measured by value alone, but by the power and authority it represents.

## Grey
**Breath Weapon**: Necrotic
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Paranoid and Cruel, they regularly trap their lairs with torturous inventions and wards that cause their prey a slow death.

**Description**: Grey dragons, also known as fang dragons, are covered in spiked carapace like scales and wicked horns. Cruel and aggressive creatures, there is suspicion that they have connection to demonic forces that give origin to their breath of necrotic pestilence.

**Habitat**: Greys make their homes in rocky and mountainous regions, often covering the entrance to their lair with boulders before leaving to hunt. Some theorise they are simply [[#White|White Dragons]] that have acclimatised to non-icy conditions over millennia, favouring the warmer climate and losing their brilliant white scales to camouflage with the rocky mountains.

**Hoards**: Grey dragons hoard the spoils of their followers. Typically favouring gold and jewels, their stash of wealth also regularly contains family heirlooms and items of power that have been swept up in their followers path. The rewards are second to the power and control over those within their territory, but are taken to maintain their dominion. Roving bands of Goliaths pillage and raid the countryside, wild men seize goods from trade routes, and pirate fleets intercept costal plunder, but all must pay the force of nature that ensures their raiding continues.

## Green
**Breath Weapon**: Poison
**Alignment**: Lawful Evil

**Temperament**: Ambitious and Insidious, Greens are creatures of incredible cunning. Their followers and foes rarely see them, their magic and well-hidden agents stoking suspicion between allies and undermining enemies.

**Description**: Greens are the smallest of their siblings. Though still gargantuan in size, their bodies are more lithe and built for agility and speed. They loathe violence, seeing it as work for their minions. 

**Habitat**: From ancient forbidden forests depths, woodlands and glades that may look pristine at first glance, Greens seek to hide their presence through complex labyrinths of wilderness where they may hide for centuries. Their seat of power often sits at the heart of forested ruins they've conquered, corrupting the tallest and most ancient tree to create hollowed out lair entrances, caverns amid the mighty roots, and branches of compounding and interwoven lattices.

**Hoards**: Greens hoard the secrets of others, prioritising compromising documents, family heirlooms and sentimental treasures that can be used to manipulate those they wish to undermine.

## Orange
**Breath Weapon**: Petrification
**Alignment**: Neutral Evil

**Temperament**: Callous and Ferocious, these dragons are volatile, unpredictable predators that revel in destruction for its own sake. They act without remorse, shifting from calculated patience to explosive violence in an instant. Some see their petrifying breath as a form of artistry, leaving behind eerie landscapes of frozen victims as both warnings and trophies.

**Description**: Their scales shimmer in vibrant burnt oranges and sunset yellows, mottled with irregular streaks of volcanic black. A jagged crest of horns and ridges runs down their elongated neck, and their powerful jaws are lined with teeth shaped like serrated obsidian shards. Unlike their more disciplined kin, their movements are erratic — fluid and graceful one moment, thrashing and destructive the next. The webbed spines along their wings harden to a stone-like finish, enabling them to batter prey mid-flight or carve through rocky terrain when pursuing targets into their lairs.

**Habitat**: Orange Dragons favour unstable, chaotic landscapes where others fear to tread; volcanic valleys, jagged badlands, crumbling canyons, and petrified forests. They often choose lairs near regions marked by seismic instability, where lava flows and sudden rockslides reshape the land. These locations serve as natural defences, and many lairs are littered with stone effigies of prey, petrified mid-flight or mid-scream, a macabre gallery of their dominance.

**Hoards**: Orange Dragons collect sculptures, relics, and artistic masterpieces, but their most prized trophies are those they create themselves. Petrified victims, mortals, beasts, and even rival dragons, are posed deliberately, forming grim tableaux within their lairs. While they covet gemstones and metals like their kin, their collections lean heavily toward rare minerals, fossils, and crystalline formations whose hues mirror the molten tones of their scales. To an Orange Dragon, wealth is measured not only in gold but in the perfection of the displays they curate from the lives they’ve stolen.

## Pink
**Breath Weapon**: Charm
**Alignment**: Lawful Evil

**Temperament**: Pink Dragons demand adoration, obedience, and absolute devotion from all who encounter them. Unlike most dragons, who view their followers as disposable, Pinks cultivate their minions like a living congregation. To them, reverence is a right, worship is currency, and betrayal is an unthinkable sin. Their influence spreads like perfume, sweet and intoxicating on the surface, but poisonous beneath the allure.

**Description**: Pink Dragons are strikingly beautiful, their opalescent scales shifting in iridescent waves of rose, lilac, and pearlescent white. Their slender, graceful forms give them an almost celestial presence, and their frilled crests ripple like silk in the wind. Unlike their brutish kin, Pinks prefer to dazzle rather than intimidate, using elegance as a weapon as sharp as any claw. Their long, arching horns are adorned with naturally forming ridges resembling ornamental crowns, an accidental but fitting reflection of their self-styled divinity.

**Habitat**: Pinks favour regions of opulence and cultural grandeur, often establishing lairs beneath ancient temples, forgotten amphitheaters, or palaces consumed by ruin. They seek locations where the echoes of worship linger; sacred groves, long-abandoned shrines, or the hollowed halls of fallen civilisations. Within these lairs, they cultivate extravagant courts of enthralled servants and supplicants, their chambers draped in silks, mosaics, and carved effigies depicting the dragon in scenes of imagined glory. For Pinks, their lair is not simply shelter, it is a cathedral to their own perfection.

**Hoards**: Pink Dragons do not hoard gold for wealth, they hoard symbols of worship and power. Their collections are filled with jewelled icons, ceremonial relics, ancient crowns, divine sceptres, and enchanted artefacts once used to inspire reverence in mortals. More unsettling, many Pink Dragons preserve physical trophies of their followers’ devotion: portraits, vows written in blood, lockets containing lovers’ ashes, and petrified “chosen ones” posed as eternal statues in displays of loyalty. To a Pink Dragon, treasure exists to affirm their godhood, and every artefact is another thread in the tapestry of their own myth.

## Purple
**Breath Weapon**: Psychic
**Alignment**: Neutral Evil

**Temperament**: Cunning and devious, purple dragons seek to take what others have built through corruption and madness. 

**Description**: Nightmarish cousins of other chromatic dragons. Their scales are deep purple, near black, and are generally smooth. Their eyes are pale and glassy with wings that attach to their forelegs. Their bodies are are low to the ground, as they typically live deep in the underground and need to navigate narrow passageways. Their breath contains poisonous spores that corrupt the mind, and as they age these spores cause fungi to bloom across their skin, especially around their heads and necks.

**Habitat**: Also going by the name Deep Dragons, these creatures live in the depths of the Underdark in the corrupted keeps of their fallen foes. They cover their lair, caverns and tunnels with fungi from their breath, their corruption slowly spreading and expanding their reach over years.

**Hoards**: Full of the relics of their conquered empires, many a Dwarven, Duergar or Gnomish artefact can be found protected by forests of mushrooms that explode with spores as unsuspecting foes near them.

## Red
**Breath Weapon**: Fire
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Cataclysmic and Tyrannical, Reds embody pure destruction. They see themselves as the pinnacle of creation, believing the world and everything within it exists to serve them, or burn. Their pride is absolute, their wrath legendary. When slighted, they do not simply punish; they obliterate entire cities, leaving ash and ruin as monuments to their dominance. Unlike chaotic killers, Reds rule with intent, seeking to subjugate kingdoms and reshape the world into their vision; a vision where they alone stand above all.

**Description**: Among the most iconic and terrifying of dragonkind, Reds are towering behemoths of muscle and flame, their scales shimmering in hues of molten crimson and obsidian black. Heat radiates from their bodies in shimmering waves, and with each breath, smoke curls between their fangs. Massive forward-curving horns crown their skulls, while jagged spines run down their colossal frames, glowing faintly with inner heat in older specimens. Their wings, vast and membranous, seem forged from fire lit storm clouds; when unfurled, they cast shadows large enough to shroud entire villages.

**Habitat**: Reds claim lairs where they can see all they dominate. They prefer volcanic mountain ranges, shattered calderas, and peaks scorched bare by ancient eruptions, selecting the tallest vantage points from which they can survey entire kingdoms. Inside their lairs, rivers of magma often flow through colossal chambers, illuminating carved spires of blackened basalt. Every surface is marked by the scars of their fury; melted stone, scorched walls, and claw marks gouged deep into rock. Their presence reshapes the land itself: forests ignite, rivers boil, and valleys transform into ashen wastelands simply from their passage.

**Hoards**: Red Dragons are the most avaricious of their kind, hoarding gold, jewels, and artefacts in quantities that dwarf the wealth of empires. But their hoards are not just symbols of greed, they are monuments to their supremacy. They covet artefacts of ancient power, the regalia of fallen kings, enchanted weapons capable of slaying gods, and the rarest treasures stolen from rival dragons. A Red Dragon’s hoard is both a shrine to their own greatness and a declaration of conquest; every piece taken, every empire toppled, a reminder that nothing, mortal or divine, can stand against them.

## White
**Breath Weapon**: Ice
**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

**Temperament**: Savage and Relentless, White Dragons are predators in their purest form, creatures of instinct and domination. They lack the grand ambitions of Reds or the cunning of Greens, but what they lack in subtlety, they make up for in ferocity and persistence. Once they choose a target, they do not stop the hunt until the prey is dead or they are. To encounter a White Dragon is to face the unyielding, brutal will of winter itself.

**Description**: White Dragons are muscular, heavily built hunters, their bodies forged for survival in the most extreme environments. Their icy-white scales blend seamlessly into snow and glacier, while faint patterns of pale blue and silver ripple across their hides like frozen starlight. Their angular skulls are crowned by jagged, backward-sweeping horns, and their breath constantly steams in the cold, forming faint halos around their fanged maws. Over time, ice clings naturally to their bodies; frozen spines, frostbitten crests, and icicle-like ridges adorn ancient Whites, giving them an almost statue-like, otherworldly appearance.

**Habitat**: White Dragons inhabit the bleakest frozen wildernesses; glacial peaks, polar tundras, and frostbitten seas where mortals rarely dare tread. They carve their lairs deep into ice caves, frozen ravines, and buried glaciers, creating elaborate networks of tunnels designed for both ambush and concealment. Many lairs sit beneath colossal icefalls or ancient permafrost layers, their entrances hidden by avalanches and drifting snowbanks. Within, the air burns with cold, and the walls are lined with frozen corpses; failed challengers preserved as warnings to intruders.

**Hoards**: While White Dragons covet treasure, their hoards reflect their predatory nature more than vanity. They collect weapons, tools of survival, rare pelts, enchanted relics, and frost-bound artefacts, valuing items that enhance their power or status as apex hunters. Unlike Reds, who amass vast displays of wealth, Whites often bury their hoards within the ice itself, forming colossal frozen vaults that are as beautiful as they are deadly to navigate.

## Yellow
**Breath Weapon**: Radiant
**Alignment**: Neutral Evil

**Temperament**: Fanatical and Inexorable, Yellow Dragons are zealous tyrants, consumed by the belief that they are the chosen executioners of divine will. Unlike the majestic benevolence of true celestial beings, their “holiness” is a mask for cruelty. When a Yellow Dragon marks a foe, there is no reasoning, no retreat, no mercy. Their judgment is absolute, and they burn entire kingdoms to ash simply to cleanse what they deem unworthy.

**Description**: Yellow Dragons are terrifyingly radiant, their scales burning with harsh, glaring brilliance, not the warm glow of sunlight, but the scorching fury of an unshielded star. Jagged halo-like horns curve violently backward from their elongated skulls, framing sunken, glowing white eyes that sear into those who meet their gaze. Those that behold them often mistake them for gold dragons before realising their terrible mistake, realising their inevitable extinction. 

**Habitat**: Yellows often choose lairs where the land itself is hostile to mortal life; desolate mesas, burning desert spires, salt-crusted canyons, and collapsed holy sites. They often claim ruins steeped in ancient divine power, desecrating temples and twisting symbols of faith into monuments of terror. Within their lairs, vast chambers are scorched into smooth, glassy perfection, their walls lined with blinding crystal veins that refract and amplify the dragon’s radiance until intruders are left disoriented, scorched, and broken.

**Hoards**: Yellow Dragons collect symbols of worship and dominion rather than wealth. They seek the relics of fallen faiths, celestial artifacts, holy weapons, radiant-bound crystals, and sacred texts, not to revere them but to corrupt and repurpose them. The crowns of kings, the regalia of high priests, and banners of once-holy armies often hang in mock displays of conquest. Among their most prized trophies are the petrified remains of prophets and champions, displayed at the edges of their lairs as a declaration: even the divine bends before them.
