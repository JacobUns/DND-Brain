# Brass
**Breath Weapon**: Sleeping Gas
**Alignment**: Chaotic Good
**Rarity**: Uncommon

**Temperament**: Diplomatic and Pacifying, Brass [[Draconomicon|Dragons|Dragons]] regularly use their eloquence and charisma to persuade their adversaries and companions alike into aiding their cause. Willing to abandon a fight before it starts, their breath puts their enemies to sleep allowing them to slip away before the fight begins.

**Description**: Magnificent to behold, these creatures scales are a mix of dark burnished browns in the undersides of their joints where shadows gather through to warm golden tones where the light glitters from them. Though Brass [[Draconomicon|Dragons|Dragons]] are rare to behold, seeing them lose control is yet rarer. Their wings begin just behind their back legs, and when spread are far larger than they may first appear.

**Habitat**: Evolving to live in desert wastelands, Brass [[Draconomicon|Dragons|Dragons]] have been forced to compete with other apex predators. As a result, they are slightly smaller than most other breeds, and hunt game the size of mountain goats and antelope. They make their homes inside desert peaks and spires, forming extensive chambers of twisting corridors filled with dead ends to dissuade intruders. 

**Hoards**: Brass [[Draconomicon|Dragons|Dragons]] are almost scholarly, their interests lying in the power of knowledge. As a result, their hoards are regularly full of ancient magic tomes, notable treaties they have made or bartered for, as well as the odd trinket of power.

# Bronze
**Breath Weapon**: Electric or Force
**Alignment**: Lawful Good
**Rarity**: Uncommon

**Temperament**: Inquisitive and Strategic, Bronze [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] have a fascination with warfare. Their intimidating appearance, covered in membraned frills, they often seek out evil and ally with humanoid armies in their humanoid form to aid their battles.

**Description**: Though most of their body is covered in reflective bronze scales, there are veins of green that run through their colourings like metal deposits in the mountainside. Their four large horns on each side of their head protruding from their cheeks point back towards their tails, with large frills on both sides of their neck. 

**Habitat**: Often living in coastal regions, they typically make their homes by the sea and make use of their aquatic abilities to conceal their underwater lair entrances. Often making their homes in naturally forming rock formations, they reinforce their lair to give them a most defensible position. Exceptional swimmers, they make prey of creatures from both the sea and land. 

**Hoards**: A history of their deeds, Bronze [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] gather trophies from their fallen foes to commemorate their victories. Their good deeds towards those they fight alongside also don't go unrewarded. Their petitioners often give large parts of the mundane spoils of war to their protectors, but they also take pleasure in salvaging from shipwrecks and the surrounding seabed. Weapons, armour and magical items lie amongst riches that tell stories of fabled heroes and mighty fallen warriors.

# Cobalt
**Breath Weapon**: Fear or Magnetic Push
**Alignment**: True Neutral
**Rarity**: Rare

**Temperament**: Domineering and Cunning, unlike many metallic [[Draconomicon|Dragons]], Cobalts are vicious towards any creature they deem weaker than them. Often, they would become vindictive tyrants that seek dominion. Unlike [[Chromatic Dragons#Blue|Blue Dragons]], Cobalt [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are not universally evil; they value strength above all else, and they have been known to ally with those that can prove themselves and become their protectorates. They are solitary creatures, coming together only to mate or when commanded by a ruling power.

**Description**: With long muzzle and tail, their scales range from deep reflective navy through to glistening metallic azures. Two horns spiral off their skulls back towards their tails and red eyes glow bright in contrast to their blue scales. Innate spellcasters, the arcane energy pulses from and through their form creating ley lines beneath the surface of their hide.

**Habitat**: Often living in deep forests and jungles, they typically make their lairs in underground complex tunnels beneath the undergrowth. Often found dominating tribes of Kobolds and other weaker beings, they use their tyranny to help defend their territory. As natural adversaries, Cobalt and Green [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are never found in the same area. As a result, some Cobalts prefer colder climate forests while Greens prefer the warmth of deep jungle as a way to mask their presence.

**Hoards**: The spoils of a Cobalt's hoard are comprised of the offerings and findings of their subjects. As payment for their protection, Cobalt [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] expect a regular flow of tributes, which regularly consist of the gold, magic items from fallen adventurers and the trophies of their expanding dominion. In the rare occasion another [[Draconomicon|dragon]] sets up within their domain, they relish the opportunity to destroy and pick through the remnants of their adversary's hoard and take the most precious of artefacts back to display.

# Copper
**Breath Weapon**: Acid and Slow
**Alignment**: Chaotic Good
**Rarity**: Very Rare

**Temperament**: Even-tempered and Sociable, Copper [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are the most reasonable of their siblings and cousins. They are known to enjoy a well-told story, compete with travellers in riddle contests, and engage in good natured humour. This made them very fond of bards, and would even go as far as to carve out space for them in their lairs to provide safe harbour in exchange for entertainment. Their sociable nature would quickly change, however, should their hoard be threatened. In this respect, they can also be seen as greedy, stingy or jealous.

**Description**: With short snouts on their stout faces, and cheek ridges that point backwards, their faces are generally thought to look pensive. Two long segmented horns that start at their brows as plates of scale extend back along their skulls. Triangular blades grow from their chin in layers show their age like rings on a tree, increasing with time. Their eyes glow with a turquoise glow that matches the lower gradients of their wings which flow from a deep copper to turquoise. Their scales are deeply coloured and reflective in the light, glistening with metallic fragments and shift from deep browns to an almost amber.

**Habitat**: Living primarily in mountain foothills, hilltops of highlands and naturally forming cavern systems, they typically use their acid breath to wear away stone and form a living space fit for their size. Their lair corridors are sinuous and feature twists, turns and dead-ends. They are also fond of creating hidden passages or false walls from clay and stone thin enough to burst through. The narrowest of their tunnels are just wide enough for their bodies to fit through and often loop onto the main system to ensure they don't need to back up within the complex maze.

**Hoards**: Great lovers of burnished metals and gemstones, they also love fine works of art, tapestries and sculptures. They also keep trophies from the games of whit and trickery they come to so love. These range from all manner of items, from crowns, armour and weapons to oddities and trinkets.

# Electrum
**Breath Weapon**: Weakening Gas (Similar to Ray of Enfeeblement and Confusion)
**Alignment**: Neutral Good
**Rarity**: Exceedingly Rare

**Temperament**: Peaceful and Formidable, Electrum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are philosophical minds that enjoy engaging in debate, and have been known to welcome visitors to trade. Even so, they prefer to live far from any civilised nations, keeping to their solitary thought and contemplative nature. Though they are naturally peaceful, should they come under threat they are tenacious fighters, their talons and teeth proving more than capable of ripping a man in two.

**Description**: Deep Green glowing eyes peer out from metallic tan to yellow hued scales. A pale yellow mix of silvers and golds glint and reflect sunlight, as powerful wings propel them through secluded realms. Their horns fan back, lying almost flat along their skulls, spikes continuing down their spine. Fierce talons and teeth like razors protrude their maws and feet.

**Habitat**: Often searching for the most remote locations in the world, Electrum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] will often make their homes on top of the most challenging climbs, through the thickest jungle, in the [[Shadowfell]], or in the [[Feywild]], where they sequester themselves in locations overlooking beautiful landscapes in peaceful serenity. With their preference for this environment, Electrum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] may be immobile for significant periods before venturing out to feed or bring back their next work of art for their hoard.

**Hoards**: Unlike some other [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] that prefer items of wealth, Electrum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] prefer beautiful sculpture, great works of literature and history, pristine artefacts of intricate detail and skilful mastery, or innately powerful magical artefacts and resources.

# Gold
**Breath Weapon**: Fire
**Alignment**: Lawful Good
**Rarity**: Rare

**Temperament**: Graceful and Self Righteous, Gold [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are considered by many to be arbiters of law and justice but under their own lens of moral and ethical standards. To many humans, they can seem arrogant and self-serving, but this is simply due to the length they live and experience they have dealing with short-lived mortals.

**Description**: Many scholars consider Gold [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] to be the most majestic and graceful of their kind. This is like due to the undulating way they fly through the sky as if they are swimming, their long sail like wings and tail rippling as they move with ease. They are often characterised by having a short face and long horns, with spines that protrude and hang from their nose like long whiskers. Like their scales, their eyes are a deep liquid gold that seems to see too much. Their scales are impressive, interlocking plates that cover the creature from head to tail provide almost impervious armour.

**Habitat**: Typically living in warmer climates, Gold [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] can be found in many locations you wouldn't expect to find them. Often making their lairs in crumbling shrines, towers reclaimed by the wilderness, and fallen fortresses, all of which appeal to a gold [[Draconomicon|dragon]]’s sense of history, they can also be found in locations of picturesque natural, and at the bottom of serene lakes.

**Hoards**: Oddly, Gold [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] consider precious gems and natural growing semi-precious stone like Pearl to be a delicacy. As a result, their hoard may often contain a large portion of precious and semi-precious raw gemstones. They also delight in items they can learn from; rare histories, metaphysical texts, mysterious relics and planar puzzles and prophecies often fill their halls and keep the [[Draconomicon|dragon]] enraptured in their secrets.

# Iron
**Breath Weapon**: Petrification (To Iron rather than Stone)
**Alignment**: Lawful Neutral
**Rarity**: Uncommon

**Temperament**: Steadfast and Determined, Iron [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are savage believers in structure and hierarchy. Many believe that they have lost the prestige that was once bestowed upon them by the races of the world, and attempt to regain their once lofty place as one of the most powerful [[Draconomicon|dragon]] breeds. Counter to many other types of [[Draconomicon|dragon]], Irons like to live in units of 2-3 called Clans. The oldest and/or most powerful rules the Clan, and Clans recognise a single Sovereign. It is rumoured that the Clan leaders have a telepathic link to the Sovereign, but these rumours have never been proven as the [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are protective of their secrets.

**Description**: With broad, almost shovel shaped, heads with multiple backwards pointing horns, the scales across their brows and jowls shield their skulls almost like a knight's helmet. Thick scale spikes run down the length of their bodies, covering their broad shoulders like plate armour. Their scales are deep metallic greys that almost fade to black like the glossy reflections of a dark pool. Its talons are razor sharp, teeth honed for combat with armoured adversaries, and wings thick and strong enough to carry their increased weight. Due to their powerful presence, many believe their power primarily lies in their physical attributes, but Iron [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are skilled spellcasters and will generally aim to subdue or incapacitate their adversaries rather than kill them.

**Habitat**: They prefer mountainous and temperate, subtropical climates rich in raw ore deposits. In rare circumstances, they have also been found in forests where similar deposits are found or where meteorites with rare metals have landed. Their preferred climate overlaps with [[Chromatic Dragons#Red|Red Dragons]], making them natural adversaries. They see [[Chromatic Dragons#Red|Red Dragons]] as a scourge of dragonkind that stains the reputation of all [[Draconomicon|Dragons]]. With a preference towards natural materials, they also have regularly come into conflict with Dwarves, either bursting into their mines in an attempt to take over or with Dwarves attempting to pillage a [[Draconomicon|dragon]]'s hoard.

**Hoards**: An Iron [[Draconomicon|Dragon]]'s hoard is primarily made up of large amounts of iron ore, requiring it for both sustenance and requiring it as part of their reproductive cycle. They collect many types of metal, gem and natural resource, as well as the artefacts of their conflict with their adversaries.

# Mercury
**Breath Weapon**: Line of Poisonous White Light
**Alignment**: Chaotic Neutral
**Rarity**: Exceedingly Rare

**Temperament**: Unstable and Unpredictable, Mercury [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are dangerous to be around. Their moods shift rapidly from placid to aggressive based on their recent experiences; the more they've seen, the more patient they are likely to be. Their attention span is short, and bored or frustrated Mercurial [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are likely to lash out with no warning. Scholars believe that their emotional states are also connected to their mental state, but this is entirely theoretical as no scholar has been able to sit with a Mercury [[Draconomicon|Dragon]] for long enough to get anything of use without risking an untimely end.

**Description**: These beautiful [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] have an almost otherworldly appearance. Their completely smooth and sleek bodies have a mirror like sheen, their scales perfectly overlapping with no perceptive gaps. Their bodies are almost serpent-like, nothing like their cousins broad chests and rippling muscular forms. Their glassy bodies are pure silver, but their reflective nature picks up colour from its surroundings, making light dance across its hide. Built for speed, they appear to be the most aerodynamic of their kind. Scholars believe their long whiplike tail is both used as a rudder in the air for sharper manoeuvring and as a weapon that lashes with unnatural speed. 

**Habitat**: Another reason for the lack of sightings, Mercury [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] like to make their lairs in areas of high volcanic activity, tunnelling into lava tubes and locations near portals to the plane of Elemental Chaos. They favour these areas due to their constant change and volatile climates. 

**Hoards**: Mercury [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] don't hoard any particular type of items or wealth. Their lairs are a chaotic mix of jewels, coins, matched sets of collectable items, artefacts and antiques from eras long passed. They are willing to trade, especially duplicate items within their collection, for curios and oddities they have never seen before, but finding a Mercury [[Draconomicon|Dragon]] to do so is a challenge that few are willing to undertake.

# Platinum
**Breath Weapon**: Radiant
**Alignment**: Lawful Good
**Rarity**: Legendary

**Temperament**: Serene and Just, Platinum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are considered by many of a religious nature as the symbol of divine justice. As with many religious figureheads, Platinum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are linked with the ideologies of peaceful meditation, righteous justice, and divine wisdom or guidance. 

**Description**: Even by [[Draconomicon|dragon]] standards, Platinum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are fabled to be enormous, making their cousins seem almost childlike in comparison. Beautiful, elegant and yet intimidating creatures of supreme majesty and magic, Platinums have such a mythical status that descriptions of their appearances vary drastically. The best depictions scholars have are those done by the followers of Bahamut. They depict a massive [[Draconomicon|dragon]] with frilled jowls, long horns that point back to their tails, and membrane frills like a serrated knife down the [[Draconomicon|dragon]]'s spine. Their scales are glossy silver with tan colouring down their neck and belly. Their tails are feathered with long strands of hair and their wings are so big they block out the sun.

**Habitat**: As as much a religion and myth as many of the gods, depictions of Platinum [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are often portrayed against a background of divine clouds and radiance. Other older depictions have them in arid planes and mountainous regions.

**Hoards**: Unknown to all, there is much speculation that Platinum [[Draconomicon|Dragon]] hoards are full of religious artefacts and meaningful texts, but this is often due to their linked nature with Bahamut.

# Silver
**Breath Weapon**: Paralysis
**Alignment**: Lawful Good
**Immunities**: Cold
**Resistances**: None

**Temperament**: Regal and Wise, Silver [[Draconomicon|Dragons|Dragons]] are protectors by nature. Compared to [[#Gold]] or [[#Bronze]], Silver [[Draconomicon|Dragons|Dragons]] do not active seek out evildoers; they wait for those in need to seek their assistance. If they see something unjust or evil in nature, they will intervene, but they do not see the maintenance of law and order as their responsibility. They typically enjoy the company of humanoids, such as Humans or Elves, and would morph into their human-form and live among them for large periods of time.

**Description**: Their scales a glistening silver flecked with metal flakes, while in their mountainous environment their scales can be mistaken for those of a white [[Draconomicon|dragon]]. Others who have seen them overhead believe they have seen the figure of Bahamut.

**Habitat**: Their habitat depends on their chosen way of life. Some live among the snow-capped mountains, vying for territory against [[Chromatic Dragons#White|White Dragons]]. Others choose to live their lives in the cosmopolitan existence of those they befriend. Of their cousins and siblings, they are the most civilised and used to engaging with cultures of other species.

**Hoards**: More than treasures of pillaged hoards and trophies of battles won, Silver [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] value mementos of those they've befriended. Whether gifts of sentimentality or tokens of thanks from grateful leaders, Silver [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] display their possessions with pride. Their hoards are an eclectic mix of items, including baubles, hats, weapons and hunting horns, and in some cases entire longships, shrunken down and preserved with their magic to display prominently. They are not entirely austere of wealth, however. They have a preference towards jewels and items that complement their colours; diamonds, turquoise, malachite, and sapphire, with colours like treasures plucked from the deep, are also coveted.

# Steel
**Breath Weapon**: Charm
**Alignment**: Neutral Good
**Rarity**: Rare
**Immunities**: Acid
**Resistances**: Poison

**Temperament**: Valiant and Headstrong, these creatures have difficulty with any kinds of absolutely authority. This makes them resistant to the leadership of [[Draconomicon|dragons]] such as [[#Bronze]] or [[#Gold]] [[Draconomicon|Dragons]], and natural adversaries to [[Chromatic Dragons]]. Generally amiable and curious creatures by nature, they tend to amalgamate into cultures providing them aid rather than attempt to rule them. Recognising and not wanting to significantly disrupt the rule of law within their chosen living territory, they politically manoeuvre themselves into positions of favour and power through use of their ability to enchant and charm individuals.

**Description**: In their natural [[Draconomicon|Dragon]] form, they have the most feline movement of their dragonkind. Their scales shine with the burnished blue-grey of steel, their wings consist of feather-like blades, and their chests and bellies are armour with large scale plates that could almost be shields. As with all [[Draconomicon|dragons]], they are innate spellcasters. Their powers include spells within the [[Cleric Domains]] of [[Cleric Domains#Knowledge|Knowledge]] and [[Cleric Domains#Trickery|Trickery]]. 

**Habitat**: Steel [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] are unlike many of their siblings and cousins. They prefer to live in their shape-changed form, similar to [[#Silver]] [[Draconomicon|Dragons]], but will choose to live in palaces, mansions, castles and towers. When hosting guests, they will make excuses that allow them to hunt secretly, the grand structures that become their lairs often setup to allow for secret ways in and out.

**Hoards**: With the lairs of Steel [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] being palaces and castles, their hoards are not immediately obvious to guests that enter their domains. They revel in expanding their reach and power within their chosen territory, and as they play their political games of [[Dragonchess]], the gifts they receive from those looking to win their favour are displayed within their lair. They have a preference towards works of art, antiques, books, finery, magic items and even gifted servants.
