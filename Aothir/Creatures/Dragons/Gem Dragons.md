# Amethyst
**Breath Weapon**: Gravitational (Force)
**Alignment**: Neutral
**Rarity**: 
**Immunities**: Frightened, Prone
**Resistances**: Force, Psychic

**Temperament**: Generally aloof creatures, dwelling in isolation for long periods of time and rarely cultivating connections with other creatures. When they do connect with others, it is most often for intellectual and philosophical pursuits or to fulfil some arcane purpose involving the [[Draconomicon|dragon]]'s echoes across the worlds.

**Description**: Frequently exhibit or develop large and beautiful clusters of amethyst crystals, including massive cathedral geodes split open to show their contents and clusters of crystal points more than a foot in diameter that extend six to ten feet from the interior stone surfaces of the lair. These crystals resonate with the [[Draconomicon|dragon]]’s presence, and glow with a soft inner light. The older the [[Draconomicon|dragon]], the deeper the purple colour of the crystals.

**Habitat**: Prefer lairs near water, ideally mountain lakes, where they take advantage of existing caves and tunnels. A lair behind a cascading waterfall is ideal, and often part of that lair is submerged, allowing the [[Draconomicon|dragon]] to rest in the water and cultivate the fish that are their preferred food. 

**Hoards**: Often collect crystals and gems, particularly their namesake stones, in raw, polished, or cut forms. They have less interest in coins and precious metals, although many have a fascination with metal items that have a silvery finish. Amethyst [[Draconomicon|dragons]] favour treasures with particular scholarly or philosophical value as a part of their hoard, including fine books, scrolls, or carved tablets; tapestries or woven “tomes” of embroidered cloth; complex scientific instruments; and religious icons or objects. They are fond of unique and unusual art objects, especially those connected to far-off or long-lost cultures, other [[planes of existence]], or other worlds—and of magic items that allow movement between those planes or worlds.
