# Changling
- **Tone:** Fluid, ambiguous, and adoptive.
- **Style:** Soft, androgynous phonemes with an arcane undertone.
- **First Names:** Often neutral; may change over time.
- **Surnames:** Symbolic or metaphorical identities (e.g., Mirrormask, Hollowtruth).
