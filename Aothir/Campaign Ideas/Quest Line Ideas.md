# Under a Blood Moon
A coven of hags or witches are kidnapping children of a town/city. The town sheriff or city mayor requests the help of an adventuring party to locate and return their children.

> [!note]- Questline Spoilers and Goals
> The coven has recently lost their leading member, a grand witch named Avella, and a witch named Adriana has taken control and wishes to elevate herself to the position of a grand witch to secure her control.
>
> Adriana has a number of servants that do her bidding, from trolls, other lesser hags, corrupted townsfolk, redcaps and other beings. They serve her in many ways, from protection of the hag lair to running errands.
>
> The coven are taking children of both sexes and intend to complete a ritual aligning with a blood moon to extract the life essence from the children. The ritual is intended to elevate one of their number to the position of the grand witch.
> 
> For the ritual to be successful, they must take a child from each month of the year. Each child must be bled, and their blood used to fill a pool big enough to fully submerge a witch within. 

# Faith is a Weapon
The party is approached by a mysterious man in a midnight blue cloak that covers his head and face almost entirely. He has learned of artefacts from a time long forgotten that, when assembled and be used in a rite that promises to grant wealth and power to the one that succeeds.

>[!note]- Questline Spoilers and Goals
>The artefacts are ancient manifestations of a god whose power was locked into seven powerful items by adventurers long dead. The god once commanded a great number of followers, and through years of turmoil and conflict the majority would be eradicated by militant forces of the ruling monarchy.
>
>A small number managed to hide themselves. When they emerged from their self imposed exile, they would come to find that their god had fallen. Over generations, the already small number would dwindle and fracture into smaller cells each researching where the deific artefacts had been stored. Factions formed and conflict between rival groups of followers looking to steal each others intel would keep their numbers limited, each believing they would be the one to return their god and win his favour.
>
>Centuries later, a few groups have independently uncovered the locations of several artefacts, and one of the cult leaders, [[Jezia Luth]], believes he has found the knowledge required to separate their deity from his power and has reverse engineered a rite capable of reuniting the power. [[Jezia Luth|Jezia]] has heard of a proficient group of adventurers who may be capable of retrieving the items. He has sent an envoy of the cult to collect the party and bring them to their underground lair.
>
>With the promise of wealth, the cult tries to employ the party with very limited information on the origin of the items. If the party goes along with the plan, they are provided the locations of two artefacts. Both are on the continent the party currently find themselves. One is in the ruined temple of the fallen god. It is here the party has their first opportunity to learn of the deity the cult is connected with. The second is held in the vault 
>
>After the first two are collected, the group start being hounded by an "artefact liberator" called [[Vessa Thornbite]]. The party has taken one of the artefacts just before she has been about to liberate for a "collector". She challenges the party to a race. She has found the location of the next item in the "collection". She knows of one other item location. If the group can beat her to the next item, then she will give the location to the group. But if they can't, they will have to give her an artefact to go back into the world to collect further stories.
>
> The party are provided the third location by [[Vessa Thornbite|Vessa]] and the race is on. It's a couple of days travel at minimum to the site cross country, and the party must succeed their travel checks or be delayed. If the group fails any of the checks on average then roll 1d4 to determine how long it takes [[Vessa Thornbite|Vessa]] to make it to the site.
