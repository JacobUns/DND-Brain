# The Workshop
> [!cite]+ Workshop Description
> From up the street you see something that looks like what the bartender described. It looks like a completely normal workshop, but with many of the businesses around the area starting up for the day, the building somewhat sticks out when you know what to look. There is seemingly no movement around the lonely looking workshop, and as you draw closer you notice that the windows are covered by drawn curtains. 

Perception check DC 9
	As you close in, you also notice that there are crooked nails jutting out from the window frames, as if someone has hastily attempted seal themselves in. On closer inspection, you see more nails hammered flat and conclude that someone has indeed sealed themselves in, and for good reason as there also appear to be crowbar marks on the frame.

> [!cite]+ Front Door Description
> On the front door, you find the peep hole has been covered over, and the mailbox removed and boarded shut. There's no perceivable handle, but a lock that has been moved 

If the characters knock:
- A curtain shifts quickly, and then from behind the door you can hear scrabbling like boxes are being moved
- The door cracks open just enough for the occupant to see you.
- One beady eye peers out, and then the door slams shut and you hear more scrabbling
- The door opens wider and a female halfling beacons you in

If the characters try to break through the front door:
- DC 15 Athletics Check breaks the lock
- The door opens onto a chain
- The door barely opens as something is blocking the other side
- A stout figure holding a small crossbow appears and fires off a bolt at the closest person, wildly missing as if the figure is using the bow for the first time.
- The figure calls out in a female voice not to try again and to make themselves known
# The Meet

- Closes the door quickly
	- "Were you followed?"

About Cerys
- A middle-aged Halfling, barely over 4ft tall
- Female, brown eyes, black hair
- She's wearing simple clothes, dyed darkly, three-quarter length trousers that fit snugly around her calves
- On a nearby table is a satchel with papers overflowing
- At her side, a simple short sword hangs and it looks new
- Used to be an accountant for the town's leadership
- Never previously broken the law

Accounts point to a single initial moniker, "A"

How she discovered the information:
- Saw a set of books she wasn't meant to
- Started following the money trail
- Found ties to a thieves guild known as The Syndicate.
- Officials are in the pocket of the guild
	- Turning a blind eye to their activities
	- Taking bribes to enable their purchasing of property
	- Law enforcement, judges, tax and accounting, and city governance
- Discovered a lot of transactions going to a bank called the Dragon's Claw Holdings

Her plan:
- She's contacted the bank pretending to be looking for an account with a bank able to be discreet.
- She hasn't been inside, but she's heard the bank uses simulacrum-like guards in the form of dragonborns to ensure privacy
- The group will need to come up with aliases
- She believes The Syndicate store material in the main vault, and she needs the party's help to retrieve it as proof
- She suggests the party travel light, and that the security are not likely to allow weapons inside the vault
- She also informs the group that, though she hasn't been inside, she understands the defences to be formidable given the less than reputable clientele

# The Bank

> As you round the corner, you see what must be the bank. It's an impressive building that's been carved into the side of a rock wall, a massive massive metal slab stands almost like a monument art piece. Across the middle is an enormous golden dragon talon. As Cerys leads you towards it, it suddenly splits down the middle and you realise that is in fact two leaves of a door which seamlessly come together at the threshold. Light seems to come through a domed ceiling that you know would be impossible from the outside, but yet its pure light shines down on a crystalline chandelier that hangs from the tall dome. It stops short of the impressive marble statue standing in the middle of the room that depicts a dragonborn clutching a sack of coins.

# The Descent
As he turns to walk, X guards emerge from the dark that seems to permeate the edges of the space and flank your group as Harig leads the way towards a corridor off the main chamber. As you walk, just off to the right you see a windowed chamber with a closed door. In the window you can see a broad set Red Dragonborn with a thick scar that runs from his temple, through his brow and down to his cheek. In his hand he holds a hefty longsword which he's sharpening with a strap and whetstone. 

Harig continues on, and starts to descend in a steep slope 35 feet before it switches back on itself and continues. It's hard to tell how deep you've gone but eventually you reach an end and start to see a line of vault doors ahead of you.

Harig turns to you and describes the area
- Private Vaults
- General bank vault for small goods, money and documents

Each vault door is easily 15 feet across. Harig guides you to the middle vault door, stops you a few feet away with the guards, and then approaches the door and murmurs something.

As he does, the door clunks heavily with the noise of moving metal and the door gently swings out with enough space to walk through the gap single file. He steps inside and fiddles with something on the wall before turning back to you, giving you a wide disingenuous smile and ushers you in.
# The Vault
As you enter, you see wall to ceiling lockers each with a key hole and a number on the front. Some are labelled, some aren't, but they're all uniform size and shape. Harig continues to talk, explaining that each are guarded with mechanical locks and you can choose to imbue each with an arcane lock if you wish.
# Traps
## Arcane Lock
You touch a closed door, window, gate, container, or hatch and magically lock it for the duration. This lock can’t be unlocked by any nonmagical means. You and any creatures you designate when you cast the spell can open and close the object despite the lock. You can also set a password that, when spoken within 5 feet of the object, unlocks it for 1 minute.

## Cloudkill Gas
You create a 20-foot-radius [Sphere](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#SphereAreaofEffect) of yellow-green fog centred on a point within range. A 20-foot-radius sphere of yellow-green fog starts to flow from the vents in the lobby. The fog lasts for the duration or until strong wind (such as the one created by [Gust of Wind](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619137-gust-of-wind)) disperses it, ending the spell. Its area is [Heavily Obscured](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#HeavilyObscured).

Each creature in the Sphere makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 5d8 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the Sphere moves into its space and when it enters the Sphere or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The Sphere moves 10 feet away from you at the start of each of your turns.

## Private Vaults

### 🔐 Vault I – “The Iron Dividend” _(Wealth-Heavy)_
#### Opening
- **Thieves’ Tools (DC 17):** Pick the primary lock.
- **Investigation (DC 17):** Identify hidden traps or false keyholes.
- **Arcana (DC 17):** Disrupt a minor arcane seal.
- **Strength (Athletics, DC 18):** Force the door if all else fails.

**Failure:** Alarm sounds, summoning guards in 2d4 rounds.
#### Contents
_Owned by a merchant prince who believes gold buys more power than magic._
- 💰 **Wealth:**
    - 35,000 gp in platinum trade bars and bearer bonds.
    - 10,000 gp in gemstones cut for fast resale.
    - 5,000 gp in foreign promissory notes and mercantile shares.
- 📜 **Leverage:**
    - Ledgers revealing systematic price-fixing and extortion rackets across three trade guilds.
    - Letters proving collusion between the vault’s owner and two sitting councillors.
- 🪄 **Magic (Minimal):**
    - **Periapt of Proof Against Poison** (_Rare_).
    - **Quaal’s Feather Token (Tree)** (_Rare_) – a bribe disguised as a “gift.”

🪩 _Feel:_ Pure wealth. Little immediate combat power — but selling the contents could fund an army or buy a noble title.
