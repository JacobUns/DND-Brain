# Session Context
You have all been adventuring as mercenaries for some time, making money going job to job protecting caravans, removing problematic pests and threats, and are not against the occasional “retrieval” of “lost or misplaced” items. Mercenaries are a dime a dozen though, and these kind of activities don’t pay well enough to properly equip you to take that next step into hero status, and start earning some real money. You’ve recently arrived in the town of [[Emray]], a medium sized mining town a short distance from the foothills of the mountains that fuel its economy. In the other direction, the city of [[Felris]], a financial powerhouse on the continent renowned for its markets and banks. It's been a long journey to [[Emray]] and you have decided to stay in a modest tavern called [[Emray#The Singing Harper|The Singing Harper]].
