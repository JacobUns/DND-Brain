Each card player rolls 1d8, keeping the die hidden. Each player then has the chance to raise the bet, call the bet, or fold. The first player must bet or fold. When all players have made equal bets, play continues.

Each player then rolls a 1d6, keeping it secret as well. Starting from the same player as the previous round, a final chance is given to each person still in play to bet, raise, call, or fold. Again, once all players have made equal bets, play continues.

Each remaining player rolls 1d4 and reveals their hand. Adding together all the face values, the winner of a hand is determined by the person with the highest total. The winner takes 80% of the pot, with the other 20% going to the casino. Ties split the 80% equally.

After each hand, the next player clockwise in the order starts play. Between hands, players can choose to leave the table and concede, or continue to play. A round finishes when no other players are willing to continue.
