A two player game, the dice rolls stand in for the act of playing a game similar to our own version of chess. To emulate the skills associated, each player makes three rolls. Emulating the ability to understand what an opponent is doing, the players roll for **Insight**. Emulating the ability to deceive an opponent into falling into traps, the players roll for **Deception**. Finally, to emulate a player's proficiency at the game, each player makes a **Dragonchess** check using their **Intelligence** modifier. If a character is proficient in **Dragonchess**, the player adds their proficiency bonus to their **Intelligence** Modifier.

The winner is determined based on the winner of two out of three rolls. If the result is equal, then the winner is determined by the player with the highest combined total. In the unlikely event this is equal also, the match is determined a stalemate.
