# Description
A land known for its sprawling forests and plains, Eryndra is considered a land of plenty flush with farmland and forest gathering. On the Northeastern bulge of [[Merathis]], its mighty trees, nourished by the storms, have caused many to believe the rain waters of the province carry with them magical properties, causing many farmers to migrate to the country over the centuries.
# Seasonal Feel
The seasons in Eryndra is mild by some of the province standards. The extremes of heat are felt more strongly than many other areas of the continent, and the [[Seasons of Aothir#Highsun|Highsun]] storms are violent enough that homes are architecturally designed to resist the worst of their impact. 
## [[Seasons of Aothir#Greentide|Greentide]]
- Explosive regrowth; forests drip with dew, the canopy dense and alive.
- Migratory creatures return; air thick with sound.
- Rivers flood briefly, renewing the soil.
## [[Seasons of Aothir#Highsun|Highsun]]
- Humid and storm-wracked; violent rains feed enormous trees.
- Forest folk and traders move goods by river.
- In clearings, festivals celebrate abundance and courage.
## [[Seasons of Aothir#Hearthrest|Hearthrest]]
- Heat softens; misty mornings and long amber afternoons.
- Leaves bronze and red, carpeting trails.
- Hunters gather; forest villages smoke meats and store herbs.
## [[Seasons of Aothir#Frostfall|Frostfall]]
- Rarely freezes; cool rains cleanse the air.
- The forest quiets, songs of insects replaced by distant wind.
- Woodcraft and repair season; fires crackle under vast tree canopies.
