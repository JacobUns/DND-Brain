# About
**Type**: Fashion Arena & Artisan’s Stage  
**Theme**: Self-expression, visual storytelling, rebellion, and craft mastery  
**Recognition**: **The Threadmark** - A golden sewing needle worn horizontally as a pin or brooch. It glows faintly when threaded through cloth, and whispers styling insights or fashion omens. Its bearer is recognised as a master of style and symbolism.

**Description**:  
At the edge of the artisan quarter, where cobbled streets gleam with dyes and enchanted mannequins peer from shop windows, sits **Thread & Thorn** - a converted manor-atelier turned performance hall. Beneath its vaulted ceiling of coloured glass, a **magical runway** pulses with shifting hues. Every surface is fabric-draped, pin-stuck, or humming with enchantment.

Here, clothing is **language**. A corset might confess betrayal; a cloak might weep its creator’s grief. Designs range from subtle woven runes to dramatic glamour-weaves that shimmer with emotion. The boldest designers might debut a cursed garment or spin an entire outfit in front of the crowd with a flick of the wrist.

Surrounding the central catwalk is a ring of seats filled with artisans, trendsetters, nobles, and revolutionaries—many masked, all judging. They don’t vote with applause, but with **ribbons**, enchanted to tie themselves to the work that moved them most. When enough ribbons cling to a garment, the artist receives **The Threadmark** and gains entrance to the elite world of style-magic, whispered influence, and myth-spinning wardrobe artistry.

**How It Works**:  
Any may submit a piece to walk the Thornwalk (the runway) or perform a live creation. Peer judges—only those who already bear a Threadmark - may affix their enchanted ribbon if they believe the piece meets the three ideals: expression, innovation, and intention.

Gaining enough ribbons awakens the golden needle from within the central loom, which floats to the artisan and pins itself gently to their work. This moment, always silent, is never forgotten.

**Why It Matters**:  
Threadmark bearers are advisors to nobles, outfitters to assassins, and symbols of rebellion to the common folk. Their designs are said to shape court politics, incite revolutions, or curse their wearers in elegant ruin.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Gain advantage on Charisma checks when using fashion or costume as part of your social presence or disguise
- May design enchanted clothing or accessories with minor magical effects (DM approval)
- Once per long rest, instantly mend, reweave, or stylize a piece of clothing or disguise to match a scene or persona

**Game Hooks**:
- A cursed ensemble designed for a political debut is missing. If worn, it may trigger arcane ruin - or assassination.
- A rival Threadmark bearer begins unveiling pieces that copy another’s style… and deeper secrets.
- A noble seeks a designer to craft a garment of _truth_ - woven from emotion, not cloth. But whose truth will it reveal?
