# About
**Type**: Illusory Theatre & Drama Hall  
**Theme**: Illusion, Presence, Acting, and Deception  
**Recognition**: **Silver Mask** – a wearable mask symbolising elite performance

**Description**:  
As patrons step into **The Mirror’s Call**, they are enveloped in the scent of exotic incense curling through a smoky, velvet-lined bar. The atmosphere is hushed, intimate—where laughter is soft and secrets are spoken in riddles. High-class cocktails clink beside vintage bottles of wine and champagne that seem to pour themselves with courtly grace.

On one wall looms an enormous, ornately framed mirror, pressed flush against the floor. It gives the illusion of a grander space beyond—a trick of perspective, some might think. But those attuned to magic feel a gentle pull, a pressure like a held breath. The surface shivers like mercury, and now, unmistakably, people are walking _through_ it without ripple or sound.

Crossing the mirror is a transformation.

Visitors emerge garbed in sumptuous, fantastical attire—gilded silks, shifting hues, and a mask that veils half the face in elegant asymmetry. Before them stretches a **midnight-black amphitheater**, thirty feet deep in descending terraces. The shallow incline creates an arena of pure attention. Across the chamber, an opulent stage waits, draped in velvet, lit by enchanted lanterns, and wreathed in shimmering illusion. Pinpricks of starlight twirl above, tracing the cosmos across the domed ceiling.

The opulence is almost suffocating—charged, as if the room itself is watching.

On certain nights, the mirror no longer reflects—it **shatters open**, revealing the entire space to all. These are the **nights of performance**, when the panel of masked adjudicators—**the Call**—take their seats in silent judgment. Any may step onto the stage to weave a tale, craft an illusion, or embody a role. Those who stir the panel earn **the Phantom Veil**, and with it, welcome into the elusive ranks of the Mirror’s Call.

Membership is secretive, adopted under a pseudonym. Real names are traded less often than gold. Those who earn the Veil are rumoured to be recruited as **spies, ambassadors, or court entertainers**, for few can rival their mastery of presence, illusion, and hidden truth.

**How It Works**:  
Actors, illusionists, and storytellers perform in disguise. Judges—also masked—vote anonymously after the show. If the performer can move them, a new **Silver Mask** is presented in ceremony.

**Why It Matters**:  
Silver Mask bearers are recruited as spies, diplomats, or court performers. Their reputations precede them—though their names often remain unknown.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Gain **advantage on Deception and Performance** while in disguise
- Recognised by espionage groups and theatrical circles alike, often opening secret doors

**Game Hooks**:
- A Silver Mask has gone rogue, using their reputation to manipulate a noble court
- A royal seeks an actor for a performance that is actually a covert message to allies
- A cursed mask is binding performers to their roles, even after the play ends
