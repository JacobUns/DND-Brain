# About
Growing up along trade roads, Vessa learned early that treasure belongs to those that can keep it. She doesn't steal for greed - she "corrects mistakes", liberating ancient relics from dusty vaults and hoarding nobles. Paid by collectors, temples and scholars, Vessa claims it's not about the gold - it's about keeping history alive. Her motto: "A trinket in a vault gathers dust. A trinket in the world gathers stories."

The party that encounters Vessa may have been hired to recover an item she has "liberated" - only to realise she's the more trustworthy side - or seek her out for a "no questions asked" smuggling job.
