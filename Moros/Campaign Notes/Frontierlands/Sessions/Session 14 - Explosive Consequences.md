We wake in the Brinehouse, a brand new day heralded by the breaking of a fresh sunrise. The tavern itself is fairly empty. There's clattering in the back, but no one is currently at the bar. The group discuss how we're going to approach the subject of the deaths of the canal workers. The point is raised that we don't have to solve the crime, just clear Jerome's name. The opportunity of being deputised is discussed as a legitimate manner of approaching the topic. Finally, we decide that approaching the mayor and attempting to sell our services to him is the ideal approach.

We head out onto the cobbled streets. To our right, the expansive lake and harbour is busy with dockhands loading ships ready to fish. The streets are relatively empty, and the usually bustling parts of the town are to our left. We start heading towards the town hall, hoping that is the best place to find the mayor, and as we walk several people run past us in the same direction. They appear angry, and we hear a commotion ahead of us. The square is in chaos. General townsfolk and militia fill the space which smells of tar and sweat. The mayor and Jerome are on a raised platform. The mayor clamours to what appears to be a mob ready to linch Jerome.

Jerome claims the workers choked on spores and that the canal supply lines are rotten, shouting that the beams bleed green in the rain. A worker  The wood by the south trench is soft like fungus. The mayor dismisses the claims, and eventually slams the platform declaring that the town meeting is over. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]], before everyone moves off, shouts that we will investigate. Jerome leaps on the opportunity, and shouts to mayor Thorn asking whether he will open the crime scene to us. The mayor agrees, but not before a final shot at the canal's significance to the town's economy. He shouts to a man called Booto, and instructs the man to lead the way.

Booto's a squat, broad shouldered man missing an ear. He carries a pike, but obviously prefers the cudgel at his hip. He leaves New Town from the south gate, and follows a track that ends in a construction road with lumber. He suggests not touching the lumber barehanded, as it makes the skin itch. The camp is quiet, a feeling of wrongness hanging in the air. No one seems to be working at the moment, almost as if the day hasn't started yet or that work has been ordered to stop. Booto goes to find the foreman and the group spends some time poking around the immediate area of the camp while we wait. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] goes to the smouldering fire and investigates the wood. It appears slightly rotten, the wood wet and soft, and a fungal odour emanates from it.

The tent flaps eventually open with a harsh rip. A massive man that fills the doorway, his jaw set like stone, spattered with sawdust, ash, and mud. When he sees us, his brow furrows and then turns to anger. He introduces himself as Yorick, and carries a mallet almost like it's an extension of his arm. He points to inside the tent, asking if we're here to fix Wipplebit's mess. Jerome's lot started hawking their relics near camp just before his men started coughing black. Wood is meant to come up from Lockborough, but a recent shipment was struck by lightning and burnt. Wipplebit's friends are the ones that sold him the timber, a man called Smanem, a merchant of many types of natural goods but timber isn't his speciality. His men aren't allowed to drink in camp, and have taken to lighting fires from offcuts further out from camp and drinking. The bodies were found at one such firepit. There are men in his camp that go out seeking trouble, but these are not the type.

We ask if we can see the bodies and where it happened. The pit shimmers slightly green as if catching light that doesn't pass over it. There were only the six men at the camp so no one actually knows what happened. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] inspects the firepit. A small pile of wet wood lies to the side, and [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] attempts the check for any magical influence. He and [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] both sense a hum from it.  [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] recognises it as Sycamore wood. Crushing the wood as he walks with it, [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] notices white veins running with the grain. He brings it to [[Arabella]] and points out the veins. She recognises it as fungal in nature, mycelic tendrils, but we don't believe it is naturally occurring. 

The foreman takes us into the storage tent. The bodies are pale, their fingers clenched into their palms. He continues to extoll that Wipplebit is the culprit. [[Arabella]] suggests that we can speak to the bodies. One of the men looks older, and it's hard to tell whether he's suffered the malady more than the others because he's older or because he was the origin point.

What was the last thing you remember?

[[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]], having been keeping watch, sees a horse drawn cart pulling up outside the tent. The twins are smaller than the others. Teeth stained dark, flashes of his tongue looked a bit mangled as if it was bitten.

The man that climbs down from the cart throws on a cloak of some kind and then dons a plague mask. The coroner claims we shouldn't be in the tent, as we may breathe in whatever is in the area. We walk out. He claims that he can't do any preliminary investigation as he doesn't have his tools. The coroner asks us to assist in taking down the tent and moving the bodies onto the cart. Between two or three of the group, we aid in lifting the bodies onto the cart. He leans over the last body, opening the mouth and inspecting inside. He pulls forceps from his back pocket and inspects down the throat. White veins similar to those within the wood grow from inside the mans throat. 

The coroner's workshop and lab is located opposite the Tea Shop. He claims his name is Coroner Halden Vexley, and holds his hand out. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] takes grasp of it and shakes. We show him the sycamore timber and he inspects it.

[[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] has heard of the handyman, Smanem, that is willing to do any physical jobs, and was instrumental in setting up the town, clearing the area, and building many of the buildings. 

We approach the woodyard, and as we do, the stench of decay permeates the air. The door to the yard has a single bloody handprint that smears the wood. The main shed at the end is opened, the ground covered in sawdust mixed with blood. Tracks, as though people had been stumbling around blindly, litter the area. Drag marks 

Smamen's body lies broken, chest wripped open as if something had ripped open. It appears that something has devoured parts of him. His face is unrecognisable, his jaw locked open and his right hand clutches ledger, its pages glued with blood.

In the shadows within the yard, [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] can hear there still seems to be a weak and bloody rasping coming from the shed. We realise that the handprint from the front door was on the way out of the yard, and we start to become concerned.

[[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] morphs into a spider form, and climbs inside the shed. He can see the shed is full of stock and timber but the sound of breathing is much clearer, and behind large boxes of stock lies a person in the fetal position. At some point they may have been cowering from whatever was happening outside, but now their skin is pallid and lifeless. They look dead, but yet they continue to breath. The doors seem to be blocked from the inside by large piece of equipment.

[[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] breaks down the door, and we find that it was likely one of the yard workers from the tool belt and clothing. He looks as though he's covered in burn scars, but in reality the pale fungus crawls across his skin. His eyes are milky white and follow [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] as he moves through the shed, his fingers twitching. As [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] calls out, the creature whips its head and the shallow breathes become a cough as a cloud of spores are projected into the air in front of it. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] casts a sorcerer's burst, and as the flames appear the sawdust particles catch fire and the shed explodes.

The fire starts to spread, and [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] runs to Smanem to grab the journal while [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] realises that [[Arabella]] is still on the ground and goes back for her. We all make it out safely, but the fire is still burning. The smoke starts to be billow and we can smell the same acrid smell recognised from the canal worker's fires. From the town, a fire bell starts to ring and we all make our way in that direction. We see townsfolk running towards the site; militia carrying buckets either empty and ready to fill or already carrying some water.

A couple of farmsteads stand at the south and south-east. On the walk back, there feels like we've earned some trust and camaraderie from the townsfolk. The group decides to head back to the Brinehouse to rest. We make our way through the streets, smoke stained faces passing with us. Soot drifts like snow over the town. Though the fire is no longer blowing smoke, the ash is being carried in. As we pass, the towns people clap and show thanks for our efforts. By the time we reach the Brinehouse, dusk is setting. The big room is full of people, the barman starting to give up on counting coin and pouring as quickly as possible. A small group in the corner murmur about Smanem in memory.

The smell of salt pork fills the air, and rain starts to set in outside. There seems to be some nods of respect from those that are with us. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] and [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] approach a couple of the militia that worked on putting out the fire with us, and they turn out to be dock workers. They explain that the crew of the woodyard were trained by Smanem himself. They were approximately 6 or 7 in number, and recently they had built a farmstead just south of the town and at Beggar's Copse. It's a small smattering of trees just east of town. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] probes for whether there has been any Wharf development, and he says "Not yet, but it'll happen". 

[[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] approaches a different person in the tavern, and asks if it could be a case of mistaken identity or not. The person says "There's one other gnome in town, Gerna, and she's a 'perfectly normal person'".

We make our way down to the docks looking for any sign of a delivery of timber. We walk the length of the docks, and are almost ready to give up, until [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] looks into the water and sees what looks to be a very newly made canoe. We check the ledger again and find there are two separate listings of wood sold to the Wharf. We check the canoe, and find the same white veins running through the sycamore wood. From a nearby boathouse a man walks out. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] calls out to him and asks whether he crafted the canoe. He tells us he is, and that he got the wood from the wharf. It's for the pyre, and points out a floating platform, and tells us that no one will come into contact with the canoe.
