It's limbs latices of fractal gold filigree force their way through the massive panes. The windows burst inwards, its face a jagged mask of rainbows and ghostly after images. Each limb articulates in unexpected ways and as it approaches it's like being caught in thunder and rain of shattered glass. At the top of the stairs, large razor sharp shards of glass scatter across the floor making the terrain difficult to traverse. It feels hard to get a clear view of the creature, the glass is transparent enough to cause trouble fixating on it.

Today it woke, and it tried to end you ([[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]]). Something in you has shifted, turned darkened."

He floats down to the floor and in a quieter voice than before says "You are not welcome here anymore. Leave [[Elarythia]] now. I will escort you to the city limits. To the gate house. Gather your belongings."

As we're walking, [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] tries to make pleasant conversation about what the Dreamweaver's existence is like. He leads us out of the city centre. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] and [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] try to understand if there is anything we can do to assist. As we draw closer to the gates. Huge fungal towers and a matted field of spores lie ahead of us. The Dreamweaver, starts muttering of the vale fraying and the coming of another sundering. The fabric of reality wearing thin enough that things can pass through. Not large things, but tiny and microscopic things. He tells us that a second sundering is another opportunity, an opportunity to kill all those within the Golden Sea. He believes there is a, 4-8 weeks away from another sundering. We start talking about the gate we crossed from the [[Material plane]] to the [[Feywild]], and the Dreamweaver calls them [[Xinderoth]] portals. He talks about a leak allowing the particles to make their way to this realm.

We make it back to the aurora path, but the magic feels different. More strained, like indistinct whispering. The statue's eyes flash with light, the magic folds around us and we are deposited back into the heart of the colossus. Seams appear to have developed, and it appears to be becoming corrupted. From the bottom of the statue, two figures sitting on a rock call up to us. [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] and [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] both sit together like mirrored bookends of time. They appear to be the same we interacted with recently. As [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] arrives at the bottom, [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] greets him and we talk through our journey since we parted. He says the sundering cannot be stopped, but it can be shaped. Bent. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] posits that if the spores are seeping through then we need to stop it, but [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] states it is merely a symptom. Annoyingly vague, [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] doesn't provide us any answers, but says that we need to take care after we pass back through the portal.

[[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] spends some time looking at [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]]'s arm and finds deep inside is a fungal rot. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] and [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] fail to keep him subdued, and he flails, before being fed a potion of sleep by [[Arabella]].

The arm lies inert. Then, without warning, it comes back to life controlled as if intelligent. The runes that once glowed faintly on [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]]'s arm spark to life again, but now in a way it did not before. The arm lunges for his throat.

We rest, and it's an uneventful night. We start our journey again and the [[Feywild]] passes before us. The forest is quiet around us, eerily so. The trees don't whisper the same way, and there's an undeniable tension. We feel watched, but not hunted. We see it again, ahead of us, the large stone archway. [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] speaks, "you go forward now, to where I've already been and you must return." He claims he remembers not passing through the gate with us and so he will not cross. 

We step through the portal, the world reforms around us, and we find ourselves once again in the cracked halls of the [[Xinderoth]] ruins. The eery stillness of this place feels different, like the world is suspended in amber. We pull the displacement device, and it clicks and shows 7 days previous. We hear harsh and imperious voices, and appear to hear the [[moon church]] priests. With dawning horror, we realise that they're dragging [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] into the chamber with us. Behind us, the portal is deactivated, and the voices feel very close. We look around and find a crack in the wall that allows enough space to crawl. We duck into the space and the priests enter the room with [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]]. Young [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]], with us in the crack, explains we must not let ourselves hear or see ourselves. We hunker down in the space until, 15 minutes later, we hear ourselves approach. We wait, until we don't hear voices. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] pokes his head out, but it's very slightly too early. He sees him, and suddenly his vision spins and a ripping psychic pain splits his skull.

We descend deeper into the ruins, and Netherbane starts talking to [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] again. It says to find him, that he's close. It's eager to find itself. [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] notices that [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] seems to be leading them. He pulls the group through forks in the paths. [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] stops him and interrogates, but [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] says its just around the corner and it's nearly there. Eventually, we hit a dead end. Netherbane vibrates. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] feels the wall and finds a slit in the wall with his hands. He pulls Netherbane and slots it into the gap. We continue down a spiral staircase, and find a circular room covered in runes and wards. At the centre of the vault is a stone cradle. The cradle seems to be small enough for an infant, with stasis glyphs carved around its edge. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] states that he remembers this space. We inspect, and [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] reaches out to touch the surface. A pulse ripples outwards, and lines of light bloom across its surface. The shape of an infant is traced within, and golden light casts strange light across [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]]'s face. Just for an instant, he feels an echo of stillness, and then something seems to change. A tightness coils in his chest, like the air in the vault has gone metallic. His skin starts to grey, veins glow beneath the surface, his features sharpening. His skin begins to ripple, and small nubs like horns start to emerge like something forgotten. The moment passes, but the change doesn't. Undisguised. The human skin he wore since childhood left behind. He is [[Xinderoth]], the last of his kind reborn in their ruins.

[[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] looks around the room and can suddenly read the runes. He realises that this tells the end of the [[Xinderoth]] people.
