We awake in the Vault of Continuance. Pale light leaks from crystal braziers, the air not stirring as if sealed against time itself. The great stone plinth of the cradle still hums with psionic radiance. The walls of splendid stone telling the stories of its people.

[[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] approaches [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] who, having returned to his previous form, is met with comments of how easy it will become to swap between. He mentions that he has seen [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] in the future several times, and he has seen [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] exhibit abilities that he will not be able to in his human form. He claims cause and effect are not a line, but a tapestry, that the flow of time for his is more like a knot pulled tight, him as the rope, and his future difficult to know for inspection yet he knows what he must do.

He mentions how [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] pulls out his Nairometer from Wipplebit, an auctioneer in a town near [[Pefergee]] that [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] recognises. The auctioneer is called Jerome Wipplebit and has his auction house New Town. Under his breath he says the weave could twist. Having claimed he will be with us for the foreseeable future, he realises he has seen the wrong future. He says he must check with his older self to see if he remembered making the mistake he has, or whether this was always meant to happen. 

[[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] subtle casts Detect Thoughts. [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] has an overwhelming sense of uncertainty whether he's done the right thing or not. He suspects old [[Earlian Dimmelwald|Earlian]] expected this to happen. He asks , so this doesn't happen again, whether we can try and keep an order to things. He calls this conversation 1, and wants us to tell him the next time we see him what the conversation number is.

When we ask how he accessed the gateway between planes, he reaches to his medallion and says that it's an [[Aurora'lyn]] artefact that has allowed him for his life to access the gateways. He bids us farewell and we go our separate ways.

We make our way out of the ruins and find a set of equipment and half filled barrel a short distance from the cliff edge we emerge onto. The beach curves into a cove where a [[Moon Church]] Cutter ship is anchored a short distance from shore. Four Moon priests in white and grey livery stand watch with shields on their back. From our cover, we can see the full scene, the ship empty while sole four figures. The rowboat we arrived on is floating off shore. The two figures near the supplies have shaved heads and are gaunt figures in robes. Their hands, painted silver, glow and pulse as chants quietly slip their lips. One on the beach with a polearm is thicker set with a sickle from his belt, and the last glows with uneasy silver light and shadow, his sticked livery barely concealing the studded leathers below.

[[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] wildshapes into a panther and stealths closer to them. The priests are talking, they use names and place names, they appear to be trying to prove their devotion is stronger than the others in their group. As they talk, the rest of the group attempt to move around the edge of the forest but [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]], not used to his lost limb, loses balance and falls out of a bush, locking eyes with one of the robed priests. They call out, and as they do Panther form [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] charges across the battlefield and attempts to shove the other robed figure off the cliff but isn't strong enough. The remaining priests join and a fight erupts. The priest with the polearm, referred to as the Tithemaster, climbs the cliff by digging the hooked into into the cliffside like a climbing axe and pulling himself to the top. The studded leather priest levitates up the distance, landing softly next to the Tithemaster. [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] shifts into his [[Xinderoth]] form as the battle starts and summons a shadow spirit, and eventually draws and strikes with Netherbane at one of the Moon Priests.

One of the priests, wearing what looks to be a rosary, reaches for the necklace and grasps it, dealing significant necrotic damage. The priest weird studded leather is referred to as a Relic Handler.

Lunar Rapture - DC 15 - 4d8 Radiant 4d8 Psychic - Charmed - Half on Success and no Charm effect.

Eventually, we manage to kill the four priest. The cutter is pitch black with a stark silver crescent and a low hatch to the cabin reveals the ghosts of moon priests that once were upon this boat; faint scents of incense linger in the space with articles of religious paraphernalia scatter the space. We rest for the night, and in the morning [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] takes to the helm and starts to give orders. With [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] navigating, [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] on the tiller, [[Arabella]] on lookout, and [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]] and [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] take control of the ropes. Unfortunately, [[Arabella]] misses seeing a Sand Bar in the Cutter's path and [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] doesn't react quickly enough as a rope is pulled tort and he's dragged into the water. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] grabs the rope, keeping [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] moving with the ship and attempts to hand it over to [[Victor Dimmelwald|Victor]].

We sail through the night, and eventually see New Town in the distance. Smoke curls skyward and the town has a visible construction site in action. This appears to be the canal that is being built south. There's a small natural harbour with a beach slightly further out from the town, where we make our way to harbour. Along a strip of shingle, black with algae, we pull the boat into the cove. The path to New Town is no more than a muddy cart trail. It's a short walk into town. Through the gates, the streets are muddy and crowded, buildings raw from the axe and shouts fill the air. Militia in mismatched armour walk the streets, trying to look stern, but most ignore them completely. A knot of onlookers have gather around a pair who are engaged in a heated engagement.

There are two major towns in the frontier, Lockborough and New Town. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]], who has been to New Town regularly before, knows vaguely where the auction house is, and leads us as we wander the streets. The Brass Cauldren, looking half warehouse half theatre, is made of pale timber and painted an Okra. A man outside appears to be attempting to peer through the gaps and windows. He appears well dressed but is wearing shoes that are not fit for the muddy area.

[[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] knows that the auction house sells artefacts that are brought back from the ruins in the north, which we know to be the [[Xinderoth]] ruins.

Six canal workers dead at their camp, poisoned, the whole town is saying it's Jerome Wipplebit. He seems suspicious of us, interrogating our motives and whether we are affiliated with the man. He claims Wipplebit has been against the canal project and has been rallying people against the canal workers. He introduces himself as Sir Jarreth Klein, of the Lockborough canal consortium. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] introduces himself, and Jarreth claims to have heard of him.

A gavel cracks against the block of the auction house as we enter. A Gnome, obviously Jerome, with a gravely voice moves onto his next lot, an iron mask from the ruins, is barking bids as a cacophony of voices discuss. The items are locked within a cabinet by him, with six items within.

The mask is eventually won by a strange looking lady sat next to her identical twin. One places bids while the other watches the room with sharp eyes. They win the bid for 55gp.

Jerome calls that three of the items have been sold, and three to go. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] recognises him as Malrick Dawn, his old business partner. There's a terse exchange. He warns us to leave [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] well enough alone, as his business ventures always end badly. He says to ask about the Lockborough Tannery incident. [[Brannick Coldrock|Brannick]] explains, a small quality issue with some of the products, leather gloves, items and other such items. The leather was poisoned and all fell apart. They sold to the workers cheaper to avoid the guilds costs. Malrick was in deeper with the guilds and bore the brunt of their poor reactions.

We intercept Dowthus as he makes his way to the back office and convince him to part with his tiny box on the condition that we open it together. [[Arabella]] asks him whether he knows of a Nairometer, his eyes light up and he says he knows of it and he claims he would be very interested in seeing one. He describes it as a psionic crystal lattice with a brass housing and dials.

Dowthus has been studying the planes for most of his life, and claims the theory has always been greatly interesting to him. He claims to have no magic, and [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] believes he seems to be telling the truth. The seller, a female anonymous party, has a 20gp minimum on the dagger.

We manage to lock pick the box, within which is a shard of amber which holds a butterfly. We part with it, giving it to Dowthus as a favour. In conversation he says he has a tower, and if we were to want to find him that he can be found in Gerna Tull's Tea Shop every afternoon.

[[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] describes the crystal latticed structure to Jerome, and he recognises the description. He leads us down to the basement, claiming he knows exactly the thing. He pulls out a contraption the size of a travelling chest. A single needle trembles gently on the front, while the other sits inert.

Jerome owns the item, it was sold to him, and as we're attempting to take it off him, two large men interrupt proceedings. They appear to be canal workers, mud caking them as they enter with knives drawn. They attack, but are quickly dealt with through incapacitation and suggestion. He ask about the canal project and why he hates it, and he claims it will rob the area of its Frontier nature.

The militia are supposedly under the Mayor's command, Valenthorn, but are fairly useless. The canal workers died last night. Jerome tells us that Yorik Dawnbinder, the canal manager, has had it out for him for a while.

The basement is mostly underground. At the very top of the walls are windows onto ground level with metal bars. As we leave the basement, there are two doors that have locks, and the main door is barred from the inside.

[[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] picks up the canal worker and carries him out of the basement, and we head towards the Brine House, a dockworkers tavern. The place smells of tar and beer, and faint mists seem to roll in from the lake. The harbour is a hum of activity, but a wirey middle aged man is behind the bar and a scrawny teen is running drinks to the tables.

[[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] picks up conversation with the teen on the canal, and enquires about the deaths of the canal workers. The teen claims the town is close to cracking, and when it does we shouldn't be near. He claims those that died, choked their own lungs out. [[Arabella]] and [[Karrik Sylvari, last of the Aurora'lyn|Karrik]] ponder a plant or poison that could cause that effect, but can't place anything that have that level of impact.

He also claims there are 14 camps between here and Lockborough, and that the only real blocker to the project is the amount of labour they can find. We ask where they died, and he claims it was at their camp. The consortium pays their meals, and that the Brine House had a contract with them before the consortium stopped that 6 months ago due to the tavern's pricing. The Moon Priests don't drink, and so don't come in the taverns, but the teen claims they keep to themselves and don't appear to be very missionary in nature. They appear to be much more established in Lockborough though and that might change when the canal finishes.
