The female figure, standing impossibly tall stretched to twice the height of a man, forms into form that almost follows the natural law, but her limbs elongated to an unnatural length seem to move in ways that feel they defy how the world pulls. Her features suggested and undefined; inferences of cheek bones, indentations where eyes should be, her skin rippling with an almost translucent nature. Across her form, fungal growths emerge that creep across the surface of her skin. As she moves, her form feels like it places itself in the space with a disconcerting jerkiness rather than move with any measure of natural mechanics, like reality is trying to pull her into reality but failing.

They walk towards us, and appear to be trying to understand what's happened. They appear aware of us, but aren't interested in us in the slightest. Each step is placed with quiet precision as they investigate. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] calls out, and strangely one says "They're attempting communication". One, appearing to be in charge, asks for information on our group. [[Kazak Blitzwater|Kazak]] and [[Arabella]], slightly annoyed, try to get some information from the group but they state that their identities are something confidential. They.
