# The Arcane Quarter (Mage District)
- A hub of magical learning, experimentation, and trade in enchanted goods.
- Features: Grand wizard towers, arcane universities, potion shops, alchemical labs, and leyline focal points.
- Residents: Wizards, alchemists, enchanters, and magical scholars.
- Security: Often protected by wards, golems, or magical sentinels.
- Unique Aspects: Strange magical phenomena, floating buildings, enchanted lanterns, and talking statues.
# The Mercantile District (Market Quarter)
- The bustling centre of trade, where merchants sell everything from mundane goods to exotic wares.
- Features: Bazaars, trade guild halls, artisan workshops, street performers, and food stalls.
- Residents: Merchants, artisans, traders, and thieves lurking in the crowd.
- Security: City watch patrols and private guards for wealthier merchants.
- Unique Aspects: Magic-infused trade (e.g., animated signs, enchanted bags), rare item dealers, and guild-controlled auctions.
# The Noble Quarter (High Ward)
- The domain of the aristocracy, filled with lavish estates and luxurious halls.
- Features: Opulent mansions, noble courts, high-end tailors, and private gardens.
- Residents: Nobles, diplomats, influential merchants, and elite bodyguards.
- Security: Well-armed private guards, magical wards, and elite city enforcers.
- Unique Aspects: Magical street lamps, self-cleaning streets, and enchanted jewellery stores.
# The Religious District (Temple Ward)
- A sacred area dedicated to the city's dominant faiths and spiritual practices.
- Features: Grand cathedrals, smaller shrines, monasteries, and clerical healing centres.
- Residents: Priests, monks, paladins, and devotees.
- Security: Holy knights, clerics, and divine protections.
- Unique Aspects: Ever-burning braziers, floating hymn scripts, statues that offer blessings.
# The Residential District (Commoner’s Ward)
- Home to the city's working-class citizens, including farmers, labourers, and craftsmen.
- Features: Modest homes, communal wells, bakeries, taverns, and schools.
- Residents: Farmers, blacksmiths, weavers, and city workers.
- Security: Occasional patrols, but less oversight than wealthier areas.
- Unique Aspects: Community-run magical wards, street charms for protection, hidden tunnels for escape.
# The Undercity (Slum District & Black Market)
- A shadowy and often lawless area where the city’s poor and criminals dwell.
- Features: Crumbling buildings, thieves’ hideouts, illicit magic vendors, and underground taverns.
- Residents: Beggars, outlaws, informants, and rogue mages.
- Security: Secret police, crime lords, and occasional city raids.
- Unique Aspects: Magical graffiti, cursed artefacts for sale, and concealed entrances to forgotten catacombs.
# The Industrial Quarter (Smithing & Artisans’ Ward)
- The city’s centre for craftsmanship, metalworking, and large-scale production.
- Features: Blacksmith forges, alchemical refineries, tanneries, and magical construct workshops.
- Residents: Blacksmiths, engineers, artificers, and industrial labourers.
- Security: Guild guards and fire-prevention mages.
- Unique Aspects: Self-heating forges, animated toolsets, and elementally-powered workshops.
# The Adventurers' District (Guild Ward)
- A gathering place for mercenaries, monster slayers, and treasure hunters.
- Features: Guild halls, bounty boards, weapon shops, and magical gear traders.
- Residents: Adventurers, mercenaries, cartographers, and informants.
- Security: Heavily armed residents; unofficial duelling culture.
- Unique Aspects: Talking bounty boards, enchanted training dummies, magically preserved trophies from past exploits.
# The Outer Ward (Agricultural & Trade Route District)
- The farmland and supply hub that keeps the city fed and connected to trade routes.
- Features: Grain mills, stables, merchant caravan stations, and supply depots.
- Residents: Farmers, stable masters, and traveling merchants.
- Security: City watch on patrol, druidic wardens, and occasional mercenaries.
- Unique Aspects: Magical irrigation, enchanted scarecrows, and self-moving carts.
# The Dockside Quarter (Harbour or Airship Port)
- If the city is coastal or has floating skyships, this would be the centre of naval or aerial trade.
- Features: Shipyards, fish markets, taverns, warehouses, and smuggling routes.
- Residents: Sailors, dockworkers, smugglers, and captains.
- Security: Harbor guards, naval patrols, and pirate hunters.
- Unique Aspects: Leviathan wards, anti-levitation runes, and animated cargo lifters.
