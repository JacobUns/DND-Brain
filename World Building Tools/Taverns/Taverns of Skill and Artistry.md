# The Anvil’s Lyre
**Type**: Crafting & Artificer’s Showcase  
**Theme**: Innovation, Artisanship, Magical Design  
**Recognition**: **Maker's Stud** – an expertly created and decorated bronze stud with the symbol of a Lyre stamped in its top awarded for excellence in creation. Usually attached to a collar or apron. Its design is magically created and so fine that it is impossible to duplicate.

**Description**:  
This forge-temple, filled with soft bellows and ember-light, invites all to present their masterworks on a stone dais. Echoing hammers fall quiet when a craftsperson speaks.

**How It Works**:  
Any artisan can submit a physical or magical creation for live demonstration and critique. Maker's Stud holders vote on quality, originality, and craft. Successful presenters are awarded the Nail and recorded in the Artisan Registry. Those creations submitted can also have their plans or designs added to the records for any to craft and sell with a percentage of the proceeds going back to the designer. An artisan can earn more than one stud by submitting multiple designs.

**Why It Matters**:  
Holders gain access to exclusive materials, commissions, and guilds. It’s a true mark of respect regardless of one’s background.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Gain **advantage on crafting checks** using tools related to your Stud-winning craft
- Can acquire rare crafting contracts and resources unavailable to the general public

**Game Hooks**:
- A Maker's Stud bearer is accused of stealing another’s blueprint—players must find the truth
- A lost Maker's Stud tied to a legendary inventor is found… and cursed
- A noble house wants a new invention to awe their rivals at the next Artisan Gala

# The Mirror Call
**Type**: Illusory Theatre & Drama Hall  
**Theme**: Illusion, Presence, Acting, and Deception  
**Recognition**: **Silver Mask** – a wearable mask symbolising elite performance

**Description**:  
A midnight-black amphitheater with shimmering floors and enchanted lighting. Speech echoes with magic, and illusion blends seamlessly with reality during performances.

**How It Works**:  
Actors, illusionists, and storytellers perform in disguise. Judges—also masked—vote anonymously after the show. If the performer can move them, a new **Silver Mask** is presented in ceremony.

**Why It Matters**:  
Silver Mask bearers are recruited as spies, diplomats, or court performers. Their reputations precede them—though their names often remain unknown.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Gain **advantage on Deception and Performance** while in disguise
- Recognised by espionage groups and theatrical circles alike, often opening secret doors

**Game Hooks**:
- A Silver Mask has gone rogue, using their reputation to manipulate a noble court
- A royal seeks an actor for a performance that is actually a covert message to allies
- A cursed mask is binding performers to their roles, even after the play ends

# The Scroll and Sickle
**Type**: Public Debate Forum  
**Theme**: Logic, Rhetoric, Philosophy, and Policy  
**Recognition**: **Iron Quill** – a stylised writing instrument worn as a lapel pin

**Description**:  
An open-air marble forum where voices rise and fall in public challenge. Rhetoric here changes lives: taxes, laws, even wars have turned based on debates held on its stones.

**How It Works**:  
Any may take the **Podium of Stone** to speak. To earn the Iron Quill, one must defeat a current holder in debate—judged by an assembled panel of peer Quills. No spells, no tricks—just words. To ensure the events are fair, an anti-magic field surrounds the debate hall.

**Why It Matters**:  
Holders of a Quill are viewed as wise thinkers, often consulted by councils, academics, and lawmakers. A single speech can elevate a farmer to the senate.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Gain **advantage on Persuasion and Insight** in formal argument or legal proceedings
- Can influence civic policy, local leadership, or redirect factional conflict

**Game Hooks**:
- A corrupt Quill is rigging debates through blackmail—exposing them risks public chaos
- A city-state’s succession will be decided by debate, not blood—players must prep a candidate
- A mysterious Quill challenges laws of magic itself—and powerful groups want them silenced

# The Gilded Verse
**Type**: Elite Literary Salon & Secret Confessional  
**Theme**: Poetry, Secrets, and Influence  
**Recognition**: **Whisperband** – A braided gold-and-ink band worn around the wrist or finger. It softly pulses when its wearer speaks in double meaning or poetic code. Only the truly insightful recognise its significance.

**Description**:  
A candlelit chamber hidden behind a great library. Patrons wear masks and speak only in verse or poetic metaphor. Every word spoken is magically recorded into the “Book of Breath.”

**How It Works**:  
Anonymous poets and thinkers write or speak before an audience of elite masked listeners. If a performance strikes true, the **Whisperband** is offered by unanimous secret vote.

**Why It Matters**:  
Holders of a Whisperband are often recruited by nobles, spies, and revolutionaries to communicate sub-textually—each is known as a master of veiled truth and evocative language.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Once per long rest, may **automatically decipher a coded message** or deliver one
- Gain access to exclusive political or spy networks operating beneath surface society 

**Game Hooks**:
- A killer is leaving stanzas from the Book of Breath at crime scenes
- A rival court poet is plagiarising a Whisperband’s work to gain influence
- A rebellion plans to hide their manifesto within a poem at the next Gilded Verse gathering
