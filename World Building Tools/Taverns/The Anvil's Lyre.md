# The Anvil’s Lyre
**Type**: Crafting & Artificer’s Showcase  
**Theme**: Innovation, Artisanship, Magical Design  
**Recognition**: **Maker's Stud** – an expertly created and decorated bronze stud with the symbol of a Lyre stamped in its top awarded for excellence in creation. Usually attached to a collar or apron. Its design is magically created and so fine that it is impossible to duplicate.

**Description**:  
Built into a former temple of the god of craftsmanship, this forge-temple is filled with bellows fanning ember-light, leather working benches and other artisan stations. Hammers echo in the hall and people move about their business, creating all manner of arcane and mundane trinkets, weapons and artefacts. The space is enormous; open enough to allow people to walk freely it still holds a reverence and intimacy once reserved only for prayer.

The room is split down the middle. In one half, a civilised tavern with good natured banter, games, laughter, and discussion of designs and innovation. In the other, craftspeople hard at work creating their latest and greatest masterwork. All, however, carefully avoid the elevated stone dais in the middle, its grand plinth welcoming the next artisan to test their skill and maybe receive a Maker's Stud.

The owner of the establishment, a gruff looking dwarf with fiery auburn hair and long beard called [[Bron Gritokk]], serves customers and engages in debate over designs with those that sit at his bar. His apron, with many bronze studs lining its entire edge, marks him as a master craftsman of utmost skill. He stands ready to judge any who believe their craft or invention stands a chance of being recognised for it's intricacy, delicacy and innovation. [[Bron Gritokk|Bron]] is an expert in many disciplines of artificery, metal working, blacksmithing, forging and silversmithing. Though he may not be an expert of all artisanal pursuits, he can certainly tell the craftsmanship required to achieve a design, and make a pretty penny from doing its sale.

The tavern is predominantly stone with marble inlay. It's arched and gothic ceilings hark back to its former religious nature, but the place is warmly lit and welcoming, and to some could be considered a temple still. Small clusters of candles provide table light while arcane globes float through the room at an almost imperceivable pace.

**How It Works**:  
Any artisan can submit a physical or magical creation for live demonstration and critique. Maker's Stud holders vote on quality, originality, and craft. Successful presenters are awarded the Nail and recorded in the Artisan Registry. Those creations submitted can also have their plans or designs added to the records for any to craft and sell with a percentage of the proceeds going back to the designer. An artisan can earn more than one stud by submitting multiple designs.

**Why It Matters**:  
Holders gain access to exclusive materials, commissions, and guilds. It’s a true mark of respect regardless of one’s background.

**In-Game Effects**:
- Gain **advantage on crafting checks** using tools related to your Stud-winning craft
- Can acquire rare crafting contracts and resources unavailable to the general public

**Game Hooks**:
- A Maker's Stud bearer is accused of stealing another’s blueprint—players must find the truth
- A lost Maker's Stud tied to a legendary inventor is found… and cursed
- A noble house wants a new invention to awe their rivals at the next Artisan Gala
