# About to Order
As light is pure, so too are the pursuits and virtues of the light domain. Believing themselves to be infused with divine radiance, they seek to dispel the darkness of lies pierce the veil of deception.

The deities of light are often associated with the ideals of:
- Truth
- Vigilance
- Beauty
- Insight
- Renewal.
Those who apply themselves to these virtues are seen as honourable and awe inspiring by some while others can see them as haughty, self righteous and arrogant due to their pursuit of what they deem to be truth.

Due to their ideals, the order may be associated with daylight, guidance, honour and morality as well as seeking truth.
