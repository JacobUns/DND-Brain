# About
Within a clerical domain, there will be differing levels of seniority that surmount to junior & senior members and their respective leadership.
## Initiates
Initiates are recently inducted members of the order that devote their time to the study of scripture. They assist with ceremonies, take small scale prayers, and learn from their higher ranking members to show the values of their deities. Initiates will be put through multiple tests of faith to prove their devotion to their deity before being admitted to the clerical order as an ordained member.
## Ordained Members
Members of an order who have shown the strength of their faith become ordained. They are trusted to lead others towards the path of their devotion. In the day-to-day life of a city, ordained members will be responsible for holding ceremonies, blessings, taking services, and preaching the scripture of their faith to bring new members to the congregation.
## High Clergy
Religious leaders of their faith, members of the high clergy are responsible for managing the disputes of kingdoms and their order to keep the peace. In times of religious war, these members will also lead crusades either in battle or from the organisation of supply lines. Often trusted figures within a city, members of the high clergy may also hold advisory positions within governments and monarchies, aiding their members in following the tenants of their belief.
# Structure
Each clerical order and domain have their own configurations of the ranks described above. The quantity of tiers and how they are organised depends on the but all will have the following:
- Acolytes
- Junior Ministers
- Senior Ministers
- Inquisitors
- Archivists
- Ordained Leadership
- Figurehead
