# About
The ancient dwarven city nestled in the mountains of the expanding [[Enlightened Empire]]. A short trip north from [[Lake Liash]] and it's floating town [[Liashton]], the path to Bal Toruhm can be found on the crossroad with [[Gomlodir]] and [[Thomyar]] and leads to a set of rocky switchbacks up the mountain path.

The city has two main entrances. A Western Gate that has not been opened in years, and is in an unknown state, and an Eastern Gate. Other, lesser known, entranceways are rumoured to exist, but none know where they may be. Those that know where to look can see ventilation shafts in the side of the mountains, but are near impossible to access through normal means. Those that have managed it report they are even harder to navigate and few that have tested their ability to navigate the labyrinth of vents have returned.

Once a sprawling metropolis, the formerly grand city now acts more as a tomb with complex winding tunnels. Aspects of its beautiful craftsmanship still remain to those that seek them out, but the years of constant warfare are plain to see to any that venture into its halls. Dwarf and goblin bodies in various states of decay lie strewn on almost every area of the city, and in many places orcs, trolls and arachnid corpses join them.

The city runs 40 miles across, its splendours still resent for those that dare to test their adventuring capabilities. The [[Vault of Tharbun]], holding the many splendours of the dwarven people within, is a tempting prospect for many. Some chasing wealth will join the thieves of the [[Hoarders]], a cut throat group who have plundered the treasures of the dwarves for many years. Scavengers and opportunists, the [[Hoarders]] have been sighted trading with goblins, benefiting from the slow genocide of the dwarven people that live there.
# Levels
Though 40 miles across, the city is on multiple levels. Areas known as the Deeps and Uppers provided the dwarves ample space to move and spread as their population grew, though most now hold only lurking perils. With four levels each in uppers and lowers, the metropolis can quickly become an inescapable maze to those that venture in too far unprepared.
