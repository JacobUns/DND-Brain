# About
![[Dor-Gairn City.png]]
Dor-Gairn, one of the free cities of [[Tarador]], stands as a monument its people and cultural history. A city that was once famed for its sale of gemstones from the neighbouring towns it supported, it is now known as a trade hub for all manner of fine materials. Positioned in a protected nook of natural red stone formations, the walled city Dor-Gairn has long stood due to its defensible positioning against both weather and siege, while providing easy access to mines and caverns.

Anyone that enters the city is assailed by all manner of sensory stimulation, from the smells of expensive spices and food cooking on street corners to the sounds of traders flogging their wears and children playing in natural pools of the city, men and women washing cloths in others. Vibrant and opulent colours decorate bazars and the adorned clothing of citizens.

Walking the bazars, smells of fresh meat mingle with the earthy smells of other produce imported by the caravans that regularly frequent the trade hub. Expensive materials, including [[Sinkarin silk]], raw and refined precious ores, and all manner of wares crafted from them can be found the deeper into the city you walk. At the city's centre is [[#The Sand's Jewel]]; a district of [[Guild Types#Silversmith|Silversmiths]] and [[Guild Types#Lapidary|Lapidarists]] that have made the area their home for generations.

Around Dor-Gairn, and its protective mountains, are arid plains with thick vegetation around naturally occurring pools. In one direction, the rough landscape softens and leads to the edge of the forest at the centre of [[Tarador]], the mountain region catching the weather patterns and keeping the land fertile. In the other, wide, open and dry landscape that has born the worst of the environment created by the mountains. Here roam hardy and predatory creatures who use the visibility of the plains to their advantage. The people of the area have learnt to stay within the fertile lands, building their defences for aerial threats, such as Roc, as well as land creatures that stalk the edges of the forest. These threats mean that work as security for trade caravans is always in demand, as merchants ferry their goods in and out of the city.

With the trade comes thievery, and with thievery comes law enforcement. Two law enforcement factions operate within the city, [[#The Sunfire Guard]] and [[#The Jade Sentinels]] 
# Factions
## [[The Sunfire Guard]]
The general law enforcement within the city, they stand guard over the city and ensure that order is maintained. In leather and bronze, they blend into the city as the silent vigil until needed. Responsible for the operation of regular patrols and defence of the city, only the eagle eyed will spot them keeping watch over much of the city.
## [[The Jade Sentinels]]
Elite guards specialised in personal protection and strategy, they stick out in the city like an oasis in a desert. Their vivid armour speaks of a force that is not scared to be seen, as, should their gaze fall upon you, the inevitability of their will is sure to be felt and their orders exacted. Though beautifully adorned, their armour is not ceremonial. Masterfully crafted for combat, they are provided elite training that puts any that cross their paths at a serious disadvantage. If seen in the city, they are to be avoided as it usually means serious dealings are underway.
## The Gilded Court
Though not a nobility, it would be foolish to dismiss the power and politics of the merchants in the city. Referred to as Merchant Princes or Princesses, for as long as Dor-Gairn has been a trade hub, the most influential traders and merchants formed an accord. Working together, they would ensure smooth operation of trade in and out of the city, agree prices, and settle disputes as a court of peers. Should you be found guilty, there would be no legal repercussion. However, you may suddenly find your place in the city become uncomfortable. Stalls would be terrorised, merchandise disappear or caravans raided or detained until an amends was made. In cases where the merchant refused they would either leave the city, losing out of huge sums of money in the process, or at times vanish altogether.

Grievances raised to [[The Sunfire Guard]] would go nowhere, with members of the Gilded Court also holding places on [[#The Sapphire Council]]. Nothing gets done in the city without the backing of the Gilded Court.
# Ruling Class
As with each of the free cities of [[Tarador]], Dor-Gairn operates on its own terms and structure as a city state. In its current structure, as a monarchy ruled by [[Sahira Veyshari|Grand Vizier Sahira Veyshari]], the throne is supported by an [[#The Sapphire Council|advisory council]] and government of [[#Viziers of Trade and Law|Viziers]]. They are responsible for the day-to-day governance in the city, as well as cultivation of relationships with the surrounding free cities and foreign governments. [[#Sunstone Magistrates|Magistrates]] appointed by the [[#Viziers of Trade and Law]] act as the first line of interaction with the general populous, whether it be in collecting taxes from the surrounding towns or redistribution of [[The Sunfire Guard]].
## Grand Vizier
**Name**: [[Sahira Veyshari]]
At the head of the city, on a throne of gold and jewels, sits the Grand Vizier. The supreme ruler of Dor-Gairn, overseeing all political, economic, and military affairs. Governing alongside their [[#Viziers of Trade and Law|Viziers]], ensuring that trade flourishes while maintaining stability and security.
Their responsibilities include:
- **Governance & Law** – Enacting policies, resolving disputes, and upholding justice as the city’s highest judge.
- **Trade & Economy** – Regulating tariffs, taxation, and major trade deals to prevent monopolisation.
- **Security & Military** – Commanding [[The Sunfire Guard]] and [[The Jade Sentinels]], managing city defences and internal threats.
- **Cultural & Religious Stewardship** – Supporting the arts, overseeing faith practices, and sanctioning festivals.
- **Political Balancing** – Maintaining power equilibrium between merchants, nobles, and underground factions like [[The Silent Ledger]].

The Grand Vizier is not a hereditary ruler - succession is determined through political manoeuvring, military strength, or economic influence, ensuring only the most capable rise to power. At the death of the pervious Grand Vizier, the standing council of Viziers vote for a replacement. This has, over the centuries, resulted in many political assassinations plots as the ambitious look to grasp at power.

[[Sahira Veyshari]] is a third-generation Grand Vizier, leading some to believe that the Veyshari family is attempting to turn the elective monarchy into a hereditary monarchy through back channel politics, bribery and blackmail. Although [[Sahira Veyshari|Sahira]] is an elegant and enigmatic ruler, there is also an undeniably ruthless intelligence behind her eyes.
## The Sapphire Council
A council of the wealthiest and most influential merchants, financiers, and trade magnates. They advise the Grand Vizier and influence policy but do not hold direct executive power unless granted it.
## Viziers of Trade and Law
The governing council of Dor-Gairn, the Viziers are delegated power by the [[#Grand Vizier]] to operate as ministers of her government. Each Vizier has designated responsibilities to ensure the city prospers to the best of its capability. The Viziers are appointed by the [[#Grand Vizier]], and can be replaced at a whim.
### Vizier of Coin
**Name**: [[Zahmir el-Rahn]]
**Responsibilities**: Oversees taxation, tariffs, and currency exchange. Ensures the city’s wealth remains secure.
### Vizier of Scales
**Name**: [[Mahtali Xicoyatl]]
**Responsibilities**: Regulates trade agreements, market fairness, and merchant disputes.
### Vizier of the Ledger
**Name**: [[Orrik Copperquill]]
**Responsibilities**: Keeps records of debts, transactions, and financial policies, working closely with banks and moneylenders.
### Vizier of Order
**Name**: [[Naima Ashkari]]
**Responsibilities**: The highest law enforcement officer, overseeing the [[#Sunfire Guard]] and ensuring justice is served.
### Vizier of the Sands
**Name**: [[Hazzin the Bronze-Hand]]
**Responsibilities**: Manages infrastructure, public works, and city expansion, ensuring roads and defences remain intact.
### Vizier of Tribute
**Name**: [[Narisa Velkaari]]

## Sunstone Magistrates
Below the Viziers, these magistrates are responsible for governing districts, enforcing city laws, and mediating disputes among merchants and citizens. They are often wealthy merchants or retired officials with strong local influence.
### Sunstone Magistrates
Govern individual districts of Dor-Gairn, ensuring trade, law, and taxation run smoothly.
### Amber Arbiters
Judges who preside over legal disputes, criminal cases, and trade conflicts.
# Locations
## Merchant's District
### Outer Markets
Around the outside of the market district, lean-tos and stalls bearing all manner of goods gather. Merchants trying to make a living flog their wares, calling to passers by. All manner of smells including dried fruits, cured meats and the fires of industry waft through the makeshift alleys. Children run half naked, their tanned skin muddy and dusty, weaving through the legs of those trying to sustain a living on the meagre foods. In places, shouts of [[The Sunfire Guard]] or victims of pickpockets making their way towards [[#The Sand's Jewel]] can be heard chasing adolescent thieves, their angry calls and curses causing patrons to jump as a flurry of movement passes.
### Inner Markets
Moving further inside the district, the ramshackle and disorganised stalls become established sellers with permanent installations and expert travelling merchants with fabulously coloured tents attached to the side of ornate caravans. Every year, the locations within the the Inner Markets, where purveyors of goods aren't able to pay their taxes, are auctioned off to the highest bidder by the [[#Vizier of the Ledger]]. 
### The Sand's Jewel
At the centre of the merchant's district is The Sand's Jewel, the Grand Bazaar of Dor-Gairn and home to the great markets filled to the brim with glistening stones, slabs and ingots of precious metals and finely crafted jewellery.

## Religious District
Like many cities in [[Tarador]], the varied peoples and cultures often require access to many different sanctuaries for worship. The religious district offers a combination of dedicated temples to larger faiths of the region as well as communal areas of worship for those less popular but just as respected.
### Temple of Dol Arrah
Dedicated to the goddess of Truth and Honour, this temple is the hub for the [[Order of Light|Order of Dol Arrah]] and one of the largest temples in the religious district of Dor-Gairn.

Built of white marble and ornately decorated with stained glass depictions of the matron of their order, its painted and mosaicked ceilings are considered by many who visit its halls to be an artistic marvel. As a visitor to the temple walks beneath its murals they will notice that they are not mundane artwork, but enchanted depictions of the tenants and doctrine that tell the story of her emergence. The moving depictions show her creation at the hands of [[Erador]] and [[Arawai]] and key events within the creation of the pantheon.

## Other Districts
### Industrial District
Filled with workshops, forges, artisanal and arcane studios that feed the [[#Merchant's District]] with high quality items, masterworks and magical creations. Though many of the works sold in the [[#Merchant's District]] are imported from all over the world by famed merchants, Dor-Gairn is also home to some phenomenal artisans and craftsmen. The district is broken up into several sectors:
#### Artisans Quarter
**Masterwork Guilds**: Home to renowned guilds that produce exquisite items sought after by many, from traders to nobles.
**Guild Halls**: Filled with the sound of hammers, chisels and looms, the workshops are collectives for craftsmen to come together and find work.
#### Enchanters Enclave
**Magical Workshops**: Enchanters and Alchemists create arcane works including enchanted items, potions and scrolls. Almost humming with energy, anyone walking the streets is hit with scents of a swath of exotic ingredients and feels the electricity of the arcane.
**Mage Towers**: A few tall spires where wizards conduct experiments and research dot the Industrial District. Their outsides are often impenetrable or marked with arcane runes.
#### Smithing Sector
**Forgers' Avenue**: A street within the district is lined with Blacksmiths and Silversmiths that create all manner of items from mundane tools to intricate weaponry, armour and jewellery.
**Ore Repositories**: With so much forging, the city also holds stores of raw and refined ores. From Iron to Platinum, the stores hold thousands of gold in materials and as a result are guarded by [[The Sunfire Guard]].
#### Glassworks
**Glassblowing Studios**: Artisans create beautiful glassware, from delicate ornaments to practical containers.
**Stained Glass Halls**: Specialising in creating intricate stained glass windows for temples, noble residences, and public buildings
#### Textile Mills
**Silk Weaving Houses**: Producing the prized silks, these mills are known for their exquisite fabrics and intricate patterns.
**Dyeing Workshops:** Using natural dyes to colour textiles, these workshops produce vibrant and lasting hues that are in high demand.
#### Stonecutters' Plaza
**Sculptors' Studios:** Artisans create statues, reliefs, and architectural details, adding beauty and grandeur to the city. Everything from the natural materials of the area to exquisite and exotic masonry can be found here and commissioned into works of art.
#### Alchemical Gardens
**Herb Gardens:** Cultivating rare and magical herbs used in potions and enchantments that feed the [[#Enchanters Enclave]]. Magical agricultural buildings rapidly grow herbs from all over the continents.
### Noble District - The Moonstone Terrace
Known as The Moonstone Terrace, the noble district is home to [[#Viziers of Trade and Law|Viziers]], [[#Sunstone Magistrates|Magistrates]] and the Royal Palace, the Noble District is a place of luxury in one of the richest cities in [[Tarador]]. Manors and Mansions line well paved streets, with buildings made of expensive stone imported from other continents. The Royal Palace is a grand building made of black and white marble and detailed with bands of gold with fist sized jewels fitted into the fixtures, its opulence a statement of the city's wealth and power to all that visit.
#### Guest Houses
Private gardens, grand halls, spas and other comforts of the wealthy dot the district with high class and expensive guest houses for visiting ambassadors, foreign dignitaries, rich merchants and those with the coin to spend.
**Guest Houses:**
- Zarina's Oasis
- The Vizier's Vineyard
- The Star and Crescent
#### The Sapphire Spire
The district is also the seat of [[#The Sapphire Council]], their quarters in a private hall referred to as the Sapphire Spire. A octagonal hall of stone beautifully decorated with lapis, sapphire and opal stands as a representation of the city's source of wealth. It's tower stands a hundred feet above the district, almost overlooking all that reside there. It is more than just a meeting place. It is a constant reminder to those in the mercantile industries of the power the council holds, location to settle disputes, forge alliances, and address the politics within [[#The Sand's Jewel]], as well as entertain dignitaries.
#### The Golden Minaret
A strategically smaller building than [[#The Sapphire Spire]], the Golden Minaret stands as a shining tower for [[#The Gilded Court]]'s operations. Being used as the court's location to hold their judgement of their peers, the building shows the power that its members hold and their established position within the city.
### Outer Ward
The Outer Ward of Dor-Gairn, a vital area for the city's sustenance, stands as a testament to the ingenuity and adaptability of its people. Nestled just outside the bustling heart of the city, this district is a lush oasis amidst the arid plains, fed by the natural pools and fertile lands created by the surrounding mountains.

As you step into The Outer Ward, the landscape transforms from the densely populated urban expanse to a serene and expansive agricultural haven. Fields of vibrant green stretch out as far as the eye can see, interspersed with patches of colorful flowers and exotic plants. The air here is filled with the earthy scent of tilled soil and the sweet fragrance of blooming flora.

Farmers, dressed in light, flowing garments, work diligently in their fields, their tanned skin glistening under the warm sun. The rhythmic sounds of hoes and rakes working the earth create a soothing symphony, accompanied by the distant laughter of children playing near the irrigation canals.

The Outer Ward is more than just an agricultural hub; it is a place where the cultural and historical tapestry of Dor-Gairn is woven into the very fabric of daily life. It stands as a testament to the resilience and resourcefulness of its people, ensuring the city's prosperity for generations to come.
#### Terraced Fields
These multi-tiered fields maximise arable land and manage water flow efficiently. Crops such as maize, beans, and squash thrive here, providing staple foods for the city.
#### Date Palm Groves
Rows of date palms line the edges of the fields, their tall, slender trunks reaching towards the sky. The sweet, rich dates are harvested and sold in the city's markets, prized for their nutritional value and delectable taste.
#### Orchards
Fruit trees, including pomegranates, figs, and citrus, add bursts of color and sweetness to the landscape. The orchards are meticulously cared for, ensuring a bountiful harvest that supports both local consumption and trade.
#### Irrigation Canals
Ingeniously designed irrigation systems channel water from the natural pools and underground springs, ensuring the fields remain fertile and well-watered. These canals are a marvel of engineering, reflecting the ingenuity of the city's inhabitants.
#### Pasturelands
Grazing areas for livestock, including goats, sheep, and camels, are interspersed throughout the ward. These animals provide milk, meat, and wool, essential resources for the city's sustenance and trade.
#### Shade Pavilions
Beautifully decorated pavilions offer shade and rest for farmers and visitors alike. Constructed in the traditional architectural styles of the region, these pavilions are adorned with intricate tile-work and colourful fabrics.

# Inspirations and Themes
Dor-Gairn pulls inspiration from Arabian, Meso-American and Persian themes. With imagery of Aztec warriors, multi-tiered architecture, Arabian styled names, and a people of tanned skin, improvisation on the content of this city should look to pull inspiration from stories such as Aladdin, Arabian Nights, Simbad, Prince of Persia and other such material.
