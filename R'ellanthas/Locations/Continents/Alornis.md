# About
The continent on the eastern side of [[R'Ellanthas]] and the locations of both the [[R'Ellanthas - The Dragon Stone Chronicles|Dragon Stone Chronicles]] and [[R'Ellanthas - A Fallen City Campaign|Fallen City]] campaigns. The [[Enlightened Empire]] is expanding, fighting against the [[Kingdom of Kalvanen]], and the Alliance of Vennar. The dwarves have also started construction of great mountain cities on Alornis.
