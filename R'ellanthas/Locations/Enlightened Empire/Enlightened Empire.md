# About
The Human Empire that is growing in the East. It's Emperor is simply know as [[the Lightbringer]], supposedly he is a living God. The Empire insists on all people following [[The Path of Light]]. Saying that the Emperor delivered the people from the darkness of the elves and carried them into the new age of light.

The Empire sends out it's priests, clerics and paladins into the wider world to help spread [[The Path of Light]]. They tell stories of the good work the empire does, partly to make it easier when the time for conquering comes.

The Empire was founded by humans but embraces any who follow [[The Path of Light]]. Their symbol is a Tower with a sun at the top emitting light all around.
