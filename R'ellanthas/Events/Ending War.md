# About
The devastating war between the [[Draconomicon|Dragons]] and the Elves that would ultimately see the end of both races, the Ending War is remembered still through the teachings of [[The Great Council]] and through the existence of [[The Pact of Il'Mondum]], along with the forces of the [[Guardians of Il'Mondum]] and [[Arcandor Academy]], whose presence is ever felt by the peoples of [[R'Ellanthas]]. ***800 years on***, not an elf or a [[Draconomicon|dragon]] seen since that final battle, several factions find themselves fighting for control over land, artefacts and technology.
# History
For near 7000 years the Elves ruled the lands of [[R'Ellanthas]]. They lorded above all others in mighty cities with towers that touched the sky having taken control of [[R'Ellanthas]] from the then ruling [[Draconomicon|Dragons]].

The [[Draconomicon|dragons]] grew tired of their enslavement to the Elven Empire, and having waited patiently for several hundred years while their plans for rebellion were formed and communicated among dragonkin, they would finally make their move.

When the day finally arrived, every [[Draconomicon|dragon]] across the world rose up, breaking free of the chains, cages, or hiding places, and a massacre ensued. Thousands of elves died in the first hours of the uprising with cities left burned and ruined. The [[Draconomicon|dragons]] were careful not to kill anyone but the elves, and the other races were allowed to flee, many living in shanty towns away from the elven cities centres.

The elves recovered quickly and soon all out war replaced the one-sided assaults that had been the opening days. Before long, the war came to one of attrition with sieges taking decades to resolve. It was in this time that the elves secretly constructed towering colossi walking machines, piloted by several elves, which carried fearsome arcane weaponry capable of dealing lasting wounds to the [[Draconomicon|dragons]]. These constructs were meant to be the saviour of the elves but instead led to more destruction of the elven way of life, their own cities being destroyed in the crossfire between construct and wyrm.

After 53 years of war the numbers on both sides were dwindling and a final and definitive battle took place. The last remaining colossus walked out, with the remaining elven armies, to face the last [[Draconomicon|dragons]]. This final battle shook the ground and burned the earth to black. The sky filled with ash, one last cataclysmic explosion ripped through the land and silence followed. The elves and [[Draconomicon|dragons]] had all been killed, not a survivor on either side.

Out of the ruins of the world, those that had once been ruled worked quickly to fill the power void left behind. Kings were crowned, Empires were formed and the world has settled into a new rhythm.
