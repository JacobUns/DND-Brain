# About
The Pact of Il'Mondum is the accord that was created and signed by the leaders of all kingdoms, empires and governments of [[R'ellanthas]] following the culmination of the [[Ending War]].

It sets out the rights of every race, ensuring that all were equal and have equal voice in the world so that nothing like the domination of the Elves can happen again. The pact created two organisations, the [[Guardians of Il'Mondum]] and the [[Arcandor Academy]]. These organisations would be given special privileges to ensure that no government or peoples would be corrupted by artefacts of old or control too many of the magically gifted.
