# About
A feared thieves guild by those in the underground, its operation is not one of brute strength, but instead a clandestine organisation that controls one thing more valuable than gold - secrets. Their influence spreads like an unseen hand, ensnaring nobles, merchants and officials in a web of blackmail and financial manipulation. Unlike common groups of thugs, The Silent Ledger operates with precision, turning whispered confessions and debts into weapons more effective than any blade.

At the helm of the organisation is [[Veylen Kaelthorne|Veylen]], a former mercenary whose strategic brilliance and cold pragmatism have built The Silent Ledger into an empire of wealth and control. He does not act on impulse - every move he makes is calculated, every debt he collects serves a purpose. Those who cross him soon find themselves stripped of their fortune, their power, and often their lives. Those who serve him well, however, are rewarded beyond measure - so long as they remain useful.

The Silent Ledger functions as an elite order of financial predators, trading in blackmail, extortion, and political leverage. Their agents are found in every major city, embedded among the powerful, ensuring that no deal is made, no crime committed, and no fortune earned by their marks without their knowledge - or their cut.

To defy The Silent Ledger is not to invite open war, but to find one's wealth drained, allies turned against them, and darkest sins revealed. By the time a victim realises they were ever in danger, it is already too late.
# Guild Roles
Each of these positions plays a crucial role in maintaining The Silent Ledger’s dominance. The majority of their power does not come from open violence, but from the invisible chains they place around every ruler, merchant, and lawkeeper. Where violence is necessary, however, they are not afraid to brandish steel.
## Guild Boss - The Ledgermaster
The absolute ruler of The Silent Ledger, overseeing all major operations. The Ledgermaster dictates strategy, approves high-level blackmail and extortion schemes, and ensures the guild’s continued influence. His word is final, and failure is not tolerated.
## Master of Coin
Responsible for handling the vast wealth of The Silent Ledger, managing bribes, laundering money, and overseeing the movement of illicit goods. The Master of Coins ensures that wealth flows efficiently while maintaining legitimate fronts for the guild’s activities.
## Master of Secrets
The most feared figure in the guild after the Ledgermaster, the Master of Secrets oversees an expansive collection of blackmail, debts, and hidden truths. Their agents gather information on nobles, merchants, and rival organisations, ensuring that no one can act against the guild without dire consequences.
## Inkwraiths
Masters of deception, forgery, and infiltration, the Inkwraiths are the guild’s unseen hands. They doctor ledgers, forge documents, and ensure that information flows in the guild’s favour. Some serve as diplomats, brokers, or couriers, embedding themselves deep within the circles of the elite.
## Debt Collectors
Unlike brutish thugs, Debt Collectors for the guild are refined, patient, and terrifyingly efficient. Their task is to ensure that all debts - whether financial, political, or personal - are repaid. They operate as both negotiators and executioners, reminding those who owe the guild that no debt goes unpaid.
## Ledgerhands
A collective of accountants, cryptographers, and mathematicians who track wealth, investments, and blackmail assets. They ensure that every secret and financial transaction is recorded and used to its fullest potential.
## Veiled Caravans
A network of merchants, transporters, and smugglers who maintain The Silent Ledger’s trade routes. They disguise the movement of stolen goods, illicit funds, and valuable artefacts, ensuring that nothing is ever traced back to the guild.
## Enforcers
An elite faction within the guild responsible for ensuring loyalty. They eliminate traitors, investigate potential leaks, and maintain internal discipline. If a member of The Silent Ledger vanishes, it is the Enforcers who made it so.
