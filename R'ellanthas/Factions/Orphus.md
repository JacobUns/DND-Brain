# About
Orphus are a rumoured organisation that actively goes against [[The Pact of Il'Mondum|The Pact]], seeking artefacts for the organisation's benefit. Agents of Orphus often seek to disrupt the goings on of the other organisations and powers around the world. What their ultimate agenda is, who works for them and who is in charge are all unknowns. OAR and [[Arcandor Academy]] tend to dismiss reports of Orphus as myth and fantasy tales.
# Encounters
An organisation who have an interest in the "best interests of the kingdom". Their agent [[Dohko Nog]] followed us from [[Kalvanen#Xuan's Books|Xuan's Books]].
# Iconography
The symbol used as the organisation's emblem is two half moons intertwined.
