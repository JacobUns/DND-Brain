# About
Formed as part of [[The Pact of Il'Mondum]], the Arcandor Academy are responsible for gathering and training all magic users born to the world. Their position, granted by [[The Great Council]], was created to ensure that no single political power in the world had access to powerful magic that would allow them to subvert the other races.

The Academy takes all magic users into their tutelage as soon as they are identified. If not given up willingly, they are taken forcefully by the faction with none able to deny the Academy taking those they deem necessary of training. Those attempt to refuse are dealt with in a ways that ensure this offence is not repeated.

Once in the Academy, children are taught how to harness their powers and given a more standard education. Upon coming of age the mages are distributed to various leaders around the world to provide advice and assistance to rule as required.

Those not chosen to serve in court are sent to various research projects, helping to improve the lives of all, or to answer question of mystery and agendas for the academy. Others gifted with a more martial magic are often made into agents of the ARA, although the Academy does keep a certain amount of these mages around for it's own protection.
