# About
The Great Council is a conclave of representatives from each major empire, kingdom and city of [[R'Ellanthas]]. A gathering of those once subservient to the elves who are now responsible for the politics of the world and to ensure a dominance such as the elves never happens again.

Being a representative on the Great Council is an honour unmatched and their members carry absolute authority wherever they go, reporting to the leaders of each major faction.

After the [[Ending War]], the leaders of the subservient races came together and created a document which would be from thereon known as [[The Pact of Il'Mondum]]. Signed by representatives of all races, barring the dwarves, it laid out the rights of each race and society, current and future, to be equal and have equal voice within the world. Two organisations were created to act freely and independently of all other governments to maintain [[The Pact of Il'Mondum|The Pact]]; the [[Guardians of Il'Mondum]] and the [[Arcandor Academy]], the former to prevent artefacts of old corrupting the people and protect [[the Great Council]] and the latter to bring the mages of the world under one banner and out of the control of any one king, emperor or government.
