# About
While not as specialised or elite as [[the Jade Sentinels]], the Sunfire Guard are a highly respected and well-trained force in urban combat, crowd control, and defensive tactics, making them the backbone of [[Dor-Gairn]]’s security.
# Appearance & Attire
- They wear bronze and leather armour, reinforced with sunstone inlays, providing a balance of protection and mobility.
- A sand-coloured cloak drapes over their shoulders, blending with the city’s architecture and terrain.
- A deep crimson or gold sash is worn to signify rank and authority.
- Their insignia, a golden sunburst over a gemstone, is displayed on their shields, armour, and banners.
![[Sunfire Armour.png]]
# Arms & Equipment
- They wield scimitars and spears, their primary weapons for maintaining control in the city streets.
- Patrols stationed at city gates or along trade routes often carry crossbows for ranged defence.
- Some guards are issued round shields, featuring the Sunfire Guard’s emblem, for added protection.
# Responsibilities
- **City Patrols** – Maintaining peace in the bustling bazaars, streets, and trade hubs.
- **Trade Protection** – Guarding merchant caravans, ensuring taxes and tariffs are properly collected.
- **Crime Prevention & Investigation** – Resolving disputes, deterring theft, and investigating crimes within the city.
- **Riot Control & Defense** – Handling civil unrest, forming defensive lines, and securing key locations in times of crisis.
- **Law Enforcement** – Upholding the decrees of [[Dor-Gairn]]’s rulers and ensuring justice is served.
