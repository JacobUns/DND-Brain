# About
The sentinels are a highly trained, prestigious force within [[Dor-Gairn]], tasked with protecting the elite, securing high-value assets in transit, and responding to major threats. Their small numbers are compensated by their exceptional skill, discipline, and high-quality equipment, making them one of the most feared and respected forces in [[Dor-Gairn]].
# Appearance & Attire
- They wear ornate lamellar armour, reinforced with jade-inlaid plates, offering both protection and prestige.
- A silk-lined green cloak drapes over their shoulders, signifying their elite status.
- Their helmets are adorned with stylised engravings and a crest of green-dyed horsehair, silk, or feathers, distinguishing them from lower-ranked guards.
- Their insignia, a jade talon clutching a sunstone, is displayed prominently on their armour and cloak fastenings.
# Arms & Equipment
- They wield masterfully crafted weaponry including scimitars, daggers and glaives, their hilts, handles and grips are inlaid with jade and gold filigree.
- They carry composite bows, designed for both precision and power, allowing them to engage threats at range.
- Some carry ceremonial jade-adorned spears, used both for combat and formal escorts of dignitaries.
- Their belts are fitted with short curved daggers, a final line of defence in close encounters.
- During ceremonial events, they carry brutal jade toothed blades with Couatl inspired armour.

# Responsibilities
- **Personal Protection** – Guarding nobles, high-ranking merchants, and other figures of great importance.
- **Asset Security** – Escorting high-value trade goods, precious gemstones, and royal treasury shipments.
- **Crisis Response** – Dealing with high-profile threats, including assassins, rogue mercenaries, and major crime syndicates.
- **Elite Combat Deployment** – When [[Dor-Gairn]] faces a direct military threat, the Jade Sentinels serve as **shock troops**, using their superior training to turn the tide of battle.
