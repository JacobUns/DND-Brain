# About
Located in the north east of [[Alornis]], The Alliance is a hardy government of mixed races made up of city states. Having always remained separate to the [[Kingdom of Kalvanen]], they are quite neutral in their dealings with the rest of the world. With less disparity between the rich and poor, the people ruled by The Alliance have a better general quality of life than many on [[R'Ellanthas]].

With their borders running along the edge of northern mountain ranges housing the fallen dwarven city of [[Bal Toruhm]], the Alliance have been seen starting to push into the mountains and dealing with those that would try to harm their people.
