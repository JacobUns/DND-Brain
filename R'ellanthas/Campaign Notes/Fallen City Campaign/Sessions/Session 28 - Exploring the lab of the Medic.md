Two goblins, an orc and a dwarf lie on the tables as part of the Medic's experiments. Each have gone beyond the point of saving, and so we put them out of their misery. Kayn finds ramblings of the Medic's experiments which he refers to "Elf Crystals". In the texts there are descriptions and notes that instruct individuals to locate items and people. They are specifically addressed to [[Varrox]], but are all written in the same handwriting. Kayn comes across a journal that refers to the Piper and having visited him in [[Nostar]]. Piper taught him the ways of the experiments, but he perfected it on goblin and orc forms. He refers to the his castle being larger than that of Piper.

As we wander around the castle, it seems fairly deserted. As we walk through the space, Frontistis believes that the reason for this is likely that those in the area are fearful of it given its alignment with the Medic's research.

As we wander, we find a feasting hall with a table fit for 50 dwarves at least. Sleeping quarters are dotted around the main building before the tower, suitable for approximately 10 guests. As we reach the tower, we find flora has taken over some of the area as if the space had become a plant nursery. They once would have sprawled the walls, but have subsequently died through lack of tending. Frontistis and Maria investigate a bit and believe that some of these are likely deadly variants and may have been the precursor for the flora creature we fought before entering the greenhouse. They were likely imported from all over the continent and different genus grown to cultivate the right strain.

We make our way through the tower, open rooms spiralling through the centre, until we reach a room at the top. In the centre of the room, is a large throne. As we walk around it, we find the remains of a dwarvish figure upon the stone throne. Spending time investigating, Kayn comes to believe it is [[King Beruck III]]. Inscription says "here rests King Beruck the Third, who so loved this garden and will watch over it forever." Around the kings neck is a pendant with a sapphire eye in the centre. Identify it, we find it is "Beruck's Eye".

Beruck's Eye:
Gives the bearer adv on perception checks and allows them to view locations they have seen before within [[Bal Toruhm]] once a day.

From the edge of the tower, we can see across the full garden and caves. Looking down, we can see the tunnel just off the main path. From here we can tell its well trodden and leads to the edge of the pond. Within the pond, fountains shoot stagnant water while statues covered in algae stand silent over the pool. It appears tracks surround the pond, but go no further. Frontistis notices within the pond fish swim still, and we theorise that goblins have been coming to find food. The river does continue into the pond, slightly renewing the water within, but it is otherwise stagnant.

Following the trickle of water from above leads to a gap that we believe was once an open air tunnel to the surface which has collapsed from the calamity. The light on the wall near us feels like sunlight, but we noticed over night that it changed to be more moonlight in warmth, and then subsequently shifted back to sunlight, which we deduce to be a day/night cycle.

We decide to head up the goblin tunnel. Approaching the pond, Kayn looks into the water and finds the fish are actually 2-3 feet long at times.
