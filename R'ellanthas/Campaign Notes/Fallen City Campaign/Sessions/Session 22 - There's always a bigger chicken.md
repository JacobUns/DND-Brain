We hide, preparing for what approaches and see two giant lizard-like creatures. On the first's back is a horde of orcs which we engage quickly after spotting them. The second carries an Orc carrying a great mace which we engage shortly after felling the first beast. We confer with [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] and he calls the creature a Scyza.

After searching the bodies, we find 37gp and a piece of amber worth 100gp.

Long Rest in a location that [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] points out to us as [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] has taken substantial damage.

[[Alliance of Vennar]] are North East of the continent of [[Alornis]]. Dwarves had been reliable trade partners for many years, but are less considered "less reliable" now. They deal a fair amount with [[Nostar]] which is separated from the bulk of the [[kingdom of kalvanen]] by the [[enlightened empire]]. They ventured into the mountain when they recognised there was only a small amount of time before the dwarves were completely wiped out. The knights are in the early parts of their expedition to survey the damage, and have made a fairly substantial foothold in the western entrance of [[Bal Toruhm]]. They intend to see if they can win by strength of arms, or whether they would need to permanently destroy the whole city, collapsing the mountain. They noticed the explosion of the mountain as we did, but have no idea what the cause would be. The mountain isn't an active volcano, isn't on a fault line, and doesn't appear to have been a natural occurrence.

The [[Knights of Vennir]] are a fresh fighting force, and believe they are a far superior fighting force. [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] believes that the Knights don't care about the dwarves at all, and that their intent to collapse the mines and city proves as such.

Days travel to reach the King's Hall still; [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] plans to let the king determine whether he stays or goes with us. [[Thion, Knight of Vennir|Thion]] is in a similar mind, with the hostility of the king determining what path he takes.

Mages within the Knights that may know of a power that would be capable of destroying the mountain as we witnessed. [[Thion, Knight of Vennir|Thion]] would be willing to introduce us to pay back our saving his life. He is unaware whether a request for aid was made by the dwarves initially, with the expectation that they just wanted to protect their own boarders, whereas [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] claims emissaries were sent, but they all returned with promises for help that never surfaced or flat refusals; none ever came to fruition.
