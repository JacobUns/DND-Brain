[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] leads off with the party in toe, and pulls [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] up to walk alongside him. She attempts to persuade him to help, but he ends up making fun of her inability to guide them. After half a day of wandering, something catches [[Thion, Knight of Vennir|Thion]]'s eye. He spots something hiding in an alcove. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] casts dancing lights and starts to make it spin, calling out for whatever is there to come out. After nothing happens, he causes the lights to spin faster and approaches stealthily.

There, he sees a large grey wolf with yellow eyes carrying a form with long white hair and yellow eyes. The being on her back introduces itself as [[Frontistis Zoon]], and claims to have been hunted by orcs for a long time. We eventually agree that strength in numbers is preferable and continue together.

As we walk, we see strange lingering smoke. Frontistis pays attention and finds there isn't a scent. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] recognises it faintly as a gaseous form spell, and motions to the group to stay low and draw weapons. We continue on, attempting to avoid being noticed, and keep low as we move.

The smoke waits for us to have moved on. Then, as it appears to , then starts floating behind us keeping pace. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] quickly whips around and fires off a firebolt centre mass. Suddenly, the smoke roils and curls down and rushes us.

We stroll on for another hour, and begin to hear the sounds of drumming in the deep. We start running and emerge onto a balcony, and the corridor gives way to a massive cavern with dim light. Below, in the faint light of fire and torches, we see a beautiful palace building to the left. To the right, an enormous thoroughfare fit for an army and lined with columns leads to its gate. Down it's main strip, the sound of drumming continues, bobbing firelight in the hands of goblin and orc smattering and giving the sense of a large force.

The palace is set back into the mountain wall. It's grand entrance is a recessed door, 40ft high, solid gold. There are significant signs of battle, bodies scattering the ground, the majority with the ugly visages of goblins and orcs.

A staircase heads down off the left of the balcony, and after a short discussion we head towards a well hidden passageway that [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] leads us to. We find an architecturally hidden corridor that leads to a deadend. [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] says a few words of dwarvish and a silvery door outline appears that we push through. We find another a shortway past and he says a different set of words and it opens ahead of us.

A chamber with a marbled floor, seamlessly transitioning into columns that tower 50-60ft above us. Dwarves run in all directions, but standing above them all is an older dwarf in full plate armour bellowing instructions. An older dwarf, his long white beard coming to mid stomach, [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] turns to us and informs us that this is [[King Barmus Beruck]]. [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] shouts something in dwarvish to the king, and we notice the king turn to us. His left arm is hanging, seemingly injured, by his side. He seems fairly wounded and that he's been in a significant battle recently.

[[King Barmus Beruck|The King]] approaches and greets [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] warmly, grasping arms and headbutting. [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] introduces us, saying that we're becoming close to being considered his friends. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], Frontistis and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] all bow to the king, but [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks if we can move on with proceedings given the approaching army.

They have had to hold here for quite some time. There was a number of battles ongoing that have driven them back into this position. As we look around the area, we find that there are approximately 100 dwarves rushing between posts attempting to sure up the door.

During discussion with the king, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] mentions the dropping of the mountain peak due to some kind of explosion, and the King shows some kind of recognition. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] suspects he might have an inkling of something further and says as much. The king confirms he might know of something, but he shows shock and sadness at the scale of the impact to the mountain. During conversation, [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] attempts to get [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] to give up the hammer, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] refuses without directly engaging on the topic. He spills 100lbs of Mithril onto the ground and uses Fabricate to create a gift of an approximation of the king's hammer, claiming he's seen depictions he can replicate from.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] get into a heated argument after the king moves off with [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]], and the party slink off in different directions, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] positioning himself as secluded as he possibly can, while [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] slumps to the ground in a fit or rage, casts of gust going off subconsciously with her frustration. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] heads off to the medical area to assist tending the injured dwarves.
