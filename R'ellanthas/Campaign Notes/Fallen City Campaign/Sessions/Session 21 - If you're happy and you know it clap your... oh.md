[[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] agrees to help us get to the King. About 300 dwarves remain with the King. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] asks [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] whether he knows a dwarf named [[Tassora]]. We head towards the King's Hall again, [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] in toe. As we walk, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] postulate on the nature of the relationships between goblin, orc and hobgoblin, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] suggesting they may be like a hive ecosystem.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] connects with the Mountain's leylines and senses through the mountain to find the direction of the King's Hall. We head into a larger hallway, dwarven runes above it, and [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] points out that it was once where the road came out before they collapsed the tunnel. Many houses layered in tiers around the edges of the cavern, looking aged with time and [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] explains that it is the origin point of the city, the west being the newly developed area as the mountain became more full. Towards the upper reaches are some flickering torch-like fires. Nyx flies off in the direction of the flickering, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] talks to [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] about what would be causing it. He explains that it's unlikely to be dwarves as they wouldn't draw such attention to themselves, but it things do camp.

With a fire in one of the buildings, Nyx finds a large campfire with orcs skulking around the edge. Some intensely stare into the darkness around, some with strange spyglasses of some kind. There are about 10 orcs, some around the fire, some in the building sleeping, and they appear to have made the area into a watchpoint overlooking the vast area.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts fly on [[Thion, Knight of Vennir|Thion]], [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] while [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] jump on the Broom of Flying. We get within 30ft of the orcs, when one happens to glance in our direction and notices us.

100gp - 20gp are reforged with an orc face
2x Looking Glasses - Allow us to see a long way in the dark. ~240ft. Zoomed perspective

We fell the orcs, flying in from above and attempting to surprise them but being found as we do. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] drops from the broom onto the chest of one of the orcs, sword driving through. A quick battle ensues and one orc tries to make a break to light a brazier. We wipe the lookout post out and discover two looking glasses that allow us to see another lookout post in the distance with three orcs.

We continue on our way stealthily. On the way, [[Thion, Knight of Vennir|Thion]] notices a lightsource coming from below in one of the chasms. Using the spyglass, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] sees a significant number of orcs and goblins that seem to have made their village. On the outskirts, two gargantuan lizards with short arms (T-Rex). Naturally penned into the rocky outskirts, one with a howdah on its back which seems to allow orcs to ride on its back.

We continue onward, and hear some large thumping footfalls. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] confer that they think they've heard footsteps, but [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] corrects them that there seem to be two creatures making that noise. It's coming out of a side tunnel and approaching. We discuss quickly, and decide to setup an ambush.
