Tormir leads us off, down one of the routes which [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to pick up signs of movement of something along this path. We continue down, Thion at the back of the party and Tormir leading. More mineshafts and walls glistening with refracted gemstone light and veins of metals. Tormir stops and says he doesn't recognise the area, as if it's newly made. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] looks into the distance, and sees some of the dwarven architecture looks modified in some way, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] assumes that it might be goblinoid.

Nyx flies over the bridge and spots something 3/4 of the way over being wrong in the stone. It appears to have a break in the bridge's rock. As she crosses she finds an archway and passes through to find the same architecture as [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] starts a very stealthy approach and reaching the crack finds that it has been design to fail over a 6-7 feet distance.

Returning and explaining to the group, we decide to attempt jumping the gap. Some back and forth later, Thion is volunteered by Tormir to try jumping first. He runs and jumps and just about clears the gap, breathing a sigh of relief. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] clears it easily, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] steps up next. She marginally steps over the line but her light-footedness isn't enough to cause it to fail. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] closes his eyes and runs, jumping the gap, and landing clear. We keep on, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] up front scouting for traps.

[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] starts conversation with Tormir about how she might become the Roadwarden and the two start talking years of age. Tormir claims to be 211 years old, whereas [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] is late teens. Tormir claims that Burruk the First lived to nearly 600.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] finds slots in the wall that indicate danger. Attempting to find the source of a trigger he fails, but asks [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] to scout with the end of her pike. As she places her pike down some distance in, the panels of the wall slam together. Using the tip of his rapier, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] pops out the trigger and disarms the trigger plates. We move through the space and under the arch to find three paths.
