[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] communes with the gods while at the [[Hall of the Road Warden]] to ask if there is anything hidden there. They come back with a Woe response, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] accepts the fact that the empty hall really is just empty.

The party head out of the hall and, once again, [[Mite]] goes in the wrong direction. After a brief admonition from [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and Mite head to the front to lead. Nix is sent ahead and keeps an eye out for any dangers. After an hour of travel, Nix alerts that there is a large lizard like creature decimating a group of goblins. Mite dashes off in the direction of his comrades and the group follows.

A 12-legged lizard creature, called a Behir, blue scales glinting appears to be cornered. Eight goblins that seem stronger than the minions we've fought before are attempting to keep the large creature at bay. The creature lets out a gout of fire, incinerating two goblins instantly. Mite gets caught in a fireball from [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and incinerates immediately as well as the majority of the rest of the goblins. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], invisible from stealthing up the tunnel, is also caught in the explosion. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] calls out as Mite falls, and then fires off a sorcerers burst at the Behir. It responds by eviscerating one of the remaining goblins, and the final remaining goblin in a blood rage critically strikes. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] jumping through flames attempts to pierce the creature's maw but critically fails and is dragged up, before throwing a second thrust into its stomach, divine energy flashing. Bursts of light continue to fire from [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]'s staff, and the goblin is eventually felled. In a final massive burst of magic, [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] fells the Behir.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] settles next to the Behir and skins it before the group goes to move on. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] looks around for any sign of where the creature may have come from. Without seeing anything, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] voices her frustration and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] join in the search. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] is not able to see anything, but [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages find a crack that may have been where it came from. Sending Nix hopping 5ft at a time, she starts exploring the area while we continue down the path. She finds the path drastically slopes before finding another dwarven tunnel split in two. It appears to likely be in one of the deeps.

The party continue down the path. Half an hour later, Nix, resummoned near us, flies ahead and sees a 40-50ft jagged crack in the corridor and runs up the side of one of the walls revealing a chasm below.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] voices a concern that we don't seem to know much about what the cause of this war is, and that there appears to be something behind the scenes pulling the strings. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] understand the concerns, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] voices wanting to find the source of the goblins to be able to wipe them out. With the question mostly unanswered, we continue onward. The chasm has a very small ledge, less than a foot wide, along one side where the crack hasn't quite reached the full width.

We send Nix down the chasm and it takes her more than 40mins to return to us. The party decide to attempt to get across the chasm with enhance ability from [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] safely make it across, but [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] makes it half way before slipping and plunging into the darkness. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] reacts quickly with feather fall and then levitates [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] back up to the edge. With a force of effort to punt across the remaining distance with her pike, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] arrives at the edge with the others. Ready to move on, the group take a breather before looking further into the darkness ahead.
