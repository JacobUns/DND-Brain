We wake, and feel like we've been going in the same general direction we were previously. Stealthily, we leave the mausoleum and pass the mine elevators again. We reach a fork, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] believes we would have arrived at the left path if we'd followed the previous paths. Going right, we again find many crossing bridges in all different directions. We investigate again for signs of a path, and having become so used to it quickly find a path. An hour passes, but without our experience it could have been a lot longer. Smaller mining platforms dot the area and machinery that look unreliable and unsafe to non-dwarvish eyes scatter the area.

We find a large chamber at the end of a staircase. Stagnant water covers the floor, and off centre is an island with a large stone throne atop it. Above us, a shattered aqueduct pathetically drips from what would previously have a flowing stream. Descending the steps, the water gently laps at the bottom step. A raft, in a loose sense, of goblin make with a blunt piece of wood for an oar rests on the edge. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] attempts to skim a stone, but it flops straight into the water. In response, the water ripples with the back of some kind of creature in the mid distance. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] fail to push off with any real ability, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] starts gently moving us along the surface.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] pushes us along gently and picks up speed. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], letting the curiosity get to him, misty steps from the raft to the island. As he does, the weight distribution changes and the raft rocks. Both [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] manage to hold on tight. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] scouts around quickly on the island. The chair is covered in runes, broad in nature, and emits a gentle magical essence. One of the runes is vaguely familiar and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] remembers it from the Road Warden's estate. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] feeling safe, casts Comprehend Languages. The runes read "Here is the last throne of the road warden. Come here to pay humble respects to the great keeper and creator of this road. Be merry in their presence." On the back "Those who keep the chair will know the secret to riches of this seat". [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] comes to the edge of the island and calls over to bring the staff of the road warden. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] levitates herself, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] shoves her across the gap.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] sits on the throne and with the staff, says the magic activating word, and suddenly the throne tilts forward revealing a set of steps beneath. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] levitates and pushes herself over after chickening out of pole vaulting. A 30x30 space below the throne holds a writing desk with a couple of books on shelves behind it. A mannequin holding some half plate and another with ceremonial style robes. A plaque holds the names of all previous wardens. A great axe hangs on the wall behind the desk. Inside the chest are two compartments. One holds scrolls and the other holds an ornate box.

Scroll of Mind Blank - 8th Level

[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] - 2x Potions of Healing

The books seem to contain journals and histories of the road wardens across a significant period of time. Filled with boastful entries of great feats, the back describes the act of the dwarves redirecting the great road and how the decision fell to the Road Warden to attempt to confuse the attacking armies of goblins and orcs. Their attempt to slow their approach to the Kings Hall and allow them to ambush. Books also cover topics like the "Inner Working of Stone", "Twin hammers of [[Pantheon of R'Ellanthas#Orag, the Hammer|Orag, the Hammer]]" and other such texts. Texts also cover working metal.

We return to the surface, close the throne chair, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] attempts to pole vault over the gap. Falling straight back down, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] saves her with Floating Disc, and both [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] hop over the gap. We pick up our trail again and continue further for another half hour. We slow as we hear voices in the distance shouting. One sounds dwarvish and the other sounds human, both shouting in common. The gruffer voice is shouting that "if (they) don't shut the fuck up (he'll) throw them off". Two disc platforms for mining elevator, one dangling vertically in the shaft, the other slightly lower than the edge of the mine with a 30ft gap. A human in unfamiliar robe, a human huddled against the metal chain, one human lying and another standing trying to play peacemaker with the dwarf shouting at them.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] steps out and introduces himself and the dwarf starts cursing. He introduces himself as [[Tormir, Champion of the King]]. Fighting deep around the kings hall, they had to make a push against approaching hoards. He claims the one in fancy robes is a Knight from the [[Alliance of Vennar]]. We talk to him and he introduces himself as "Thion Xia". They were skirting the outside of [[Bal Toruhm]] and was separated his group fighting a band of orcs. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts Switcheroo twice and swaps [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] onto the platform. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks the Hoarder "What have you got in your pockets?", and the Hoarder responds with scepticism. After a shoving match, the hoarder ends up on the floor dangling over the edge. He relents and eventually says "Coins, I have coins in my pocket". [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] promptly lets him fall over the edge. We levitate Thion and push him towards the edge after he promises to introduce us to the captain of [[Alliance of Vennar|the Alliance]]. After asking what we have that he can use as a weapon, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] hands [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] the Giant Slayer Greataxe, which he recognises as the Road Warden's. Promptly the conversation turns to what else we have, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] mentions the Staff of the Road Warden. A scuffle starts with [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] trying to snatch the staff, but being persuaded to leave it with us until we reach the king as he has no pockets.

Eventually, we make our way off down one of the paths that lead off the mineshaft, following [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]] to find the band of Orcs that robbed him of his possessions.
