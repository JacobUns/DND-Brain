Having divided up the contents of the chest, the party spends some time in the stalactite searching for information as to what the fortress was being used for. We find some paintings that make us think this is a hall for a few in number to find safety while they discuss important affairs. At the top floor we find the kitchens. There we also find a secret doorway in the ceiling. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] pulls the staff out and touches the door. It swings downwards and a set of carved steps open up to us. A fairly low tunnel, it seems to run in the same direction as the tunnel below.

We climb up and venture through. An hour later, we reach a solid rock wall and each member tries their best to listen to the other side. We hear nothing, and put the staff against the door again. It swings open without sound or fuss, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] leaves. Seeing a cluster of stalagmites crusted with crystals, we enter into the space and find an impressive cavern. Natural pillars line the space and it appears to have been a naturally grown location. The path slopes down gently and suspended 50-60ft above us are massive chains, pulleys and wheels going into holes in the ground with a walkway down the centre. One of the lifts is a huge platform, a 40ft circle of stone suspended. Another is leaning, one chain snapped, with four large storage containers atop brimming with a shiny ore. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] carves the negative impression of an owl on the door.

Navigating the cavern, [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] start to notice little trinkets, small stacks of coins and treasure, remnants of candles and torches. It starts to feel like this area is more than it seems, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] looks up and around. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] looks closer and all of the candles appear to have been lit and burned down over time. It's all clearly dwarvish and large amounts of wealth that has been accumulated over time. All the coins have been minted with dwarvish markings and runes depicting different figures. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] starts to think to her religious training and get a sense of reverence from the space. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], oddly, starts picking up some of the coins and pocketing them while [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] looks at some of the dwarven trinkets that have been left in the area. The trinkets are all of royal figures and seem to be depicting the same person in different interpretations. Each have runes that appear to be of the same name, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] realises that the coins also have the same name. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] ritually casts detect magic and finds a wall in an arch emanates a very strong abjuration magic. The magic feels ancient in its forming, but the wall feels just like stone. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] calls [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] over and asks her to use the staff on the area. Massive stone doors slide open. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] ritually casts comprehend languages and finds the words such as "Beruck 3", "Kingdom of ", "King Beruck Father of the Dwarves". Beruck the 2nd - 5nd are represented. We recognise it as [[King Barmus Beruck]].

We wander back towards the Mithril, little gift piles continuing, and a sloped path that appears well trodden, unworked and left natural, and emerge into a larger basin that flattens out. The large bridge has deep caverns that disappear into the dark on either side as we carefully peer over. The intact elevator is hovering in the air, the broken one dangling slightly below the level we're on, supported by eight chains. Five of the chains have snapped on the broken elevator and it hangs at an angle. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts levitate on [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and rises him 1ft off the ground. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] uses her banner and pushes [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] 1/3 of the way over the platform but doesn't have enough reach to go further. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] pulls [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] back, gives him the whip which he uses to pull himself closer to the cart. With hands quickly putting as much mithril into his bag of holding as possible, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to get 250lbs. Creeping closer to the cart, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to balance and take another 125lbs before deciding it's enough and returning with Misty Step.

As we turn back, a strange creature that's blending in with the rock in the cavern throws a boulder at us which we manage to avoid. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] with his knowledge of the arcane assumes that it may be an arcane golem used to guard the area from thieves. It starts throwing boulders constantly as we run as fast as possible back towards the door. Shards of rock pummel the area around us as boulders explode from impact. We manage to avoid everything until the last round, when the golem gets a good throw that would smash into [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] before he casts shield. In an explosion of arcane and rock fragments, the boulder explodes and the party make it through the door.

A worked hallways leads to a stairway 30ft away. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], keeping eyes out, finds that the wall has been lined with a marble-like finish. A gold inlay mixed with silvery colouring, probably mithril or platinum, in dwarven designs work up the walls. This appears to have almost perfectly preserved but is a massive display of wealth. Up the stairs we find a level platform that then rises again before reaching the next platform. The tunnel gives way to a room 60ft x 80ft x 40ft. Similar motifs line the walls and beautiful scenes have been carved into reliefs of fantastic scenes. In the centre of the room is a stone mausoleum, and in the centre is a sarcophagus made of a mix of stone and precious metals. As we walk towards the sarcophagus is an incredible looking hammer, and written upon the top in golden lettering is "Beruck, first of his name, father of his folk, a lord of [[Bal Toruhm]]." The hammer is obviously an incredibly finely made weapon, a piece of art among blacksmiths. The area around is completely free of dust and in pristine condition.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] communes with the gods and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] ritually detects magic. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] asks the gods two questions; the first, "is this mausoleum lost to the dwarves", Wheel response, and the second, "if we remove the hammer, will there be an immediate consequence?", which has a Woe response. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]]'s cast shows magic everywhere around her, various enchantments of an ancient nature, the room so well protected that it is likely the safest place within this mountain. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] picks up the hammer and finds it surprisingly light. Incredibly balanced, with a feeling of raw power in his hand, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] is surprised that nothing has happened to the room. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] tries one more time to commune with gods and asks whether removing the hammer will harm the group, which the gods respond with a Woe.

Forgefire, Hammer of Beruck. +3 Warhammer, Additional 4d6 fire damage as it rains the fire of its forge on its adversaries, and gives the bearer +4 to their strength score (going above the max of 20) as they gain the strength required to lead the dwarves. 

The group decide this is a good place to rest. We notice the reliefs depict the life and achievements of King Beruck the First, including the founding and construction of the city, great wars won, his rule as a prominent king of the dwarven people. We also see a very different stone amongst the reliefs which seems to depict a story of the dwarves mining deeper into the mountain and finding a precious purple looking stone. It seems to be similar to the stones we found previously made of the odd crystal material, and a large sample is embedded in the wall.
