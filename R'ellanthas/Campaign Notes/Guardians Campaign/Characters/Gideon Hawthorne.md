**Description**:
A rugged human ranger in his middle years. He has a strong, weathered face, thick blond-brown hair swept back messily, and a full pale-golden beard. His natural expression is calm but tired.

He wears practical adventuring leathers reinforced with metal fittings, straps, buckles, and worn armour pieces. A heavy white fur mantle covers his shoulders, giving him a northern, hunter-like presence. His clothing looks well-used rather than ceremonial, suggesting someone experienced, capable, and accustomed to hardship.

His build is broad and sturdy, with large hands, heavy boots, and a relaxed but powerful posture. Even at rest, he gives the impression of someone physically strong and dependable.

Beside him is a brightly coloured drake companion, red-scaled with teal-green feathering and long pale horns. The creature leans close to him protectively and affectionately, making the human feel less like a lone mercenary and more like a bonded beastmaster or drakewarden.

Overall, he reads as a seasoned, stoic, outdoorsman-type hero: protective, grounded, battle-worn, and deeply connected to his companion.
