We give the captain the information for Mavers and he sets us underway. Mavers is run by guildmasters, split into four major factions; Building, Trade Logistics, Merchants, and Artisan guilds carry great power within the city. Builders are responsible for the construction and labour within the city, Trade for exporting and managing logistics, Merchants for sale of goods, and Artisan for their craft. The city has stood for over 1000 years and is a mix of ancient and modern architecture. A fairly large class divide splits the area into those that have and those that have not. Those that own and operate the guilds far outstrip those that work for them in wealth.

Several key and famous museums dot the landscape, and though the area isn't specifically friendly with the Guardians, they are also not actively antagonistic. The city guard usually patrol the streets, man gates and fortifications, but for much of the day they are a deterrent more than an enforcement group. When needed, however, the guard are capable of acting as a standing army. Usually fairly well trained and equipped, they have the ability to defend their home and hold their fortifications with specialised groups capable of investigation, special forces and high strength enforcement.
