[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] sees a cloaked figure inside the doorway of the greenhouse. At this [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s attention flicks up. It seems segmented, glowing lights within the area. Arched dirty windows with plants growing all over them line the walls. Small walls, which seem to be planters with trees and plants that crawl their surfaces. From somewhere in the room, a creature chuckles, saying that there are more creatures for him to experiment on and that "they've sent him a blue one". Next to the tank at the back, a hunched creature in a cloak moves out holding a scalpel. He appears to be a wiry orc and he turns to the tank and starts fiddling with some kind of levers. Steam pours from the sides of the tank, and the liquid starts to lower. There is crystal that to be attached embedded in his side, and as [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] approaches he calls out that [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] has already been worked on.

[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and Mother dash forward and attempt to knock the Orc over. From within the tank, an ominous noise escapes the creature that appears to be waking inside. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] plugs the pipe back into the tank, and the orc says that he "has seen those upgrades before".

Bleeding condition - damage over time, healers kit use to stop the hitpoints lost.

Within the tank, the troll-like creature has 4 large crystals embedded into its back. It appears to be getting more aggressive as a result of whatever magic is held within. Remembering the orders, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] closes the valve on the chamber which starts to fill it with liquid. He threatens the Orc, eyes billowing with shadow, claiming if the medic doesn't tell him where Piper is he'll carve the crystals from its skin and bones.

As [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] drops the Medic, he pulls out a vial of liquid and drinks it. Suddenly, his body morphs and starts growing in scale. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], enraged, drops his rapier and pulls a dagger which the shadows gratefully accept. He starts to hack large chunks of flesh from the creature, eventually mounting it's shoulders and separating it's neck and body from top to bottom. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], almost in fugue, continues to dig his blade into the creature's side, flaying the flesh from bone. Inside, two medium arc cells glow. He lets out an almighty bellow of frustration and anger that to any in the area could read as pain.
