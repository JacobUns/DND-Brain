The gates are a bit rudimentary, and the original seem to have long fallen from their hinges. A stout Dwarf leads us in named [[Hurden]].  We explain that we're looking for their king, and that we've liberated the [[The Citadel of the Ringsmiths|Citadel of the Ringsmiths]]. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] looks around, He tells us the road ahead is straight with no exits along the path, about 6 miles before the first point of interest, the [[Hall of the Road Warden]]. It was a feast house and a safe haven for travellers. We inform him that the top of their mountain imploded, and [[Hurden]] is surprised by that. The dwarves have held the gate following instruction from the previous captain. [[Hurden]] introduces [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] to [[Malrus]], an apprentice wizard of the group, and he teaches the boy the sending spell. In the course of teaching the spell, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] gets Malrus to message [[Amnur & Beldren|Amnur]] after describing him to the young dwarf and send a scout to connect the groups and bring them back to the [[The Citadel of the Ringsmiths|Citadel of the Ringsmiths]].

We share food and stories with the dwarves and rest for the night. In the morning, they, with some dissent, agree to go back to the [[The Citadel of the Ringsmiths|Citadel of the Ringsmiths]]. Boulders and smashed scenes that look like siege defences scatter the floor, showing the scars of the area's history. The dwarves open their ramshackle gate door and we step through into the dark. Stealthily, we start moving down the tunnel. The bodies of the goblins and troll from the previous day lie lifeless, and further past shows the bones and decay of long passed battles. 

On the bodies of the goblins and trolls we find:
- A Scroll of Fog Cloud
- Jewels, Gems and Trinkets amounting to 500gp
- A Whip of Warning

Nix interrupts [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s thoughts as they group walk down the tunnel. She has found a break in the wall that looks like an alcove. It appears to have a tunnel leading off it and in front is a large fountain bubbling with water. The tunnel appears to have some rooms that lead off it after a quick fly by from Nix.

The tunnel is about 40-50ft long with 5 rooms branching off. Each room is 30x30. Shelves and broken boxes makes one seem like a store room. The next seems like a dorm, and next two look like they've been ripped apart. The last room has a goblin inside, fast asleep on the bed. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] attempts to walk in but stands on a piece of wood waking the goblin. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] whips out his rapier and walks down the goblin who trembles dropping his shank. 

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks the goblin his name, which the goblin claims is [[Mite]]. He throws Mite out of the room into the hall, where [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] stand. He claims there's a big hole in the ground along the path. The group carry on down the tunnel, and he talks about mean goblins that are tall as people with red skin. He describes a big village ahead of us with trading posts which have goblins who barter the goods that lesser goblins bring back. As we go, another way stop is visible with upended minecarts along the way. Sizable piles of bodies lie along our path, and the road starts to feel like it hasn't had any real usage in many many years. A bit more than half a days walk passes, and mid-afternoon the group comes onto a grand sight. The road opens up and a hall holds an ornately decorated area. The building seems almost intact, as though it hasn't been touched in hundreds or thousands of years and we assume this to be the [[Hall of the Road Warden]].

The doors are decorated with important looking dwarves and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] sends Nix ahead. It seems to be very similar to the waystations we've seen. The bedrooms are worn with time and up the stairs she finds a grandiose staircase to a hall line with benches and tables surrounded by fire pits. The walls are decorated with dwarves building the connecting road. The top of table has a large dwarven throne-like chair with writing on it. The depictions are carved, not painted, into the stone. Walking in, "Hall of the Road Warden" is written along the back wall in Dwarven, and the throne-like seat "May the honour of the road warden watch over all who travel this route". Looking around, there is a depiction of a dwarf who may have been the first road warden. Looking around, it seems like a very ordinary hall that holds a title of honour.

Mite describes "The Gauntlet". Everyone in your band of goblins line up on either side and the goblin who has disobeyed get beaten by their peers as they walk.

The group beds down in the hall, finding a secluded spot and setting up a mental alarm down a hallway.
