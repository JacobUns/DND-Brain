Nix continues to lead the way, small alcoves occasionally along the way. She halts and tells us there's a problem. Flicking into her vision, it becomes clear that the tunnel has been intentionally collapsed by something which [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] assumes is the dwarves. A little back from the tunnel is another dwarven tunnel. It appears to be more recently tunnelled into the rock, 150ft down with steps approximately two dwarves width. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] spots a symbol that has been left by a dwarven scout.

Down the tunnel we come to a cavern with many small paths. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] spots a few mining instruments and the other tunnels in the area seem to imply this might have been a mining area. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] finds a path up and left with the same symbol that he spotted at the top of the stairs. 1 safe, 2 different symbol, 1 with another different symbol. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s interpretation is that the first symbol is probably the safe passage, the second and third symbols [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] interprets false passages and traps. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] notes the different symbols in his book and their meanings.

Moving down the safely marked passage, it leads to a steep staircase up. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] notices that though the stairs are two dwarves wide, they don't have any side rails and as the steps ascend into the cavern we see many crisscrossed paths that have been made to confuse and mislead people without the knowledge to traverse the space. Travelling on, each platform has at least one more exit off including the safe path. The symbols are very obviously made to only be visible to those that know to look for them. Steps continue, and we can see veins of something running through the rock. They appear to be gemstones of moderate value that were being mined by the dwarves here. Gold, Iron and sparkling Emeralds and Rubies dot the area. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] attempts to discern whether the crystal material from previous mining station is in these veins but doesn't find any. Along the paths, rappelling stations and scaffolding structures make it possible to traverse the space vertically as well as laterally.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] starts to get the hang of the symbols and tunnels and starts spotting them herself. The group has been travelling for a couple of hours at this point and it's becoming very disorientating. None of us really know whether we're going in the direction we wanted to, but time is slipping by and fatigue is starting to kick in for the group. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] start to lose concentration but [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] keeps things on track. Four hours pass, and the symbols of paths for the slower directions are regular. Eventually, a platform ahead of us leads up to a tunnelled staircase leading upwards. As we reach the top, [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] see a room with a faint light emanating from it and there appears to be the murmur of voices speaking neither common nor dwarvish. The voices appear to be deeper than goblins though. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], with Nix on his palm, sees through the familiar's eyes and sees an impressive room. A stalactite of immense proportions has been carved into a defensible position with arrow slits and switchback staircase that leads up from the bottom of the stalactite's tip. On the opposite side appears to be another way of leaving the space. The floor is strewn with a mixture of bodies of goblin and dwarf.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] go invisible and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], in a feat of stealth unseen before, stealth's into the fortress. Without a lookout spotting us, we climb up the switchback staircase which leads to a door in the side of the rock. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] believes it to be orcish spoken inside the fortress as the group approaches. In a moment of luck, we try the door and it appears to be unlocked, revealing a tiny room with a sleeping orc on the other side. Behind him is a shield he's lying against and spear to his side. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] picks up the spear, the orc reacts with a gentle snore, and she walks it out of the door. The group move past without waking him and continue up. As we reach the next level we find a room with many windows making for good lines of sight to the bottom. Bows, crossbows, bolts and arrows make this an obvious place to hold the small entry from. Continuing up, the next room is similar but [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] detect that the voices we've heard seem to be on the level above us. We can discern four or five voices. They appear to be simply in conversation, relaxed without specific care for their duty.

At the top of the next stairs, five orcs sitting in fancy dwarven chairs. They appear snug to the orcs forms with their knees slightly raised. Lit braziers give off gentle light. This room seems to be the central hall to the fortress. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] surprises the group of orcs with a fireball and manages to deal significant damage across all orcs on the floor. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts Tasha's Mind Whip and drops one of the orcs and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] charges up the stairs and pierces the orc engaged by [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] drops two orcs in quick succession with his shadow blade, but one manages to get a critical hit through [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s shield. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] fires off her sorcerers burst shocking one to death, and the orc gets off a throw of its spear into [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]'s arm. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] closes on the last orc and with simple rapier strikes drops it deftly. It makes a feeble attempt to strike as it falls and the area falls quiet. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] positions herself for the approaching orc from the entry that had been on lookout while [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] prepares to let off a burst of magic. The orc sprints into the room, dodging the attack from [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and taking the burst of lightning. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] runs off up the stairs with a motion from [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] to approaching footsteps and sees two orcs with a commander running down the narrow stairs. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] blocks the steps fighting the orcs in a choke.

The orc in front of the staircase attempts to grapple [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] down the stairs but fails. The commander shouts to his minions. After some back and forth combat, the orc in the middle of the room falls to [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]]'s attacks and they move to the stairs. Meanwhile, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] is knocked down the stairs by one orc who kills himself falling at an awkward angle braking his neck. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] attempts to slow the commander but fails but succeeds against the other orc. The commander calls out in a rage and appears to heal slightly. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] drops the last orc, but in a ditch effort the orc hits his attack and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] drops focus on the flame blade. Recasting it, he steps into combat with the commander striking with flame blade and striking true. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] drops commander and blood spills over us. We venture up and find an ornate orcish throne with a metal staff inlaid with mithril and a massive diamond atop.

Staff of the Road Warden - Said to be able to control the hidden passageways and doors within the city. A pretty large chest sits next to the throne, its contents to be identified after our rest. We bar the doors and keep watch over night while we rest.
