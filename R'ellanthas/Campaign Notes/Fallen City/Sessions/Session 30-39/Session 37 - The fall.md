We travel back through the caverns towards Goblintown. The journey back is to take a couple of days, but by now the path is well known to us. We stumble across a couple of bands of orcs and goblins who seem to be coming out of the goblin town direction in an aimless manner. We dispatch them with minimal effort. They argue and squabble enough to not pay attention to us, and their lax nature is their downfall.

Goblintown itself has more of a sense of order, but its obvious that the fall of [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]] has left a power vacuum that was been filled by someone. We assume that the [[Hoarder Queen]] has stepped in to fill it as grandmother goblin, and the guards of [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]]'s quarters still stand watch but don't really seem to understand what for.

We head into the hoarder shop and manage to find a set of Mithral Half-Plate (2k) for [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]]. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to get a Tome of Leadership and Influence (20k) and [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] also manages to find a Monster Hunter's Glaive +3 (20k). 42k lighter, we make our way towards our next stop. As we head out, we see Grandmother Goblin wandering around the town. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] goes to the rat stand and gets a rat kebab, and asks about the goblins we'd seen leaving this place. The goblin tells us there was a power struggle with the warlord of the area, who had been having a feast and one of his very own men murdered everyone in the house and then went back to sleep. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] fanes surprise, and asks what happened to him, and says he was killed. He was in charge, but someone challenged him. The challenger wasn't very impressive, but somehow managed to kill the new warlord.

From there we head back through the [[Caves of Thistlebeard]]. We encounter a few more goblins who discuss how many fish they're meant to catch, and fell them before they even know we're there. The dwarves have made rapid progress on fortifications at the cave entrance. Set back a bit to lull goblins out, they have created hides and holes to catch the goblins unaware. A large stone door has been created that blocks the way towards the keep. The caves are bright with sunlight, the ground being made ready for planting. The population of the cave seems to have expanded with population around the area.

There, we reconnect with [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]]. We explain what's happened to us since we last met and suggest while a power struggle is underway they take back as much land as they can. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] asks about whether there are any stories of [[Draconomicon|dragons]] in these caverns. They have no stories of [[Draconomicon|dragons]], but do have some of a great creature that laid waste to many dwarves. We say that we've been told that it may have been a [[Draconomicon|dragon]] that caused the chasm covered with ice. He laughs, and says there are no [[Draconomicon|dragons]] in these parts. He explains about the chasm, and that there are tunnels where the entire place is blocked by a wall of ice. He points out the mask that [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s wearing, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] answers that it was that of the Bloodlord who assaulted the Halls of the King.

We meet with [[Amnur & Beldren]], and [[Amnur & Beldren|Amnur]] warms us with his theory of what's on the other side being cold. We laugh together, and ask for him to lead on. As we leave the Caves, we find some other dwarven lookouts and defensive towers that have been erected and [[Amnur & Beldren|Amnur]] calls up by name to the watchmen. We head into the dark, encountering a few small goblin parties, some with orcs trying to form their own little warband. A half day's travel onward, we eventually decide to camp.

24/48hrs done on the book.

In the morning, we head onwards and eventually find a tunnel that seems to just go for 300ft before abruptly ending. There, we find a wall of ice shimmering a deep blue, a fresh sheen of water on the surface. We spend some time attempting to melt our way through the wall before settling on creating a Flaming Sphere that bores a hole 5ft high through the ice. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] steps away and sets up her incense and communes with [[Olladra]].

She asks whether a live [[Draconomicon|dragon]] exists at the bottom of the chasm - the answer is yes.
She asks whether the [[Draconomicon|dragon]] has the ability to create ice - the answer is yes.
She then asks if she'd stayed at the temple, as a new oracle has been born, would she have been killed by her people - the answer is yes.

The spell fizzles, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] retreats into her own thoughts. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] approaches and asks if it was productive, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] responds that it was informative. She shares that we're to face a [[Draconomicon|dragon]] and the ice is indeed created by it. [[Amnur & Beldren]] bid us good luck at the mention of a [[Draconomicon|dragon]], and we wish them the best of luck.

Looking over the edge of the hole, we look into darkness below further than we can see. Above us stretches into darkness also, no sense of light permeates this place. The ice stretches below us. One by one, we jump from the edge of the chasm into the freezing cold, leaving what little warmth was left to us in the dwarven tunnels. We free fall for a short period before [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts feather fall, catching our momentum and slowing us to a safer speed.

Crystalline ice line the walls as we fall. 1200ft passes, and below [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] see a small amount of light below in a pink haze. There's at least another 1200 ft to go. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to pick out a shifting among the light, blocking areas of the glow. From the distance, it doesn't seem too big, but as we drop further it the size of the shape becomes apparent. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] recognises the scene from her vision, and starts to absently thing that maybe it wasn't the crystal, but the dark centre that drew her to it. We feather fall again, and as we descend we see that the cavern expands towards the bottom.

At the bottom, we find an array of Arc Crystals alight with the purplish glow, its light reflecting and refracting around us. As we hit land, [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] sees a large dark shadow looming at the edge of the glow. White scales ripple up a long neck as a head breaks the darkness and into the light. It booms "Who enters my lair", taking four massive steps forward and shaking the ground beneath us. It spreads its wings, and the pure size of its wingspan is awe inspiring and terrifying at the same time. It arches, teeth bared, fury in its eyes and takes a step towards us.
