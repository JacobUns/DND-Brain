The party lands softly at the bottom of the chasm from the featherfall, glistening crystals and sheets of ice running the length of the cavern's floor. From the darkness, thundering footsteps pound as a bellowing voice calls "Who enters my lair?!"

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] steps forward and calls out to it, saying we are here to find the source of the damage to this mountain. It lowers its head and snarls "My arrival caused the destruction of this place. You may call me the [[Iqanihiro, Bringer of Death|Bringer of Death]]." [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], draws his blade and smirks, responding "We shall see if you're worthy". He darts forward, sliding across the floor and between the legs of the [[Draconomicon|dragon]], slashing as he moves and deftly popping to his feet.

All hell breaks loose. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and Mother bound into action biting and slashing. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] starts consuming potions, unconcerned for the effects of mixing liquids. Crackling lightning streaks from [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]'s finger tips, breaking across the [[Draconomicon|dragons]] hide and electrifying the air in the room as an orb of chromatic light bursts from her form impacts with a burst of energy.

The [[Draconomicon|dragon]] launches into the air and with an almighty burst of its massive wings, pushes all but [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] back, impacting the walls of the chasm. It flits with unnatural speed behind [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and unleashes a massive burst of ice and cold that burns to the core of the party. Several members are encased in ice where they lie. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] misty steps onto the [[Draconomicon|dragons]] back, and attempts to burry his blades in its back, but can't pierce the thick scale and hide. It twists its neck and snaps at him, sinking its teeth into his form. It throws [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] from its back, then slams its massive forepaws and talons into the ice, spraying [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] with sharp spikes.

The battle rages, and in the course of the encounter with the [[Iqanihiro, Bringer of Death|Bringer of Death]] both sides take significant damage. Eventually, a lull in the battle leaves Mother, [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] unconscious and bleeding out, with [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] breathing heavily having barely survived the last energy blast from the [[Draconomicon|dragon]]. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] bears his teeth, and there is almost an air of sick enjoyment across his face. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] reaches into the bag of holding and pulls out a glowing arc crystal. He calls out in draconic "You prove worthy wrym. Lend me your strength to crush my foes, and I will serve. Deny me, and your life ends here". He starts to charge with arcane energy. The White [[Draconomicon|Dragon]] raises itself to its full height and responds in a deep and reverberating voice "You have also proven your worth, you would be a power ally in the battles to come. Swear your loyalty to me now by finishing your last remaining party member".

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s eyes flick down to [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] who continues to bear down on the [[Draconomicon|dragon]]. His eyes briefly close, and as they reopen, a hardness and wickedness runs through him. He recognises the sacrifice required to ascend to the power required to exact the vengeance he so desires on those that have made him what he became against his will. The charge that's been building in [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] comes to a boil, and a mote of blue white fire emits from his index finger with a twist and flourish that finishes the somatic spell component. Like the world temporarily enters a temporal shift, the mote floats silently towards the rocky bluff between [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and the White [[Draconomicon|Dragon]]. As it moves, closer, it drops short of the [[Draconomicon|dragon]] and consumes [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] in fire. He falls, and the reverberation of the blast echoes up the chasm followed by a yawning gulf of silence.

The [[Draconomicon|dragon]] takes one powerful step forward, lowering its head down to [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s level. In the gruff draconic staccato, [[Iqanihiro, Bringer of Death|Iqanihiro]] barks out "Kneel and take your place as my champion". Still recovering from the battle, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] steps forward with the rapier still hanging from his right hand. He flips it up and buries its point in the ice. The [[Draconomicon|dragon]] lifts its massive claw and touches the point of one talon lightly against [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s head, bestowing an Epic Boon upon him. 

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] lightly steps up the foreleg of the [[Draconomicon|dragon]] to its back and positions himself between thick scale and horn, in a position that almost fits like a natural saddle. In a heartbeat, [[Iqanihiro, Bringer of Death|Iqanihiro]] launches into the chasm, bounding from walls with massive talons digging into rock and ice. In an instant, the two travel from the floor to the peak of the mountain and burst through to the ice cold air and a plateau of cold wrought stone. There stand two figures; one a massive red [[Draconomicon|dragon]], its blood red scales dancing with dark wine shades in the shadows and reflecting the ice and snow around with an almost rose tone. Its form dwarfs [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], but it is nothing in comparison to the gargantuan black [[Draconomicon|dragon]] the stands beside it. With talons that look as though they could cleave the ground in two with but a single stroke, and teeth longer than the height of a man, its glistening scales look like an impenetrable onyx suit of armour. The two converse in gruff draconic speech that diminishes as [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Iqanihiro, Bringer of Death|Iqanihiro]] approach.

[[Iqanihiro, Bringer of Death|Iqanihiro]] bows low to the black [[Draconomicon|dragon]], and as he does [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] slips from his back. As the White [[Draconomicon|Dragon]] rises, he turns to [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and says "There is much work to do to reshape this world, and you will be a great part of that".

In the cold of the ice below, [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]], who had fallen unconscious following the attack by [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], stirs as they barely cling to life. They find themselves alone in the silence again. Mother lies still, her body lifeless, as does [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]]. In a solemn sign of companionship, they move [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] together and link their hands. Forming a small kindling, they cremate their fallen companions. From there, they move to the body of Mother. Performing a blood ritual, they birth a pup from the remains of the she-wolf's body. With the low noises of new birthed pup, they slowly and with great pain limp towards the exit. In the final light of the glowing crystals, had anyone been there to see, the form of [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] looks to be permanently altered by the rite of rebirth. Blade-like protrusions of bone extend from their skin, and a thatched crown of bone sits upon their brow as they slink into the dark.
