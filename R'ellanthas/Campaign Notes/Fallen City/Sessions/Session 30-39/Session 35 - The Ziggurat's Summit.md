Having a quick search around, amongst the detritus and junk that has been collected over years of the trolls camping, we find:

4442gp Gems - 1111gp 2x (Kayn and Frontistis) 1110 (Maria and Oh)
2x Potion of Storm Giant Strength (Frontistis and Maria)
Figurine of Wonderous Power (Marble Elephant) (Frontistis)
Dwarven Thrower (Maria)

We return to the dwarves and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], on the flying broom, ferries them over to the other side. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] engages them in conversation while [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] uses her prayer of healing. They were 30 dwarves before. The three trolls ambushed them, killing many of their number immediately and capturing the rest of them. Before that, they were simply moving from place to place looking for a be safe after their last camp was discovered.

They don't know where the eagle came from. There used to be eagles that lived in the upper reaches of the mountain that were used as messaging. We give them a bit of information on the king, the fall of his hall, and the rejuvenation of the caves. We then ask if they have any news of the area, whether they know of the Bloodlord and where he's living or the Den of Stars. They don't know much of him, only that he has a temple and we're heading in the direction for it. The Den of Stars they know as an old mine that hit a deep mithril vein.

**[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]** attempts to use **Perception** to look and feel for currents of air that feel fresher - 21 #travelcheck 
**[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]]** attempts to use **Religion** to give a sermon to get us through the time - 8 #travelcheck 
**[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]]** attempts to use **Insight** to try to figure out if there is going to be a fork in the path - 24 #travelcheck 
**[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]** attempts to **Performance** to mock [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]]'s sermon and turn it into dirty marching songs - 14 #travelcheck 

We have a fairly successful travel through the cavern for the remainder of the day. We eventually find an end to the path that moves into a closed tunnel. The juvenile eagle nips at [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and bows before jumping from the ledge and flies towards the top of the cavern. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] stares off with sadness as it leaves the party, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] comforts her with stories of the Eagle mounted Paladins of the Empire.

We eventually find a tiered residential area that makes us feel confident we won't be snuck up on. The group bed down, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] barely managing to avoid smothering [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] after she starts telling us the story of her family again.

After a generally quiet watch, with [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] spending some time looking for the way out from our higher vantage point, we wake to the next day. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] tells us that the sign above the corridor leading out reads "The [[Den of the Forgotten Stars]]". 

We start to traverse across the space, looking for a way down before we reach the Den, but eventually start coming across an area that appears to be the beginning of a mine. Not long after, [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] picks up the acrid smell of smoke and starts leading us in that direction. Before too long, the smell starts to intensify and the rest of us pick up on it. Knowing that the dwarves are wary of open fires and free flowing smoke. We get the feeling as we move on that it's below us to our right. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] leads us further on to a set or staircases and then we see it. A large diameter tunnel, rough hewn from the rock and obvious not dwarven, is billing black smoke. We see tracks that show recent movement on the stone below, but not with enough detail to know how long they've been there.

The tunnel seems fairly innocuous. No traps or hiding holes are visible, and after a 50ft walk we find a dull orange light emanating from the end of the corridor. At the edge of a small slope, we lie flat and peer over the edge to find a three-tiered ziggurat. The cavern is massive, but even so it is lit enough to see the grandeur of the structure before us. Six watch towers with rope bridges connecting each surround the area with bridges also connecting on the second tier of the ziggurat. On each tier, many windows open in irregular and asymmetrical patterns.

This is obviously not dwarvish. The architecture is uneven, sticking at odd angles in places, and rough in nature. In steel on top of it is an enormous structure almost like a crown which is billowing smoke. The floor of the cavern hasn't been cleared like we would expect of a dwarvish construction. Chipped rock and oddly shaped terrain give some cover between us and the door. Along the bridges walk a few guards, and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] believes she spots quivers but can't determine what weaponry they carry.

We cast fly on [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] after she has drunk the potion of storm giant strength. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] on the broom and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] carrying everyone else, we cast invisibility on the party and fly to the top of the second tier. Several doorways lead in from the sizable landing zone. There don't appear to be patrols inside the perimeter of towers. Flying up to a window, we look inside and find a single massive room on the same level as the outside of the tier. On the far side, a couple of stair cases lead up to a raised dais. It feels like a throne room, with a large and vicious looking chair. Upon it sits a large hobgoblin, and deeper into the raised platform is a long table with several other hobgoblins sitting at it talking and eating. No patrols inside give us the feeling they're confident of their defences outside making them aware.

We decide on the most direct route, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] attempts to open the door as quietly as possible. A questioning tone in orcish or goblin calls out as the door moves and we rush through quietly. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], at the back of the group bundling through the small space, walks directly into the hobgoblin. The creature in confusion starts looking about for what it hit. We find that the hobgoblins are all now aware and wary. The guard slams open the door looking outside then slams the door shut, dropping a large and sturdy plank across locking it.

Telepathically, we agree to charge the dais with [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] focusing the Bloodlord and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]], [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and Mother taking the guards. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] fly straight for him.
