After a quick discussion, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] goes invisible and follows the Grandmother Goblin as she moves through the town greeting creatures as she passes. Those in her path seem to have a look of fear and move to the side out of her way as she approaches them, keeping their eyes down and trying to be unnoticed. She passes the tavern and stops at a table of goblins, drinks one of their drinks without even a word. Two orcs follow her a few steps behind. No one is really looking at the orcs, but any sight of her is met with fear.

From one of the houses, a scuffle happens and a goblin is flung out of the door and into the street. After a bit of watching, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] thinks that Grandmother is looking for something that is owed to her. After being picked up by one of the orcs, he eventually goes limp and stops struggling before being turned upside down and shaken. They throw him to the ground, kicking the goblin as he lands before it retreats back into its house. The street leads to a market thoroughfare, and eventually to a fancy building that appears to be a bank or similar counting house. Whoever lived here was obviously a wealthy dwarf and the grandmother has taken up residence where she sees fit.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] quickly closes the distance, and with the guards paying all of their attention to Grandmother, he manages to squeeze through the open door. Within, a courtyard stands with a rotten tree at its centre. Bioluminescent plants and mushrooms dot the area giving a faint glow to the room. A balcony runs around the courtyard with stairs to climb up to it. A heavy stone doorway with a carved mural of a dwarven figure stands at the opposite end of the courtyard from the door. Gilded with gold, it speaks of the opulence of this place. Grandmother figure enters through the space and the two orcs take their places standing guard. She shuffles down the hall and approaches the door, lightly touches it and it swings open. She enters, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] notes the balcony has windows. Going over to one, he tries to look through but sees nothing. He opens the window quietly and finds himself in a guest room. Finally seeing sense, he turns back and makes his way to a balcony that overlooks the main square and the estate wall. He jumps over the edge, stumbling and rolling his ankle, and finds the group to debrief.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] provides a summary of the events within and his findings, and the group decides to head back to [[Caves of Thistlebeard|the Caves]] of Thistlebeard, wait for the dwarves, and discuss the next steps. 

We set up in the castle area, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] going to the stream to clean up leaving [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] to sit and transcribe the Seeming spell into his book. After a short time, the first of the dwarves start to arrive. A few hours later, the king arrives with the majority of the dwarves. We fill him in on the events and he then turns to organising the forces. We're greeted by [[Tormir, Champion of the King|Tormir]], [[Thion, Knight of Vennir|Thion]] and several others we have engaged with on our travels. The king greets us in the morning, and we talk through the place we now stand. We show him the Arc Crystal. He explains that they used to mine them and sell them to the elves primarily. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] also talks to the battle with the Medic, but [[King Barmus Beruck|King Beruck V]] and his attendants haven't heard of him. We talk about the town, and the existence of Grandmother. The King says that he believes they are the Houses of the Exiles, dwarf refugees that had travelled from another fallen city. The Dwarves of [[Bal Toruhm]] and those of the Exiles' relationships were strained, quarrelling over petty things. The king commits scouts to the investigation of the town. 

They want to setup a foothold in [[Caves of Thistlebeard|the Caves]], and their primary task while here will be to repair the mirrors to allow light into [[Caves of Thistlebeard|the caves]]. This would make both the orcs and goblins weaker in the area. We discuss the possibility of downtime.

30/30hrs on designs for the hammer
48/48hrs on the Manual of Bodily Health

We discuss next steps, and the king asks if we would be willing to meet [[Tassora]]'s group and help guide them back. He offers the services of Horrak and Bergron, two scouts, to join us in the journey.

The king lays out their needs:
- Investigate the Mirrors and restore them
- Investigate the cause of the mountain's collapse
- Remove key figure heads from the goblinoid creatures
	- [[Dohma Rashkovar]]
	- [[Bloodlord Tharox]] (Hobgoblin) - Somewhere in the deeps
	- [[Imrolth]] - Don't know much about the creature. Mentioned in writings but never seen or heard spoken.

He believes that something must be giving the orcs and goblins some reason to keep fighting, some great evil forcing them onwards.

[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] manages to make contact with her deity and asks what they want of her in this place. The light energises her, and the being says to her that she needs to "bring light to the darkness". The next night, she is shown a vision of a great cavern in the deeps of this mountain. From above, she sees a glow around the cavern of purple light. In the centre of a purple circle of light is a darkness that cannot be penetrated. As she comes out of the vision, she thinks of the purple glow sconces emanate the glow of the purple light from the Arc Crystals.

We leave for [[the Citadel of the Ringsmiths]] and meet with [[Gulnur, the Ringwright|Gulnur]], and sets out to start the crafting of an item to impress the [[Dwarven Ringsmiths|Ringsmith]]. With skill, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to craft an item worthy of the [[Dwarven Ringsmiths|Ringsmiths]].
