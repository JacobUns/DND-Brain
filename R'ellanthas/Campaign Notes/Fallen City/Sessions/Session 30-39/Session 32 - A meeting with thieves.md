We walk through the gallery looking over the edge of the path to see pipes leading below us. Six cisterns show this is likely an area that once may have been used for water control. We emerge the other side of a short bridge to find a built up area of residences which get bigger and bolder as we progress through the area. Larger, obviously once beautifully carved arched mansions start to pop up before we eventually reach a large door guarded by two minotaur. One holds a greataxe, the other a large sword strapped to his back. We approach as [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts Enhance Ability on [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and herself focusing charisma.

The space is wide open and easy to see anything approaching, and the minotaur don't seem phased as we get closer. The group approaches together and one calls out to ask if we have an appointment. We say we do, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] gives our names. The minotaur pulls out a list and scans it but obviously can't find our names. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] attempts to approach closer and look at the list, but the other lowers his axe and warns us off. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] does manage to see the list and reads a name, Azumrale. He attempts to lie their way past, but the minotaur says that Azumrale is a large firbolg who has already been here today. He calls out our lie as an attempt to gain access without an appointment and briskly pushes us through the door saying the Queen will like us.

As we enter, we find a lobby that has sconces on either side with a staircase that leads up in a spiral. It doesn't feel as straight edged as a dwarven construction; its lines flow slightly too easily. At the top of the stairs we find a double door entryway with a landing covered in boxes stacked in organised groups. They appear to be shipping crates, and nearby stand carts that seem to be ready to take them. To the left are another pair of double doors guarded by another minotaur. Through it, we see the breaking of light through the door cracks. He points to another door across the way from us and says "That way".

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to convince him to open the doors for us. He talks into the air, seemingly, and asks if he can let us outside. He gets confirmation from somewhere, and lifts the massive wood keeping the door closed. He throws open the doors and reveals a paved track that leads down the mountain. Snow blows through the door, air frigid with sub-zero crispness. As we peak through, a tower looms 150ft tall and breaks through the clouds. We have a magic trick battle briefly, the minotaur pulling a coin from behind [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s ear and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] pulling light from behind the minotaur's.

In the distraction, [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] sees Dwarvish Armour, Weaponary and other paraphernalia being loaded into the boxes. The floor is marked from the cart track, and there is an area to tie horses up.

We head up the staircase, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] realises there is a handrail; something that we haven't seen in any other dwarven location. We climb for what feels like ages. Another landing platform holds another minotaur guarding a door. Again, another landing, and beyond is an opulently decorated drawing room furnished richly with a raging fire. Sofas, chairs, and a desk make this feel like a space for people to meet. Fine drapes hang from the walls and lush rugs cover the floor. A window on either side of the room look out onto beautiful vistas of the surrounding landscape. Opposite this room is a closed doorway.

[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] doesn't comprehend the material of the rugs, believing it to be moss. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] convinces him its poisonous, and [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] goes to sniff it just in case. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] takes the opportunity to knock him face first onto the rug. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] moves over to a window and looks out. He believes we're halfway up the tower and that there's likely only one more floor above us. The road below drifts away and twists into the mountain. Noticeably, there isn't a large city on a lake visible which implies we're looking east rather than west. The other window almost entirely looks out onto the mountainside.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] muses as to why there isn't a guard on this level, until a voice from behind the desk calls out that it may be because the person on this level doesn't need guarding. A human woman in her 40s with a large scar cleaving her cheek emerges as if from nowhere. Dressed in rich fabric dyed purple, she sits confidently behind the table, her arms lazily propping her up.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] speaks first, asking if she is the queen. She says she is, and when asked what we can call her Queen, or we can just call her Leah. She stands and motions towards the sofas for us to sit and talk. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] compliments her decoration of the space. She says the space was originally made by elves. She explains that once the dwarves and elves had a good relationship and that the elves once relied heavily on this mountain. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks why they would, and she produces an illusion of an Arc Crystal. The elves would put the crystals to use in their society for things such as lighting and powering things.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks why she would be interested in it, and she says she only is for trade. There's apparently a bustling trade in the towns if you know where to look. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] pulls conversation back to the mountain and asks why her [[Hoarders]] have never seen her, and she says that the mystery is sometimes better protection. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] asks why she allowed us an audience, and she says she was interested in us. Some [[Hoarders]] have attempted to gain access in the past when joining the group, but attempting to lie our way past the guards was an interesting tactic. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] professes that it wouldn't have made sense to just say can we come in, which she acknowledges.

[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] asks whether she has information on what caused the mountain explosion. She says she does, but asks what we intend to trade. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] pulls out the mithril weapons he has fabricated and says she hasn't named her price. She walks over and picks up the mithril rapier, swishing it expertly.

She explains that the fissure was created by a release of massive arcane force that could only have been caused by an [[Arc Cells|arc cell]]. She explains that she has been trading [[arc cells]] with the orcs of this mountain for quite some time. She believes they were performing some kind of ritual or something and it released the stored energy. Her best intel was that it may have even been below the deeps. One particularly good spy managed to find a great cavern filled with [[Arc cells]] surrounding the edge of a strange purple glow around the cavern. It seemed to create the fissure that unfortunately caused the collapse of the structures above. She calls out [[Tarloch Halls]], saying it is a ruin of what it once was with the fissure covered in ice.

We ask why she's not concerned by it at all, but she says there's simply money to be made. The fissure is concerning, but it's far enough away that it hasn't affect her tower. We pressed for the information on whether the gate and road had always been here, she explains its an elvish road that has stood for centuries. She also explains that the mountain holds significant knowledge which is the real jewel. 

When asked about the goblin and orcish names, she explains that she knows of two of them well and one she has only heard whispers. She hears whispers of Imrolth amongst the goblins, and see it written in various locations.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] fabricates another mithril rapier for the queen as payment for the names, leaving a personalised touch of her insignia in the pommel. She admires the personal touch and then begins to divulge the information.

[[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]] resides in the west of the city, mostly in a little village called Goblintown. He will rest there after lengthier travels. She produces an illusory version in life size of [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]]. Before us is a 6'5'' orc with bulging muscles, fine ropes wrapped around his arms that connects to a deep red cloak with various golden coins and trinkets hanging from its hem. A finely made breastplate adorns his chest with long braided hair from which hang more golden baubles. His deep green skin plays against the opulence that he wears, obviously enjoying the spoils of his conquest. Fierce features and fangs cut a stark difference. She explains he's a tough individual, but probably no match for a group such as ours. On his own, he's easy, but with a collection of warriors we may find a harder time.

[[Bloodlord Tharox]] is the leader of the goblins and hobgoblins in this mountain. She produces another illusory image showing a pink skinned hobgoblin with a pointy helmet adorned with skulls. On his face is a white mask that appears to be draconic in nature. At his side is a jutted blade in a scimitar fashion. He speaks of power without needing to show it off. His armour is all made in dark metals that end in spikes and wicked edges. Leah explains that he takes up residence in the headquarters of the orcs and goblins; the [[Udun Temple]] located in the deeps somewhere between two and three on the eastern side of the mountain. The temple is not of dwarven construction, but orc and goblin. He's likely to return to his seat of power.

[[Imrolth]] continues to be a mystery, with only goblins whispering. She suspects that if we head towards the bottom of the fissure. We're likely to find whatever he might be there. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks why she would be afraid of myth or legend. She says she's not scared of myth, but history. Saying she believes it to be ridiculous to say, but she believes it may be a [[Draconomicon|dragon]]. She continues that something gives ill power to these creatures, and one hobgoblin surely wouldn't be capable of keeping them together.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks what Leah's end goal is, surely she can't be intending to stay there forever. She says that she believes her days here are numbered. If what she suspects is true, she doesn't think the dwarves would take 

The group discusses the idea of just goals, and eventually we pry out of Leah that she wishes to never see the coming of a [[Draconomicon|dragon]]. She's not against helping anyone, and having made an obscene amount of money from the fallen kingdom this place once was, she is coming to rethink her place in the world. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] asks what she could offer to aid the dwarves in their effort to retake their home.

The tower was once an outpost for traders who assisted the dwarves. It could be again. She offers opening her wealth of knowledge to us, and her hefty resources to assisting the dwarves in exchange for our risking our lives in the bottom of this fissure and putting in a good word with the king.
