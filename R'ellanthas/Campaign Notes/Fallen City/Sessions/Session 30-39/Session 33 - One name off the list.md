[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] spends time going through the contents of the books in the [[Hoarder Queen]]'s library. He finds some general information about the use of arcane crystals and their use within biological creatures. But after some time of searching he comes up blank, only finding information about the use of arc crystals in weaponry and constructs. In the other books, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] finds spells of all manner of kinds and pulls out a number of items to transcribe into his spell book.

We approach the [[Hoarder Queen]] and give her our path, telling her we are heading to Goblintown to deal with [[Dohma Rashkovar]]. She informs us that she can teleport us there as she has a residence there. She leads us up to the library and starts to scrawl the magical sigils and runes for her teleportation circle

Have the etching for the Teleportation Circle for the Goblintown residence of the [[Hoarder Queen]].

We arrive in a feasting hall, a long table down its centre. This appears to be a grand room that's started to fall to disrepair. Reliefs of important dwarvish events, religious figures and other similar iconography. After a short wait, the goblin figure of Grandmother Goblin walks towards us. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] realises that he's seen the place before when sneaking through the house. We discuss that we came close to attempting to kill her. She then tells us that she is responsible for the creation of Goblintown. 

She sneaks us out the back of the manor into an alleyway with one exit. We encounter a goblin at the end of it relieving itself against a wall. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] goes the spear it through with their glaive, but [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] pauses him to not make a scene. He confronts the goblin, asking if he knows whose establishment its pissing on. In full flow, the startled goblin turns and continues to piss directly onto [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]]'s boot. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] looks down slowly, and back up to it, a snarl forming on his lips. He tells the goblin to put that thing away before he regrets it. With a nervous laugh, the goblin gets himself together and runs off.

We head towards the market and see Grandmother Goblin with her guards. We follow behind her at a distance as she makes her way through, goblins giving her a wide birth. She approaches a grandiose residence and stops in front of it, her guards stopping to talk with her. We attempt to blend in with a nearby group of orcs within hearing range of her conversation. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] manages to make out Grandmother's words, asking after [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]] and whether he is likely in. They seem to reply and their body language reads as though they are agreeing. Grandmother snaps at one of them, and it runs inside before she turns to us and messages [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]]. She says that [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]] is in and been told by the guard to not bother the town today.

We sneak around the edge of the property, its mid-sized wall that runs around the premises giving just enough cover to hide us behind it at full height. Just tall enough to peer over on tiptoes, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] try to get a look over the top. Beyond, we're presented with a small garden. Stone raised planters full of weeds and mushrooms growing within and we think this would once have been a nice area to relax in, but has now fallen to disrepair. The orcish guards seem to be focused primarily on the front of the property, with a few sparse watches upheld at the back.

[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] heads around the edge and climbs over the wall while invisible. She starts attempting to make animal noises to lure guards over. She starts making cow and chicken noises, managing to pull two guards out of their stupor of boredom and pulling their eyes away just long enough to allow the rest of the group to hop the wall and find some cover.

[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] hides herself behind a wall and throws a rock in a random direction to lead them off so she can rejoin us. We find an open window on the outside wall to the property. Inside, we can see make shift beds and the sounds of snoring. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] mantles over the windowsill and finds the snoring creature is an orc guard taking its turn in resting. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] turns and mimes being quiet and then waves the group in. We start to move across, then note the orc's sword and shield. Following a message from [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]], [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] heads back, puts the sword in the bag of holding, and then moves back to the hall. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] peak around the corner, and [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] picks up on raucous laughter and merriment coming down the stairs from above us. The corridor cuts across the estate, and on one end we see the entry that we would have come through if we hadn't come through a window, and on the other is the main entryway.

Before we move leave the room, we go back and close the window we came through, and [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] finds an exterior wall. She presses her staff against the wall and whispers the magical trigger word "Fortunum", fortifying and sealing all entry ways from the outside. With that, we head up the twin staircases to the top landing. We hear sounds of feasting from ahead of us we emerge onto a large open space with two rooms leaving off the sides. On one end is [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]], none the wiser to our presence.

We rush the table and quickly take down the 7 orcs and [[Dohma Rashkovar|Dohma]] who sit around the table. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]'s Chromatic Orb bounces rapidly around the table. Between the party, we make our way through the group cleaving throw flesh, eventually leaving the bodies lying not far from where they sat eating.

Around the area, we investigate the rooms off each side and find not much of note in one room, finding 200gp worth of coins and items. In the other, we find a map which has [[the Citadel of the Ringsmiths]] marked with a large circle and the main road with dwarvish iconography. Ink pots and quills dot the space with a lavish bed in one corner. At its edge, is a trunk.
