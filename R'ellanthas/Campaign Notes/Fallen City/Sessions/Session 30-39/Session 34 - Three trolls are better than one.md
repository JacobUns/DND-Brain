We agree to make our way to the next target, but first stop by the merchant in the town picking up several items for cold resistance and potions of advantage. We head towards the mansion of Grandmother Goblin and sneak back inside to avoid the guards. As we enter and set down to wait for her, [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] gets an itch to check the room she seems to turn into other guises. She finds a room that's nicely furnished and stocked with books of varying languages, but oddly she finds no bed. Returning to the group, [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] explains that 
Vampires, Elves, and similar creatures don't require sleep, but there are also certain types of magics that would allow someone to sleep elsewhere.

We discuss our plans to move on [[Bloodlord Tharox]]. She explains we have to go down through the mines and move across to what was known as the [[Den of the Forgotten Stars]]. The den itself was one of the principal mithril mining locations. The [[Udun Temple]] was built more recently in the deeps nearby. It will be a trek across from here as the [[Hoarder Queen]] doesn't have a manner of teleporting.

We get access to Rary's Telepathic Bond and are offered a bed for the night. We accept, staying in the mansion. We exit through goblintown and start seeing signs towards the deeps. We move through the long straight residential areas before we eventually hit the winding tunnels.

**[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]]** attempts to use **Medicine** to clear their senses and help pick up the direction - 23 #travelcheck 
[[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] attempts to use **Persuasion** to tell us about her family and keep us awake - 14 #travelcheck 
[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] uses a **Constitution Saving Throw** after listening to try and keep his temper - 23 #travelcheck 
[[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] uses **Athletics** to try and use exercise as a manner of distraction from the stories - 3 #travelcheck 

We manage to cross the distance, weaving through what has become a bit more familiar territory. It feels as though we've been through some of these tunnels and eventually cross into some unfamiliar parts towards the end of the afternoon. We eventually find a 10ft wide ledge that backs onto the rock wall with a chasm gaping on its other edge. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] spot a path off the left part of the path ahead. We start carefully making our way across, before [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] then spot torches faintly flickering across the way. On the opposite side of the chasm, 12 shadowy figures seem to be moving in the opposite direction. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] takes out her spyglass and look through it, finding a surprising sight of 12 dwarves and a large bird that appears to be chained. They appear to be stripped of their protective gear and emaciated. A door is at one end, but it appears to be closed and unable to be opened on this side, so we start to think it's a form of imprisonment.

[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] fly over on the broom, and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] asks what they're doing on this ledge. An elderly female dwarf calls back in a hushed tone that there are 3 trolls that have kept them prisoner on the other side of the door. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] drops off [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] near what appears to be a giant juvenile eagle. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] talks to the woman who says to move the rest of them over first. Getting help from a fighter, we ferry the first dwarf over and [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] starts to assess them for wounds. Ferrying [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] across, we start to find try and move more across quickly with Fly. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] approaches the eagle carefully and gently bows. Its sad eye looks at them wearily but it returns the bow. Wrapped in chains and muzzled, they reach up to the muzzle and try to remove it. As we're arriving for the second trip, a Troll with plate armour and a massive sword in hand. It is reaching towards the elderly woman and [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] lets out a hold monster. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] lets off an arrow attempting to ensnaring strike it, but the vines are ripped apart with ease. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]], in a fit of desperation, stands on the back of the broom and jumps into the abyss before casting Switcheroo but the creature resists, sending [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] into a freefall.

[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] summons her beast of the sky which rends the troll. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]], balancing on the broom, launches himself forward and misty steps gripping the troll's plate and stabbing it in the neck. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]], unable to assist, checks the other side of the ledge to ensure there's nothing coming to ambush her. [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] casts fly as she falls, extending her spell cast with her innate abilities, to make it back up to the edge. The troll grabs [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] and hurls him off the edge at [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]], impacting with a thump in mid air.

[[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] manages to recall the broom and fly back towards it, but [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] attempts again to switch with the troll. This time, it fails to resist the magic and finds itself 90ft over the edge with [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]] safely in its place. It falls, bellowing as it falls into the depths of a chasm of unknown depth. From the other side of the door we hear a guttural voice of surprise. [[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] casts a biohazard area near the door.

A hulking form appears in the doorway, looking out towards [[Oh, Air Genasi|Oh]]. It turns in confusion and says "Who are you?" and her response is "I'm your new boss! I just dispatched the other troll". She lets off a sorcerer's burst, and the troll charges her. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] flies over and ineffectually attempts to stab the troll but his blade glances off its natural hide armour. Battle continues back and forth briefly, before the troll turns and runs. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] teleports back to the other side, grabs [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] and dimension doors on the other side of the now closed and locked prison door. The troll immediately knocks [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] to the ground and lays into her with vicious strikes, almost knocking her unconscious.

[[Frontistis Zoon|Frontistis]] attempts to strike the door and open it. The first strike dents it, and the second strike blows it open. [[Kayn, Light's Bane|Kayn]] slides along the ground between its legs, stabbing as it goes, and then laying into it with two more strikes. [[Maria Ravencroft|Maria]] climbs to her feet and pierces it through twice with her pike. As it falls, she uses the momentum of the pike to mount its chest. Looking dishevelled and worse for wear, the group collects themselves before going back to the dwarves.
