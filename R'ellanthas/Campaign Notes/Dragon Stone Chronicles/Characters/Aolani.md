# About
A tall figure with human features, something isn't quite normal about the man in well kept and finely made black armour. It might be the starkness of the streak of black that runs through his otherwise pale, almost white, hair or it might be the slight shimmer from his temples that almost looks like tiny gemstones under his smooth skin. Whatever it is, Aolani cuts an intimidating and slightly unnatural figure.
## Appearance
**Height**: 6' 0''
**Eye Colour**: Amber/Gold
**Skin Colour**: Lightly Tanned
**Hair Colour**: Pale Blonde - Almost White, with a 2'' thick black stripe
**Physical Description**: Though not overtly strong, his quiet confidence and broad shoulders portray a man who is undeniably powerful - as if honed through years of relentless training. Tight, sinewy muscles speak not of brute strength but of efficiency and elegance, his skin baring the scars of his training. Not imposing in sheer size, there is an unmistakable intensity in his stance and stride that commands the attention of those around him.

# Detailed Backstory

> [!spoiler]- Spoilers Ahead
>There is little Aolani remembers of his life before the clergy. Growing up in the warmer climate of [[Dor-Gairn]], one of the free cities of [[Tarador]], would have been idealistic for many that had normal childhoods. Where most children of the city would spend their hours playing in natural pools and springs, Ao would spend his days reading the scriptures of the [[Light Domain]] and learning the teachings of [[Dol Arrah]]
>
>Only a few faint traces of memory for the woman who birthed him remained. Phantom scents of freshly picked wild flowers occasionally bring back almost shattered recollections of a woman called El, her face shadowed in his fading memory. The clerics of his order tell the story of a woman bringing him as an infant to the order's temple in [[Dor-Gairn]] claiming to be incapable or unfit to raise him. The temple initially was unwilling to take the child, but one of the [[Light Domain|Order of Dol Arrah]], a [[Light Domain#Radiant Watcher|Radiant Watchers]] by the name of [[#Seraphine Liora]], felt unable to let a child be raised in the rough conditions of the free city workhouses. She agreed to care for him and, when he was old enough, take him on as an apprentice to the order.
>
>Ao progressed through the ranks quickly, reaching the rank of [[Light Domain#Flamebearer|Flamebearer]] much faster than expected. He almost seemed innately connected to the divine and so would be tested more quickly and rigorously than other acolytes. Eventually, [[#Seraphine Liora|Seraphine]] would discuss Ao's abandonment with him after a faith test was stopped abruptly. Ao, who was being tested on his ability to pierce a veil of deceit laid before him, would suddenly emit a terrifying flood of darkness but seemed completely unaware, his eyes rolled back in his head in a fit. [[#Seraphine Liora|Seraphine]] mentioned his mother had given him up after an incident but hadn't explained further, leading [[#Seraphine Liora|Seraphine]] to assume a sad but mundane event. She now wasn't so sure.
>
>Following his fit, Ao's appearance would start to change. His previously platinum blonde hair, almost white, suddenly started to develop a black streak. Initially only a few dark strands, it appeared spread until half a palm's width. From that point on, Ao would regularly catch looks from others of fear, distain or disgust, or would catch muffled and muted conversation in his direction. Instead of dissuade or intimidate him, it instead lit a fire within him. He would come to control the darkness that had started to reveal itself and over the next few years fine points that glittered in the light would start to appear from the corners of his eyes and spread to his hairline. Suddenly, the murmurs of something nefarious started to become something of the divine, and this was the first Ao would hear of [[Aasimar]].
>
>Feeling the pull of the world, Aolani would decide he needed to understand more of his divine heritage. After an initial search in the city of [[Dor-Gairn]] for his mother, which turned up nothing, Ao pulled at the last remaining thread and searched for information on the [[Aasimar]]. 
>
>Finding only rumours, speculation and folk stories backed by tradition and mysticism, Aolani would search for documented accounts of beings referred to as [[Aasimar]]. He would find limited information in the temple and city libraries of [[Tarador]] and realised his journey would require him to leave the place that had been his home as long as his memory served.
>
>Knowing the road ahead would be rough, Aolani decided to push further through the ranks on the [[Light Domain|Order of Dol Arrah]] and prepare his mind and body for what may assail him in his travels. He would find his place amongst the [[Light Domain#Ashen Confessor|Ashen Confessors]], an elite unit of inquisitors within the Order that became interested in him following his fit. As with all oddities that occur within a trial of faith, the event had been reported to the inquisitors who started to keep tabs on Aolani's movements and actions. The [[Ashen Confessors|Confessor]] appointed was [[Ashen Confessors#Iskiel Mourndawn|Iskiel Mourndawn]], a fateful appointment as many others would have considered Ao's fit proof of corruption. But the Tiefling saw something of himself in Ao, and, having lightly interrogated him, found no sign of corruption - rather, he found a soul that so purely exhibited the tenants of their order that he was moved to offer him an opportunity to join the ranks of the [[Ashen Confessors]]. An opportunity such as this was not lightly turned down, and seeing a possible link to his own desires, Ao graciously accepted.
>
>Through mentorship with [[Ashen Confessors#Iskiel Mourndawn|Iskiel]], Aolani started to learn the martial path. [[#Seraphine Liora|Seraphine]] though filled with trepidation for the brutal journey to come, given the [[Ashen Confessors]] reputation, would support the decisions of the man she had raised Ao to be with pride. The journey of a [[Ashen Confessors#Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)|Duskbound Initiate]] would be tough from the start, and through the next weeks and months Ao would be taught skills with simple weaponry and outfitted with the garb that fitted his station.
>
>Clad in luxurious black scale-mail armour filigreed with gold, he cut an intimidating figure even as an [[Ashen Confessors#Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)|Initiate]]; this was not given for show, however. Though his presence would be deemed intimidating by those that were of the clergy, this paled in comparison to other members of the Inquisition. Ao would face opponents during his training with much greater skill and they would show no mercy. He would learn mental and physical interrogation techniques through experience rather than theory, [[Ashen Confessors|Confessors]] using their initiates to demonstrate techniques.
>
>In time, Ao would come to earn the respect of both [[Ashen Confessors#Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)|Initiate]] and [[Ashen Confessors|Confessor]]. The strength of his convictions and mental discipline would prove him worthy of their number. Before long, [[Ashen Confessors#Iskiel Mourndawn|Iskiel]] would believe it time to test Ao's faith and either prove him worthy of joining the ranks of the [[Ashen Confessors#Confessors (Voices of Repentance)|Confessors]] or snuff out a weak flame. [[Ashen Confessors#Iskiel Mourndawn|Iskiel]] would set Ao's [[Ashen Confessors#Trial of Cinders|Trial of Cinders]] as the ultimate test of his faith. To be inducted into their number, Ao's [[Ashen Confessors#The Ordeal of Fire|Ordeal of Fire]] would be set as finding the answer to the question that plagued him. [[Ashen Confessors#Iskiel Mourndawn|Iskiel]] would warn him that the answer would determine his [[Ashen Confessors#The Purging of Weakness|Purging of Weakness]] trial.
>
>With his faith burning strong, Ao left the sanctuary of the temple and joined with an adventuring group traveling to the continent of [[Alornis]]. On new shores, he set out to find the largest repository of knowledge he could. Ao, operating as a mercenary healer to adventuring groups, learnt of the [[Enlightened Empire]] having an enormous library. Eventually finding a group traveling north, he went to the city of light with some hope starting to kindle within him.
>
>A considerable amount of time passed, Ao delving deep into the library in search of information on celestials and signs that would connect to his Faith Trial. With aid from scholars there who assisted in his research, they confirmed that he was indeed likely to be an [[Aasimar]], but for all their time they could not find an answer to his lineage. During this time he would see some of the faith of the people and it would sicken him. What initially felt like a variant of his religion quickly felt more like an intentional twisting and perversion of their tenants and beliefs. Ao had heard talk of the [[Enlightened Empire]] between [[Ashen Confessors]], and he had started to understand why. After managing to contact the temple with some of his observations using some of his remaining coin, [[Ashen Confessors#Iskiel Mourndawn|Iskiel]] would provide him information on a [[Ashen Confessors|Confessor]] outpost he could use to keep himself supplied while in the area.
>
>With so much of his time being spent in the city, and his black armour being fairly inconspicuous, his presents would be noticed by opportunists. One such opportunist introduced himself to Aolani as [[#Howie]] and slowly started to build Ao's trust. [[#Howie]] learnt of Ao's skill as a healer, his time with the [[Order of Light|Order of Dol Arrah]] in [[Dor-Gairn]], and some information on what Ao was searching for without mention of the [[Ashen Confessors]] or his [[Ashen Confessors#Trial of Cinders|Trials]]. [[#Howie]] claimed to work for some people that might be able to help him if he were willing to help them. Not being entirely naive, Ao assumed there would probably be a catch, but, starting to feel desperate to find something useful, he agreed in the hopes it would expedite his search.
>
>Quickly, Ao would come to regret this risk. Inadvertently becoming entangled with [[The Silent Ledger]], Ao would become involved in increasingly risky and unsavoury tasks. He would be required to undertake jobs he fundamentally disagreed with while the guild boss, [[#Veylen "The Butcher" Kaelthorne]], continued to dangle having information for him as payment for "one more job". With so much invested in getting his payment, Ao was loathed to give up before his payoff. His temper would begin to fray, however, and, after confronting the guild boss, Ao would leave their halls having nearly come to blows with the man. He agreed to one more assignment, knowing that if the information was not forthcoming he would take pleasure in cleansing the mans deception from the world.
>
>The task would take him to [[Nuvrin]], and into a shady looking alleyway.
# Associates & Enemies
## Seraphine Liora
A [[Light Domain#Radiant Watcher|Radiant Watcher]] of the [[Light Domain|Order of Dol Arrah]], Seraphine Liora took pity on Ao after he was left at the temple in [[Dor-Gairn]]. Unable to have children of her own due to the oaths taken during her ordainment, and having been raised from adolescence herself in the temple, she took to the task of raising him as best she could.
## Howie
An [[The Silent Ledger#Inkwraiths|Inkwraith]] of [[The Silent Ledger]] who picked out Aolani as a mark in the City of Light. A man of fairly aloof and cocky personality, he has a knack for charming his way into places he shouldn't be and finding people who don't want to be found.
## [[Veylen Kaelthorne]]
The guild boss of [[The Silent Ledger]], a former mercenary whose strategic brilliance and cold pragmatism have built the guild into an empire of wealth and power. Aolani and [[Veylen Kaelthorne|Veylen]] have an incredibly tense relationship, the guild boss being confident that Ao is all bluster and no bite given his position and status.
## Iskiel Mourndawn
An [[Ashen Confessors|Ashen Confessor]] of high regard, Iskiel took Ao as an apprentice and inducted him as a [[Ashen Confessors#Duskbound Initiates (Ashen Acolytes)|Duskbound Initiate]] to the inquisitorial order. Hard but fair, Iskiel led Aolani's training in martial disciplines and taught him the ways of interrogation both physically and mentally.
