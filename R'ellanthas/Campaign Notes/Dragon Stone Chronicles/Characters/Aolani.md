## Early Life
There is little Aolani remembers of his life before the clergy. Growing up in the warmer climate of [[Dor-Gairn]], one of the free cities of [[Tarador]], would have been idealistic for many that grew up there with normal childhoods. Where most children of the city would spend their hours playing in natural pools and springs, Ao would spend his days being reading the scriptures of the [[Light Domain]].

Only a few faint traces of memory of the woman who birthed him remain. Phantom scents of freshly picked wild flowers occasionally bring back almost shattered recollections of a woman called El, her face shadowed in his fading memory. The clerics of his order tell the story of a woman bringing him as an infant to the order's temple in [[Dor-Gairn]] claiming to be incapable or unfit to raise him. Unwilling to let a child be raised in the rough conditions of the free city workhouses, a [[Light Domain#Dawnkeeper|Dawnkeeper]], by the name of , from the [[Light Domain|Order of Dol Arrah]].

As he grew up, he heard whispers around corners and conversations where the participants didn’t believe him to be able to hear them whispering that something was different about him and his mother feared judgement by her peers, had kept him secret from birth before giving him up as an infant.
