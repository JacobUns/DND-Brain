# About
A man seemingly in his middle years, Tiscordin carries with him a darkness both literally and figuratively. A warlock who hides in shadow flawlessly, almost becoming one with it, Tiscordin tends to play his knowledge close to his chest and maintains some level of secrecy.
## Backstory
After questioning from [[Aolani|Ao]], who connected his knowledge of religion with a slip of the tongue in Tiscordin referring to his patron as "she" and the raven ever by his side, Tiscordin reveals that he is a warlock of [[the Raven Queen]]. In a period of his life where he was in his lowest, having been ill for a long time, [[the Raven Queen]] revealed herself to him. Physically and emotionally weak, spiritually broken and emotionally wrecked, she offered him power and purpose where he lacked both.

During [[Session 11 - A Fateful Find]], Tiscordin reveals he is one of the elves. His name was Tiscally Cor'elladin, first architect of the elves. Having woken to a world where all textbooks claim the elves have died out, Tiscordin is now attempting to discover if any other elves are alive. With the High King of the elves having decided that the elves should go into stasis, and having woken in his own stasis pod, he's aware that a contingency existed but not where any may have resided. He didn't dare to hope that any other elves lived, but his goal had been, and continues to be, to find the last of his kind.

# Associates
## Rámainen
The raven familiar of Tiscordin, Rámainen is never far away from the dark and mysterious man. Spirit guide, first met on the second meeting of his patron. His patron made a shadow appear and the bird climbed out of the ground. Rámainen communes with his patron.

Rámainen was a warlock/cleric of [[the Raven Queen]] previously, rebirthed as a spirit in raven form to guide Tiscordin.

He tells the [[The Raven Queen#Tiscordin's Tale of the Raven Queen|the Tale of the Raven Queen]].
