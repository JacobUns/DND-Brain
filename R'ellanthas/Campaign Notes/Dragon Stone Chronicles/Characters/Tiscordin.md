# About
A man in his middle years, Tiscordin carries with him a darkness both literally and figuratively. A warlock who hides in shadow flawlessly, almost becoming one with it, Tiscordin tends to play his knowledge close to his chest and maintains some level of secrecy.
## Backstory
After questioning from [[Aolani|Ao]], who connected his knowledge of religion with a slip of the tongue in Tiscordin referring to his patron as "she" and the raven ever by his side, Tiscordin reveals that he is a warlock of [[the Raven Queen]]. In a period of his life where he was in his lowest, having been ill for a long time, [[the Raven Queen]] revealed herself to him. Physically and emotionally weak, spiritually broken and emotionally wrecked, she offered him power and purpose where he lacked both.

# Associates
## Rámainen
The raven familiar of Tiscordin, Rámainen is never far away from the dark and mysterious man. Spirit guide, first met on the second meeting of his patron. His patron made a shadow appear and the bird climbed out of the ground. Rámainen communes with his patron.

Rámainen was a warlock/cleric of [[the Raven Queen]] previously, rebirthed as a spirit in raven form to guide Tiscordin.

He tells the following story of [[the Raven Queen]]'s origin:

It is said that [[the Raven Queen]] was once an elf queen, whose people loved her more than the gods. While the gods were locked in conflict, she tried to use the souls and magic of her people to elevate herself to godly status in order to salvage the fracturing pantheon of the elves.

As the queen rose in power, many elves become inspired by her, freely offering their souls and their magical abilities to help her achieve her goal. They hoped that once she achieved divinity, she would unify all of elf-kind. However, a group of evil wizards saw an opportunity to siphon her followers energy and use it to become stronger than the elven wizards of legend.

When the queen discovered what these wizards were doing she attempted to stop them. But in her quasi-divine state, her supernatural rage corrupted their ritual into a phenomenon that took on a terrible strength of its own.

By the time the queen realised her error, it was too late. She was powerless to stop herself and her followers being sucked into the [[Shadowfell]].

She was instantly killed.

From the ruined mind and body of this elf queen, the queen used the last vestiges of her personal power to pull dead memories from the [[Shadowfell]] to coalesce like a cloak about her, and [[the Raven Queen]] was born.
