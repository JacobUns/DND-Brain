# About
First appearing in [[Session 05 - Into the Capital|Session 5]] of the [[R'Ellanthas - The Dragon Stone Chronicles|Dragon Stone Chronicles]], Kimvac Xuan is a member of the information network maintained by [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin]] and maintains the store for [[Kalvenen]], [[Kalvenen#Xuan's Books|Xuan's Books]].
