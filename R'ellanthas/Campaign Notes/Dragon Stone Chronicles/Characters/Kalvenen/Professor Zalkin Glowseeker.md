# About
An unkempt halfling in his later years, Zalkin Glowseeker is the Professor of Astronomy at the [[Kalvenen#Kalvenen University|Kalvenen University]]. An expert in the cosmos, star maps and mathematics, Zalkin provides insight into the Starry Night painting in [[Session 07 - Finery and Fierce Pursuit|Session 7]] of the [[R'Ellanthas - The Dragon Stone Chronicles|Dragon Stone Chronicles]].
