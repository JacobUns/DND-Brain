# About
Veylen Kaelthorne is a man whose very presence makes people wary. In his late 40s, he carries himself with the controlled poise of a predator who knows they are at the top of the food chain. His sharp, angular features are framed by thick black hair streaked with grey but always immaculately groomed, no hair out of place. Piercing eyes assess everyone with the cold precision of a man measuring your worth - determining whether you are an asset or a liability.

A deep scar runs from his left temple to his jaw, a remnant of his mercenary past, a life where he once relied on steel rather than secrets. Though he rarely needs to resort to violence himself anymore, the weight of the countless lives he has ruined dealings lingers in his gaze. His clothing is refined, almost noble, but never ostentatious. In a dark tailored coat that cuts the lines of his muscled form, his garb speaks of wealth without excess. On his hand rests a heavy signet ring bearing the mark of [[The Silent Ledger]], which, to the right people, bears more weight than many crowns.

He moves with confidence though with a slight limp, supported by a finely carved wooden cane. When he speaks, his voice is smooth yet devoid of warmth. Those who stand before him find he never raises his voice, nor does he need to. The weight of his words is enough to topple reputations, destroy fortunes and end the lives of his enemies without ever drawing a blade. Those who find themselves facing the wrong end of his blade learn that his moniker still shows how he has come to maintain his power and reputation.
