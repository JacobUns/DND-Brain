**Days to Mossbreak**: 38 (24 days with foot travel)

After waking in the morning, the group make a plan for the day. After contacting her via sending stone, [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] says she will meet us at the Rose Garden connected to the Citadel at midday. Plan to explain to [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] what we have experienced at the ball.

[[Tiscordin]] returns to Edna's to drop off the clothing at her store and she prepares it to be displayed in her shop front. Head to [[Kalvanen#Xuan's Books|Xuan's]] and ask him to send someone to investigate the plants growing in the district of the city that glow. [[Kimvac Xuan|Xuan]] informs us that there is someone there he wants us to meet. He takes us to the back and at the table sits a red skinned Tiefling with blue hair, silver eyes, wearing reserved and common clothing. Her horns are modestly sized with a gentle curve, and [[Kimvac Xuan|Xuan]] introduces her as [[Seraphina]].

[[Seraphina]] informs us she's done some jobs for the information network before and that she excels at nefarious deeds. We get [[Seraphina]] up to speed with some of the goings on before her meeting. [[Kimvac Xuan|Xuan]] informs us there is a teleportation circle in each of the bookstores and that it may be possible for us to use them for our own travels. We ask him to get his agents to investigate the surrounding area and see if there are any more plants growing up through the city, which he agrees to and says it will take a few hours for the information to be gathered.

The group travel from the bookstore to the rose garden to meet [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Duchess Cho]]. Rámainen and the rest of the group keep eyes out as they move and don't notice anyone following as they move through the city. The rose garden has an ornate gate that is closed as we approach, but pushing on the gate reveals it is unlocked and the group push in. The gardens are well kept and pretty with elaborate botanical works of art in rose bush forms. There are many paths that have been created with trees and shrubs being used to create natural arches. There are many nooks and private meeting points throughout but moving through we can't find anyone around. As we wait, we see [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] moving up one of the paths towards wearing a purple dress and a hood up. As [[Aolani|Ao]] approaches, he feels a strange sensation almost like passing into a magical bubble. [[Aolani|Ao]] isn't completely sure but feels like his hearing dulls slightly. [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] lowers her hood and greets the party.

[[Aolani|Ao]] brings up the death of [[Dohko Nog]], and [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] explains that [[Orphus]] is an interesting collection of people working towards the "good of the kingdom". They tend to go against [[the pact of Il'mondum]], seeking out artefacts that could be of use and as best they can tell seems to be working towards a better society. They're a fairly new development, and the threat they pose is not known to the kingdom yet. [[Tiscordin]] laughs to [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] about [[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] almost being caught for his murder again due to Anika's actions. 

We discuss the information from the man in the blue cloak, and [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] doesn't know much of them or their motives. She has not been able to locate a base of operations, haven't been able to capture one, and haven't talked to someone that has encountered them and lived to tell the tale. [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] mentions that the poison they added would have had no effect on people. Volunteers agreed to consume the champagne and it had no effect.

[[Tiscordin]] asks about whether [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] has noticed [[Oda]] wearing the stone and when it started. She says it has been a number of months since it started being worn openly. [[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] tells [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] he believes [[Oda]] is part of the cult. [[Tiscordin]] quotes the man saying there were people within the palace that were working for the dragons. [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] explains the cult appears to be fairly new but that more seem to be recruited. She's unaware of how big the cult is but that it appears to be growing in number and may very well be kingdom wide.

[[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] asks whether there is a connection with the [[Enlightened Empire]] and [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] explains that they are a zealot group that seem to be a different problem in their own right. They are quite civil if you are willing to follow their tenants, but become violent when they are refused. [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] advised against the armies being sent north and that it was a distraction to draw them away. The king trusts the army general, but that [[Oda]] would have the ability to override and direct the army himself is a possibility and that there was elevated risk with him being in close proximity with a dragonstone.

[[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] then asks about the painting. We inform her of the information from [[Professor Zalkin Glowseeker|Professor Glowseeker]] and that we intended to keep pursing it. [[Tiscordin]] reveals he told [[Duchess Joci Bellowfern|Duchess Bellowfern]] of [[Duchess Joci Bellowfern#Jelin Bellowfern|Jelin Bellowfern]] and reveals to [[Duchess Cho Nuvrin|Cho]] that [[King Caladeth Kalvanen#Pargir Kalvenen|Pargir Kalvenen]] was also depicted by the elves. She encourages us to continue pulling at the threads we have, and [[Aolani|Ao]] provides the other threads we have no discussed as a source of thread pulling.

[[Aolani|Ao]] asks [[Kimvac Xuan|Kimvac]] whether he's tracked down more information on celestial heritages and [[Tiscordin]] spends the downtime to the evening investigating a ciphered journal recovered from Buxton Forest near [[Milanalin]]. It needs 24hrs of deciphering, but after 4hrs he has managed to make 8hrs worth of progress. In the evening, the group heads down to [[Vaxalin]] and moves through the tunnels to the heart of the vines and roots. [[Seraphina]] calls out in Common, Dwarven and Giant but nothing responds and [[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] tries Primordial, still nothing responds.

Eventually, [[Seraphina]] fires off a sorcerers burst and combat ensues. During, [[Tiscordin]] summons a shadow spirit of despair that reduces movement of creatures within 5ft by 20ft.

We discuss the possibilities of finding a box that protects the painting or finding something weighty enough to weigh down the crate, whether we can investigate the [[Kalvanen#Redbark Hill|Redbark Hill]] area to see if we can find a location that allows us to watch or whether we go in blasting. It seems fairly dilapidated inside without much movement going on. [[Tiscordin]] relays the information he's seeing to the rest of the group.

We long rest for the night.
