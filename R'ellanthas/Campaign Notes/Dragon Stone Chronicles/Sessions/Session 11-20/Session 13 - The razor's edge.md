**Days to Mossbreak**: 6 days

After two days travel through the mountains, we camp overnight a short distance away from the small village. During the night, Borin, Seraphina and Lumen all see carts travelling through the the village in the dead of night which seems suspicious.

**Days to Mossbreak**: 5 days

We wake with 5 days remaining and the village starts to awake. Herding Dogs bark and bound ahead of their Shepard's while the rest of the village starts to wake and start their morning activities. From the distance, we can see carts in the village we could use to camouflage. We decide to go around the edge of the village instead, and follow a path that runs in parallel to the main road.

On the other side of the village, it appears there is no one else in the area, but as a cart trundles down the road we notice several figures that pop out of the underbrush to check them. In the distance, there appears to be a heavily disguised checkpoint through a little ravine. There, we see more security stopping the carts and checking the contents before they continue. Further down the path we see an impression in the terrain, almost like a crater. There, the cart seems to dip down the road and then disappear within an entryway. It stops for a few minutes, then continues on.

Lumen gets Borin into a closer range to the door. He sends Alfred down and attempts to find information from within. The arc cells appear to be unloaded from the carts and then moved into mine carts instead. Tiscordin sends out his flock of familiars to find another entry or exit to the mine, but the hills just appear to roll onward without any other exits to be found.

Inside, Red Kobolds do the majority of the grunt work. Approximately 50 move things between locations and a mixture of races oversee the activities; some dragonborn, some tabaxi, some halfling. Alfred flies back around the cavern and spots a humanoid figure, dragonborn, who is towards the back of the cavern on a raise dais. Around him stand hooded figures in a line behind him. As he's flying past, the dragonborn looks directly at Alfred and so flies back to us. Ao uses Locate Object and finds that the stone is moving back and forth 250ft below us.

We decide on dimension dooring into the main chamber and attempting to take out the dragonborn that is carrying the dragonstone. With some trepidation, we teleport down.
