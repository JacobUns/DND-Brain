We bid farewell to  [[Veil of the Valley]], a male Tabaxi that runs Veil Books. Looking at the map we've been given it would be a two day walk, but with the horses and spectral steeds we can cut that down to 1 day. It'll be a hard ride across wide open plains, passing farmers going about their daily lives, oblivious to the impending calamity that may befall the continent. We pass trade caravans coming from or to [[Oda]] make their way across the land before we leave the worn track and enter the grasslands with rolling foothills ahead of us. The winds blow long grass like sea waves as our horses hooves trample stems like snapping thunder below. [[Tiscordin]], merged with [[Tiscordin#Rámainen|Rámainen]], scouts ahead and finds 

[[Aolani|Ao]], scrying again on the location of the lair, sees that the metal arch that was under construction is now complete. Where the middle of the arch was clear before, it now has a shimmering veil. 30-40 small Kobolds run around the area performing tasks, [[arc cells]] piled about seem to be glowing brightly. They appear slightly different than we've seen them before, but [[Aolani|Ao]] is not sure what. Around the area, ten or so heavily armed dragonborn guards stand watch while the masked dragonborn paces back and forth impatiently.

[[Tiscordin]] communes with [[the raven queen]] and asks:
- Is this a gate to another plane? Yes
- Can the gate be closed? Yes
- Do we have the means on us close the gate? Yes
- Will damage from weapons or spells close the gate? Yes
- Would damaging part of the gate close the gate? No

We rest for the night, the stars above keeping solemn watch while the bright moon. 

[[Aolani|Ao]] uses divination and asks "With the gate now active, what is the safest way to close it?" Dol'Arrah responds "Deactivate the gate from the other side". With that sombre thought, we rest for the night and prepare mentally for what comes.

**Days until Mossbreak**: 2 days

Tiscodin leads the way in the morning, and it becomes obvious that we're getting lost after several hours. Being from these lands, [[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] eventually steps up and takes over and starts to undo some of the damage done and we make good time. Eventually, we come across a man-made structure that looks like some kind of outpost. From where we stand, we realise the outpost is positioned to be able to see everything in the area so sneaking will be difficult. [[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] determines that some figures move around in the area below, too far to see what, but [[Lumen Keijo|Lumen]] flying above sees a couple of guards standing at the gated entrance to the valley. Up the valley path, he sees a constructed archway just beyond that appears to be the entrance to the lair.

Eventually, we agree that the best-worst option is to walk around the edge of the valley, then use an upcasted invisibility to quietly sneak through the archway while [[Tiscordin]] uses a flock of familiars to scout through the canyon. They find the walled gate, barred with no one on our side to operate it. We assume that it requires calling up to the watchtower for entry. The tower is a crude wooden structure, almost just a wooden platform on top of four stilts; it's sturdy, but not incredibly so. A small camp is made inside perimeter with tents erected within. Each guard has a large bow and one that carries a staff. The archway strikes [[Tiscordin]] as odd; its large entryway, 40-feet across, doesn't have a portal or gate but appears to be built into the solid stone and appears to be eleven in construction. It's been weathered by time and element, but is otherwise intact. Within, darkness greets the familiar's vision, but gentle light emanates from deeper inside the caves. The crow familiars try to act naturally, but one is sent further inside. No one is at the entrance and it quickly turns from natural rock formation to worked path into the depths. A couple of sconces flickering with light placed into the worked stone and eventually reaches a fork. One path leads down further, while the other splits off into this level of the cave.

The familiars continue their path down and the path disappears into the depths of the mountainside until it eventually breaks into a balcony that overlooks a town. Immediately, [[Tiscordin]] recognises the architecture as elven. Kobolds scurry through the space, crawling over every area carrying cargo while their dragonborn masters whip at their backs to drive them onwards. The crows flight above the town reveals temples, a town hall, dwellings and other signs of normal life. In the centre of the town square, an enormous pit has been dug straight down with cranes and pulley systems setup to enable the mining operation. It goes 50-60 feet down with magical torchlight glowing below. [[Tiscordin]] directs one of the crows to land on the cargo being lowered down, Kobolds swipe at them but after fruitless attempts eventually give up. The rough hewn rock leads down to eventually reveal the chamber from the scry. The oval room seems naturally occurring or expended within minimal care for appearance.

While we walk the edge, we discuss how we're going to get out. Though we are optimistic given the information we've managed to scout, there is still grim knowledge that this may be a one way trip. As a group, we make our way down the edge of the valley towards the archway entrance, scrambling down the rock wall. As Tiscordin takes a step to descend he dislodges a rock and loses his footing before quickly morphing with [[Tiscordin#Rámainen|Rámainen]] and landing on a rock top. From there, he can see that the guards attention has been drawn to where the noise came from but seeing the bird lose interest.

As we enter the cave paths, Kobolds dragging boxes to the surfaces make enough noise that we're able to easily move through the caverns unnoticed. We make it to the lift and pit, and find the lift is currently at its lowest point. There doesn't appear to be any obvious camps around the edge, but in a few places Kobolds have fallen asleep where they lie. Ao notices on the temple face has the symbol of Dol'Arrah. We make the quick decision to head around to the temple with some remaining time on our invisibility. The architecture strikes Tiscordin as a later elven styling; a circular chamber with a large altar in the centre. On it, a large representation of a female figure that has an uncanny resemblance to Dol'Arrah. Borin, moving through the chamber, see depictions that he doesn't recognise, but moving around double takes as he finds a depiction of what seems unnervingly the same as Ao. The more he looks, the more features seem to connect back to us. He calls out and Ao joins him at the wall where the rest of the group joins.

The scene of the mural seems to depict a battle between good and evil. Whether this is literal of metaphorical battle is unclear, but there are elvish words written below, which says "Kamneth, champion of Dlarrah". Everyone recognises the same as Borin has, but Ao looks to the other murals. He seems important moments from the religious stories of Dol'Arrah. We take some time, and eventually Ao returns to the altar and wraps the statue in a strip of cloth before storing it in his bag of holding. We head back to the pit, more confused than anything, and this place lingers in Ao's mind.

We winch the platform lift back up and then climb onto it before Lumen starts lowering us back down. He then jumps down and runs the vertical surface down to the floor. The Kobold's below shout up to their comrades above in draconic with insults after they haven't removed what was on the pallet. We move through the space, still invisible, but we start to sense a change in alertness. They were relaxed but something about the platform landing in the same state seems to have raised their hackles and there's a hardness to the eyes of the Dragonborn with the oddity of the reappearance.

We assess the gate and find a circular metal platform a short distance from the gate that we assume is the point from which the ritual will be started. There is no location on the arch that seems weak or anything that appears to give control capabilities to it. We peer around the room, and realise that we have little to go on than the individuals within the room. With dawning realisation, the truth of the fight and consequences of our failure loom large.
