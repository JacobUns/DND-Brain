# Description
A crystal structure banded with metal, the smokey stone radiates an essence of power. 

![[Arc Cell.png]]
# Usage
Use to increase level by 2 or +2 to attack roll or save dc
Usage like this causes wild magic use

## Uses
Tiny 1 - Carry with 1 hand
Small 2 - Carry with 1 hand
Medium 4 - Carry with 1 hand
Large 8 - Carry with 2 hand
Huge 16 - Carry with 2 hand
