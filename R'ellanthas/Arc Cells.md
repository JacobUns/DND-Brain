# Description
A crystal structure banded with metal, the smokey stone radiates an essence of power. 

![[Arc Cell.png]]
# Usage
An Arc Cell has charges determined by its size. Once all charges are expended, the Arc Cell is inert until it is charged using the required technology.

The below table details each type of Arc Cell.

| Size   | No. of Charges | Value | Weight | Carry Requirement |
| ------ | -------------- | ----- | ------ | ----------------- |
| Tiny   | 1              | 25    | 1lbs   | 1 Hand            |
| Small  | 2              | 50    | 2lbs   | 1 Hand            |
| Medium | 4              | 200   | 4lbs   | 1 Hand            |
| Large  | 8              | 500   | 8lbs   | 2 Hands           |
| Huge   | 16             | 2000  | 16lbs  | 2 Hands           |

You may expend 1, 2 or 4 charges to gain the following benefits:
**1 Charge**: Gain +1 to Spell Attack, Spell DC or Spell Casting Level
**2 Charges**: Gain +2 to Spell Attack, Spell DC or Spell Casting Level
**4 Charges**: Gain +3 to Spell Attack, Spell DC or Spell Casting Level

Arc Cells can also be attached to weapons to empower them by those capable with magic and appropriate crafting tools for the material, such as Smithing Tools for swords or Carpenter's Tools for bows. 

Attaching an Arc Cell can allow you to gain the effects of the Magic Weapon or Elemental Weapon spell effects by using charges of your Arc Cells without requiring a bonus action to activate. 

The effect duration depends on the spell effect chosen:
- **Magic Weapon**: 1 Action
- **Elemental Weapon**: 1 Attack

The Elemental Weapon spell effect is capped at +1 to hit but continues to scale in damage of 1d4 per charge used.

Invoking the effects of an Arc Cell, unless crafted into a weapon by a master of the technology, causes a Wild Magic surge. Roll a d100 on the Wild Magic table to identify the effect.
