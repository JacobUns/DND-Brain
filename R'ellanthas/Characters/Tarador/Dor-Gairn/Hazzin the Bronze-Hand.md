**Race**: Giff (Hippo-folk)
**Gender**: Male
**Appearance**: A massive, broad-shouldered Giff with dusky gray skin, Hazzin wears bronze-plated gauntlets and an artificer’s beltfilled with drafting tools. His booming voice carries over the city’s construction sites.

**Personality**: Proud, methodical, and deeply loyal to the city. Hazzin is responsible for buildings, roads, and fortifications, ensuring Dor-Gairn remains defensible and thriving. He admires craftsmen and despises wasteful spending

**Notable Traits**: Brilliant engineer, fluent in multiple languages, and rumoured to have hidden vaults within the city's defences.
