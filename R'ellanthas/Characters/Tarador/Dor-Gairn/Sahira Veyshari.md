# About
[[Dor-Gairn#Grand Vizier|Grand Vizier]] of [[Dor-Gairn]], Sahira speaks to her [[Dor-Gairn#Viziers of Trade and Law|Viziers]] in the royal audience chamber. Though she speaks calmly and without raising her voice, it is clear she commands the room and that none are willing to openly challenge her decisions. She sits straight backed, enraptured in the reports of her servants, upon the golden throne, the perfect vision of courteous femininity. Beneath the surface, she rages at what she hears letting nothing show on her face.
# Appearance
A human woman in her late forties with deep bronze skin and raven-black hair, worn in intricate brains adorned with tiny gemstones embedded in gold chains. Her eyes are a piercing emerald with a sharp intelligence. Her flowing opulent silk robes in sunburst gold and crimson lightly stroke her delicate skin as she moves. Upon her head is a golden diadem flecked with gems of all colours like shattered glass, light spraying refracted colour from each. In the centre is an enormous emerald cut as a beautiful pear cut, it's tip almost connecting to a plume of Couatl feathers behind it which move in a gradient from green to red at the tips.
# Personality & Traits
Elegant, calculating and enigmatic, as her measured tones lilt in conversation any engaged with her quickly realise she misses nothing. Giving up only the details she wishes in a deal, the stories claim she brokered her way to power through ruthless trade deals and alliances, keeping a balance between factions ensuring none grow strong enough to challenge her. Calm, courteous and refined, she has come to be known as the Sable Viper for the venom that infects those that disappoint her. Said to have spies in every merchant, vizier and high ranking house on [[Tarador]], her agents are said to listen for secrets that could ruin or enrich fortunes overnight. Those that disappoint her can find their houses rot from the inside, never even aware they've been bitten by the viper.
