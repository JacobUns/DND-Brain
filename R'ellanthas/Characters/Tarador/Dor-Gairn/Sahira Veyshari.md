# About
[[Dor-Gairn#Grand Vizier|Grand Vizier]] of [[Dor-Gairn]], 
# Description
A woman in her late forties with deep bronze skin and raven-black hair, often woven into intricate braids adorned with tiny gemstones and gold chains. Her piercing emerald eyes are sharp with intelligence, missing nothing in a conversation. She wears flowing opulent silk robes in sunburst gold and crimson. On her head sits a golden diadem flecked with gems of all colours, like shattered glass refracting the light. In the centre is an enormous emerald with a small plume of Couatl feathers which fade in a gradient from green to red.

Elegant, calculating, and enigmatic. Sahira speaks in measured tones, revealing only what she wishes. She brokered her way to power through ruthless trade deals and alliances, and she maintains it by ensuring no faction grows strong enough to challenge her. Some whisper that she has spies in every merchant house, listening to secrets that could ruin or enrich fortunes overnight.
