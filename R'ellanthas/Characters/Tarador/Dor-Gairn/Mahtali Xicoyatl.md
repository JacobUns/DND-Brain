**Race**: Yuan-ti Pureblood
**Gender**: Female
**Nickname**: Jade Palm
**Appearance**: A serpentine grace marks Mahtali’s every movement, with golden-green scales shimmering on her hands and neck. Her dark hair is woven with jade rings, and she wears emerald robes lined with silver thread.

**Personality**: Smooth, diplomatic, and unshakable. Mahtali regulates trade fairness and market disputes, ensuring merchants do not cheat buyers—or each other. She favours subtle coercion over brute force.

**Notable Traits**: A master negotiator, uncannily perceptive, and rumoured to have spies in every guild.
