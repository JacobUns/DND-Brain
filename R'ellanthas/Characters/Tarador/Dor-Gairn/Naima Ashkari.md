**Race**: Human
**Gender**: Female
**Appearance**: Naima is a tall, statuesque woman with rich brown skin, piercing dark eyes, and raven-black hair streaked with silver, always kept in intricate braids wrapped in golden thread. Her sharp features and ever-calculating gaze give her an air of authority that few dare to challenge. She wears a crimson and gold-trimmed robe over a reinforced leather cuirass, a nod to her past as a warrior before she ascended to power.

**Personality**: Unyielding, calculating, and incorruptible. Naima is deeply committed to stability and the rule of law, ensuring that order prevails no matter the cost. She believes that a strict, impartial hand is required to keep the city's many factions in check. Though fair, she has a reputation for ruthless efficiency, and her word is law within [[The Sunfire Guard]].

**Notable Traits**: A former high-ranking officer in [[The Sunfire Guard]], she still commands deep respect from the ranks. A brilliant tactician, unwaveringly fair, and trained in both swordplay and diplomacy.
