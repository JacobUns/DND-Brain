**Race**: Tiefling
**Gender**: Female
**Appearance**: Narisa has deep indigo skin, curving silver-tipped horns, and piercing golden eyes that seem to see through deception. She wears rich silken robes embroidered with feathered patterns, a nod to the desert tribes who once paid homage to [[Dor-Gairn]]. Her clawed fingers are adorned with rings from various kingdoms, each a symbol of a tribute deal she has struck.

**Personality**: Seductive, ruthless, and endlessly patient. Narisa is a master of negotiation and manipulation, able to extract tribute, favours, and alliances without drawing a blade. She views every interaction as a trade—whether of goods, influence, or secrets.

**Notable Traits**: Fluent in many languages, knows how to exploit weaknesses, and is rumoured to have connections to [[The Silent Ledger]].
