**Race**: Fire Genasi
**Gender**: Male
**Appearance**: Zahmir has crimson-hued skin, glowing ember-like eyes, and coal-black hair that flickers like dying embers. He wears deep navy robes embroidered with golden filigree and a chain of minted gold coins denoting his authority.

**Personality**: Cunning, meticulous, and ambitious. Zahmir rose from a family of moneylenders and trade financiers, securing his position by outmanoeuvring rivals and ensuring city coffers are never empty. Some whisper he profits from hidden debts.

**Notable Traits**: Ruthless in collecting dues, an expert in accounting, and never forgets a debt.
