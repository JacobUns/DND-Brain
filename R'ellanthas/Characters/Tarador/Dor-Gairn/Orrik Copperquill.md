**Race**: Dwarf
**Gender**: Male
**Appearance**: A stocky, well-groomed dwarf with graying auburn hair, always dressed in dark purple robes with a brass-ringed ledger chained to his belt. His spectacles are enchanted to detect forgeries.

**Personality**: Gruff, precise, and incorruptible. Orrik is utterly devoted to record-keeping, treating debts as sacred oaths. He has no patience for dishonesty and holds merchants accountable without fear or favor.

**Notable Traits**: Photographic memory, an uncanny ability to detect lies, and rumoured to have ciphers hidden in every contract.
