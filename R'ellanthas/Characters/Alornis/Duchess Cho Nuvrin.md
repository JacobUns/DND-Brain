# About
A human woman in her late thirties, Duchess Cho Nuvrin controls one of the Duchies of the [[Kingdom of Kalvanen]] through covert information networks, agents and spies. Her seat of power is [[Nuvrin]], a city of stone buildings covered in white rendering with some painted in a variety of vivid colours. With the smell of salt in the air, the coastal city has a fairly high standard of living thanks to her ability to control and rule her lands with a confidence and wisdom of someone far older.

Cho's information network covers the whole [[Kingdom of Kalvanen|Kingdom]], with her agents able to report to safe houses in all of the major cities. The safe houses operate as bookstores, their owners members of the information network, with some having secret rooms and access to the underbelly of their cities.
